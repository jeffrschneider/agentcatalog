# Value stream: Account and billing requests

`vs/sup/account-and-billing-requests v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a customer cannot sign in, when someone asks for control
of a business account because whoever held it has left, when a customer
asks to close its account, when a person asks for a copy, a correction or
the deletion of the data the company holds about them, when a customer
asks about a charge, an invoice or a payment, when a customer asks for
money back, or when a customer has borne a failure the company should make
good. The processes here check every person under the identity policy
before anything in an account is read out, changed or handed over, tell
the account's existing contacts through details already on file, close
each account on the date the customer is entitled to without delaying it
for anything else, answer each data request by the deadline the local
rules set, answer billing questions from records support can read and
never change, hand every claim that an invoice is wrong to Finance's
dispute process, and prepare each refund and goodwill credit with its
evidence for the named person who decides it. Handing over access to an
account or control of it, closing an account, deciding what a data request
releases, corrects or deletes, sending the answer to a data request,
deciding every refund and credit, and sending any reply that admits fault,
promises a date or offers money always stay with named people. Whoever
proposes a refund or a credit never approves it, Finance issues every
credit note and pays every refund, and no agent moves money or changes a
bill itself.
