# Value stream: Outages and incidents

`vs/sup/outages-and-incidents v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when requests on one symptom arrive faster than the threshold
the company set, when customers start saying in public that something is
broken, when an incident customers can see is confirmed, or when such an
incident closes. The processes here group the cases on a symptom and raise
them at once with the engineer on call, who confirms or rules out an
incident. While the incident lasts, they post an update on the status page
and inside the product on the cadence the incident policy sets, each one
drawn only from what the incident lead confirmed and in step with any
statement Marketing issues, and they list the accounts the incident
affects, send every linked case each approved update, and send every
notice an agreement names for an outage by its deadline. Once the incident
closes, they send the affected customers an account of what happened and
what changes, close or keep open every linked case with its reason, and
work out the credits each agreement owes. Engineering runs the incident
itself, and support runs the customer side of it. Confirming an incident
stays with the engineer on call, every word the company says in public
about an outage and the account sent to customers afterwards are approved
by a named person before anyone outside reads them, every credit is
decided by a named person who did not propose it and booked by Finance,
and no agent posts or sends words a person has not approved or moves any
money.
