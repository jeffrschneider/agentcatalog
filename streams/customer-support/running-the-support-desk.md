# Value stream: Running the support desk

`vs/sup/running-the-support-desk v1`

**position:** 8
A run starts when the period's volume forecast falls due, when the
schedule for the coming weeks is due to be built or a support specialist
asks to swap a shift, when the support team's day opens, when someone
asks to change how requests are sorted and routed, when a saved reply is
needed or has to change, when a service level is proposed or reaches its
review date, or when a reporting period closes. The processes here
forecast the requests each channel will receive in each interval and the
people each interval needs to meet the service levels, build the schedule
and the after-hours rota inside the working time rules and the approved
leave, watch the day's queues against their service levels and set out
what a team lead can do about a queue about to miss, change each routing
rule as plain sentences tested on past requests away from live, publish
each saved reply from approved content with every reply a person must
send marked to go through review each time it is used, set each service
level with what it would take in people and what customers' agreements
already promise, and report every queue against its promise with every
figure traced to the support system. Approving the schedule and each
swap, deciding what to do about a queue about to miss, approving each
routing change and each saved reply, deciding each service level, and
deciding what the service level report calls for always stay with named
people. No agent approves a schedule or reports a named person's time
for discipline, and anything that touches a support person's employment
goes to Human resources.
