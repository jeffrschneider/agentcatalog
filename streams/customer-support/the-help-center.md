# Value stream: The help center

`vs/sup/the-help-center v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts when a question customers keep asking needs an article, when
an article reaches its review date or a change to the product touches what
it covers, when a reporting period ends, or when a release customers will
see has a date. The processes here write each help article from the
product as it behaves with every step tested, have it edited, checked
against its sources and scored for accessibility, publish it at a version
with its owner and its review date, and mark as content the agents may
answer from only a version its named owner approved. They read every
article on its schedule against the product, the cases that cite it and
how often it answered the question, and take content off the list the
agents answer from on the day it is found out of date. They rank the gaps
that the cases, the searches that found nothing and the questions the
agents could not answer reveal by how many customers each touches, and
before each release reaches customers they update the articles and saved
replies it touches and brief the support specialists on it. Approving each
article, approving what an article says about refunds, service levels, a
person's data or the contract, deciding whether an article is kept,
rewritten or taken down, choosing which gaps are written next, confirming
that each support specialist is ready for a release, and deciding what
customers are told when support will not be ready always stay with named
people. No agent approves an article, the agents publish only what its
owner approved, and no article promises a fix, a date or a feature that
has not shipped.
