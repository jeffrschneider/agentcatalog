# Value stream: Quality and coaching

`vs/sup/quality-and-coaching v1`

**position:** 7
A run starts when a review period closes, when the date for calibrating
the reviewers comes round or the quality lead calls a round, when a
support specialist's coaching session comes due, or when a new support
specialist is handed on from Human resources after the first day. The
processes here draw each period's sample of closed cases at a size set
before any case is drawn and score each case against the quality rubric
with the moment in the case behind each score, have the reviewers score
the same cases separately and agree what each part of the rubric means,
bring each specialist's reviewed cases to a private coaching session with
their team lead and keep the actions the two agree in a record only they
can open, and carry each new specialist from access to the desk through
teaching and a sitting on each topic to replies of their own. Agents score
cases, and no agent scores, ranks or rates a support specialist. A
specialist's results go only to that specialist and their team lead. Every
change to the rubric is decided by named people, a team lead signs each
topic pass and each step to replies of a new specialist's own, and nothing
from a case review or a coaching session reaches a decision about anyone's
job unless the team lead, as a person, takes it to Human resources.
