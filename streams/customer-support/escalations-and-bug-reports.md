# Value stream: Escalations and bug reports

`vs/sup/escalations-and-bug-reports v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts when a support specialist cannot resolve a case or the case
passes the escalation threshold, when a specialist reproduces a fault in
the product, when a customer asks for something the product does not do,
when a bug is fixed or a requested feature is released, or when a customer
reports a weakness in the product's security, a possible exposure of their
data or a product that may cause harm. The processes here hand each case
the first specialist cannot resolve to a named specialist with its full
history, so the customer never has to say it twice, check each reproduced
fault against the bugs already filed and file it once with the steps, the
evidence and every account it affects, follow each bug up with the
engineering team on the cadence its severity sets, write each feature
request in the customer's own words and count the accounts asking with
what they pay, tell every customer linked to a fix or a release once it
has reached them, and put each security or safety report in the hands of a
named person in the security team within the hour while keeping it out of
every public channel. Accepting an escalated case, confirming a bug and
setting its severity, deciding what is built, approving any date a
customer hears for a fix, approving each notice about a fix, deciding
whether a fix resolves a case, and approving and sending every reply to a
person who reported a security or safety concern always stay with named
people, and no agent promises a customer a fix, a feature or a date.
