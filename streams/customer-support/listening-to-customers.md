# Value stream: Listening to customers

`vs/sup/listening-to-customers v1`

**position:** 6
A run starts when a survey period opens for the cases that close in it,
when a customer makes a formal complaint or says something that
`<your complaints policy>` treats as one, when a reporting period ends, or
when a support specialist marks a sign on a case that an account may leave
or buy more. The processes here ask each customer how their case went only
where the consent record allows and no more often than
`<your survey interval>`, send each low score to the team lead to follow
up, and report the scores with their counts and whether a change is larger
than the noise. They log each formal complaint with its deadline,
acknowledge it in the approved words, send a legal threat to the legal team
the same day, and bring each complaint to an answer from a named person who
had no part in the matter. They set out each period what customers said,
with the cases, the survey results, the complaints and the feature requests
under each finding, for the product team, the account owners and the
leaders, and they pass each sign that an account may leave or buy more, in
the customer's own words, to its owner through Sales' processes. Answering
a formal complaint, deciding what to do about what customers say, and every
offer, price or save for an account always stay with named people. A
support specialist's own scores go only to that specialist and their team
lead, and support never delays or conditions a cancellation.
