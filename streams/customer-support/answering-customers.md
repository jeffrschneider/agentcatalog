# Value stream: Answering customers

`vs/sup/answering-customers v1`

**position:** 1
A run starts when a customer asks for help by email, chat, phone, the web
form or inside the product, or when the community-manager agent passes on
a question from the community or a social inbox, when someone is about to
read out, change or act on anything in a customer's account, when a
drafted reply falls in a category a person must send, or when the support
team's hours end for the day. The processes here match every request to
the customer and the account, join it to any case already open on the same
matter, set the service level the customer's plan carries and place it in
the right queue, check the person asking against what the account already
holds before anyone touches the account, answer routine questions at once
from approved content in a reply that says it comes from an agent and
offers a person, keep every other case with one named support specialist
until the customer has the answer or the fix, and cover the hours when the
team is away with answers from approved content, a page to the person on
call for anything urgent and a queue set in order for the morning. Every
reply that admits fault, promises a fix or a date, offers money or any
change to what the contract says, or answers a formal complaint, a legal
threat, a security or safety report, a regulator, the press or a request
about a person's own data is reviewed and sent by a named person, and a
named person decides what happens to a request held as a possible account
takeover. An agent answers only from approved content and the customer's
own records, and no agent decides a refund or a credit, moves money,
changes a bill or hands control of an account to anyone.
