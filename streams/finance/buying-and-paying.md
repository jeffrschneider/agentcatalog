# Value stream: Buying and paying

`vs/fin/buying-and-paying v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts when someone in the company asks to buy something, when a
purchase large enough to need a supplier chosen has none, when a new
supplier is needed, when someone asks to change the bank account a
supplier is paid into, when a supplier's invoice arrives, or when the
payment calendar reaches a payment date. The processes here check each
request to buy against its budget line and the delegation of authority and
send the supplier an approved purchase order before any work starts,
choose the supplier for a large purchase against criteria written down
before any supplier is asked, set up each supplier with its legal and tax
details checked and its bank details confirmed by a person, hold every
change to a supplier's bank details until a person has confirmed it
through a contact already on file, match each invoice to its order and to
what was received before it is approved, and pay what is approved and due
on its date. Approving a purchase, choosing a supplier, approving a
supplier's record, approving a change to a supplier's bank details,
deciding an invoice that does not match, and approving and releasing every
payment always stay with named people. Whoever prepares a payment run
never approves it, two named people release every payment, and no agent
moves money or holds the access to do so.
