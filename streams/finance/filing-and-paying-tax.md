# Value stream: Filing and paying tax

`vs/fin/filing-and-paying-tax v1`

**position:** 7
A run starts when a period for an indirect tax return ends, when a
quarter or a year has closed far enough for the income tax in the
accounts to be worked out, when an entity's income tax return comes due,
when a letter or an assessment arrives from a tax authority, or when an
authority opens an audit. The processes here work out the tax charged on
sales and paid on purchases from checked data and file and pay each
indirect tax return by its date, calculate the current and deferred
income tax each quarter and year with every uncertain position set out
for a named person, prepare each entity's income tax return from the
closed accounts and carry its difference from the provision into the
books, and answer each notice and each audit by its date with only what
it asks for. One tax calendar holds every return and payment the company
owes in each jurisdiction, and each local rule is a blank the company's
own accountants and advisers fill in. Tax withheld from pay is paid and
filed in Human resources, and a fund's returns and its investors' tax
documents are prepared in Private capital. Every tax position, every
signature on a return, every response sent to a tax authority, every
decision to agree, contest or appeal an assessment, and every approval
and release of a tax payment always stay with named people, and no agent
moves money.
