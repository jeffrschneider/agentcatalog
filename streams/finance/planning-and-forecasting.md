# Value stream: Planning and forecasting

`vs/fin/planning-and-forecasting v1`

**position:** 1
A run starts when the executive team sets the company's targets for the
next financial year, when a month on the reforecast cadence closes or the
chief financial officer (CFO) calls a reforecast, when the controller
signs off a month's close, or when someone proposes to spend more than
`<your threshold>` on a project, an asset or a commitment, or to spend on
anything the budget does not hold. The processes here set the year's
budget from agreed assumptions, each department head's draft and the
labor cost in the approved workforce plan, rebuild the outlook for the
rest of the year from the results so far, the revenue forecast Revenue
operations publishes and the hiring plan, set each month's results beside
the budget and the latest forecast with every difference that matters
explained by its owner and traced to the ledger, and write up each
investment case with its cost, its return and its risks on assumptions
finance has checked. The agents gather, check, model, draft and record,
and they approve nothing. Agreeing the assumptions, settling the
trade-offs between departments, approving the budget, each reforecast
and each investment case, and releasing the monthly report always stay
with named people, and every approver is read from the delegation of
authority at the version in force.
