# Value stream: Billing and collecting

`vs/fin/billing-and-collecting v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a customer wants to buy on credit or needs more credit
than it has, when a booked order or a period of metered use reaches its
billing date, when a customer's payment lands in the bank, when an invoice
passes its due date unpaid, when a customer says an invoice is wrong, or
when collection has ended without recovering a debt. The processes here
assess each customer's credit against the credit policy, build each
invoice from the booking, the billing terms of the agreement and the
metered usage and send it on its date, match each payment to the invoices
it pays and hold aside whatever does not match, follow up each overdue
invoice on a schedule set by the customer's risk, answer each billing
dispute with evidence inside the time promised, and set out each debt that
cannot be collected for the people who decide on it. Quotes, discounts and
the booking itself stay in Sales and Revenue operations, and this stream
starts from the booked order. Setting a customer's credit limit, holding a
customer's orders, agreeing a payment plan or a settlement, deciding a
dispute, approving a credit note or a refund, passing a debt to an agency
and writing off a debt always stay with the named people the delegation of
authority names, and no agent moves money, sets credit or gives up any
part of a debt on its own.
