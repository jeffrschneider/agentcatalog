# Value stream: Travel and expenses

`vs/fin/travel-and-expenses v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when an employee asks for a business trip, when an employee
submits an expense claim, when a manager asks for a company card for an
employee or for a change to a card's limit, when a card holder's last day
is recorded or a named person finds that a holder has broken the card
rules, or when a card statement period closes. The processes here book
each trip inside the travel policy and send any trip outside it to a
named approver before anything is booked, keep each traveler's itinerary
and whereabouts for the length of the trip so a named person can reach
them in an emergency, check every expense claim and every card
transaction line by line against the expense policy, its receipt and its
business reason, pay each approved claim back in the next payment run
with any part that local rules treat as pay passed to payroll, issue each
company card at the limit and the merchant rules a named person set and
freeze or close it on the day its holder breaks the rules or leaves, and
reconcile each card account to the issuer's statement and the ledger. The
employee's manager approves every claim and every card transaction, and
named people decide each trip, line or transaction outside the policy,
set each card limit, approve and release every payment in the payment
run, and sign off each card reconciliation they did not prepare. No agent
sets a limit, approves an exception or moves money itself.
