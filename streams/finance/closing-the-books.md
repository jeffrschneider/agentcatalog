# Value stream: Closing the books

`vs/fin/closing-the-books v1`

**position:** 6
A run starts when an entry has to go into the ledger that no system feed
produces, when a period ends and its accruals, reconciliations, revenue
and asset entries are due, when a lease is signed or changed, when a month
has to be closed, or when a quarter or a year ends and the group's accounts
and financial statements are due. The processes here prepare and post each
manual journal with its support, book what the period owes and has not yet
been invoiced or paid, reconcile every balance sheet account to its
statement, sub-ledger or schedule, work out each period's revenue from what
each customer agreement promised and what was delivered, keep the register
of fixed assets and leases and post their entries each period, close each
month on its calendar, consolidate the entities into one set of group
accounts in one currency, and draft the financial statements and their
notes under `<your accounting framework>`. Payroll is run in Human
resources, commissions are calculated in Revenue operations and a fund's
accounts are kept in Private capital, and this stream takes their figures
into the company's own books. Whoever prepares a journal or a
reconciliation never approves it. Approving each manual journal entry,
with those above `<your threshold>` going to the approvers the delegation
of authority names, deciding each judgment about revenue, an asset or a
lease, signing off the close, and signing off the financial statements
before any of them is reported outside the company always stay with named
people.
