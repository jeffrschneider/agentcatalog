# Value stream: Cash and banking

`vs/fin/cash-and-banking v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts each working morning when the banks report the day's
balances, each week when the cash forecast falls due, when an account
needs cash for the payments due from it or holds more than it needs, when
Human resources approves a pay register, when someone asks to open or
close a bank account or to change who may sign for an account or release
payments from it, including when a leaver or a mover holds that
authority, and when a loan or a credit facility reaches a date on which
its covenants are tested. The processes here gather every account into
one cash position each morning and raise anything unexpected the same
day, forecast the cash the company will hold over the coming weeks and
months and raise any shortfall early with the options for meeting it,
prepare and check each transfer between the company's accounts and each
funding of the payroll account, keep every bank account, signer and
releaser current with the approval behind each, and test every covenant
on the loan agreement's own definitions before the compliance
certificate goes to the lenders. The agents gather, match, forecast,
prepare, check and record, and no agent moves money or holds the access
to do so. The approval and the release of every transfer, every decision
on a shortfall or a suspected fraud, the placing of surplus cash, every
opening or closing of an account and every change to who may sign or
release, the response to a covenant at risk, and the signature on every
certificate that goes to a lender always stay with named people, and
every transfer is released by two of them under the company's dual
control rule.
