# Value stream: Controls and audit

`vs/fin/controls-and-audit v1`

**position:** 8
A run starts when the board or the people it names change who may
approve what, when a finance policy is needed or reaches its review date,
when a control on the control list reaches its test date, when the period
for checking access to the finance systems ends, or when the external
auditor's engagement for the year is signed. The processes here keep one
delegation of authority at a version that every approval in the company
reads, publish each finance policy at a version and collect the
acknowledgments of the people it covers, test each key control on its
schedule with a sample sized before testing starts and test each fix
again before its finding closes, find every person who could both prepare
and approve the same thing and remove the access nobody approved, and
take the external auditor from the agreed plan to the signed opinion with
every request answered from the records. The agents draft, list, sample,
test, track and remind. Approving the delegation of authority and each
finance policy, rating each failed control and closing its finding,
confirming each access that stays and allowing any clash of duties with
another check in its place, approving each answer before it reaches the
auditor, deciding each adjustment the auditor proposes, and signing the
representation letter always stay with named people.
