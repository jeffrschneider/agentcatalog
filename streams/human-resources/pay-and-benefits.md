# Value stream: Pay and benefits

`vs/hr/pay-and-benefits v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a pay period reaches its cutoff for changes, when the
payroll year ends, when a manager asks to change an employee's pay, when
the year's pay review or the renewal of the benefit plans opens, when an
employee becomes eligible for benefits or reports a life event, or when an
employee asks for a leave of absence. The processes here gather each
period's changes and pay every employee the right amount on the right day
with the withholding paid and filed, reconcile the payroll year and send
each person the year-end pay and tax statement their location requires,
check every pay change and the yearly pay review against the range, the
budget and the pay of people doing like work, renew the benefit plans and
carry every election to the carriers and to payroll, and support each
employee through a leave from the request to the return. A named finance
person checks each pay register before a named approver releases the
payments. Every pay change, the approval of the pay review, the choice of
benefit plans, each leave approval, and every signature and release of
money always stay with named people, and the agents never weigh a
protected characteristic in anything they do about an employee's pay,
benefits or leave.
