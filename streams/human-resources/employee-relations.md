# Value stream: Employee relations

`vs/hr/employee-relations v1`

**position:** 6
A run starts when an employee raises a concern in any channel, named or
anonymous, when a complaint is sent for investigation, when a manager
reports a conduct matter, when an employee asks for a change to a job or a
workplace, or when the employee survey comes due. The processes here log
and acknowledge every concern and bring it to a written answer from a
named person inside the promised time, support a named investigator from
the allegations to findings the investigator signs, set a conduct matter
against the policy and the outcomes of similar past cases before anyone
decides it, carry an accommodation request from the first conversation to
a change that is reviewed on its date, and run a survey in which no answer
can be traced to a person, reported only for groups above a minimum size
and turned into actions with owners and dates. Health and investigation
records are open only to the named people who must see them, and the
agents never weigh a protected characteristic in anything they do about a
person. The route a concern takes and the answer it gets, the findings of
every investigation, every disciplinary outcome and appeal, and every
accommodation decision always stay with named people.
