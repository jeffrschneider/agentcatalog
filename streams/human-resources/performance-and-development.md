# Value stream: Performance and development

`vs/hr/performance-and-development v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts when the company opens its review cycle or its promotion
round, when a manager raises a lasting gap in someone's work, when leaders
review the roles the company can least afford to lose, when the year's
learning is planned or a required course falls due, or when an employee
applies for or is offered another role inside the company. The processes
here gather the evidence of each person's work and check that every rating
and every promotion case rests on an example of it, prepare the packs that
calibration and promotion panels read, keep score on an improvement plan
for the employee and for the support the company promised, set out who
could step into each key role and how ready each one is, turn the year's
needs into a learning program within its budget, assign and chase required
training until the record is complete, and carry an internal move to a set
date with the pay, the record and the work handed over. Every rating,
promotion, pay change, improvement plan outcome, choice of successor and
internal hiring decision stays with a named person, and the agents put the
evidence in front of that person, leave every protected characteristic out
of what they prepare, and record the decision.
