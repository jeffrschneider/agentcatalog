# Value stream: Onboarding

`vs/hr/onboarding v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when a candidate signs an offer and the hire passes from
hiring to onboarding. The processes here prepare for the new hire before
the first day: they create the employee record, collect the forms and the
proof of the right to work under the rules of the place the new hire
works, set up payroll and benefits, and order and test the access and the
equipment. They take each group of new starters through the company, its
policies and the first required training, with every acknowledgment of a
policy recorded at its version, and they carry the manager and the new
hire through a plan for the first months, with each check-in on its date,
to an outcome on the employee record. The agents gather the forms,
schedule, check, record and remind, and each one sees only the personal
data its step needs. A person examines the documents for the right to
work, and a start that cannot go ahead and the confirmation, extension or
end of a probation period always stay with a named person.
