# Value stream: Hiring

`vs/hr/hiring v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts when a position has been approved and the hiring manager asks
for it to be filled. The processes here agree the must-haves and a
structured interview plan with scored questions before anyone is
approached, write the job posting and check it for the statements each
location requires before it is published, find people who match the
must-haves through employee referrals and past applicants who agreed to be
kept on file, read each application against the written must-haves with
the details the step does not need withheld, brief the interview panel and
score each interview on its own against the plan, take references and run
the checks the role requires with the candidate's written consent, and
build, approve, negotiate and sign the offer that passes the hire to
onboarding. Every applicant hears back. Who goes forward, who is hired and
who is turned down, the decision on any background check finding, and
every offer stay with named people, and the agents never weigh a protected
characteristic in anything they do about a candidate.
