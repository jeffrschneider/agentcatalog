# Value stream: HR operations

`vs/hr/hr-operations v1`

**position:** 8
A run starts when an employee asks HR a question, a change to an
employee's record arrives, a policy has to be written or changed, a date
on the compliance calendar comes up, an auditor or an inspector writes,
someone asks to see, correct or delete the data the company holds about a
person, or the period for checking who can open employee data ends. The
processes here answer each employee from the approved policy library at
the version for their location, apply each record change on its effective
date with its evidence and pass it to payroll, benefits and access,
publish each policy at a version and collect the acknowledgments, prepare
each required filing from checked data and file it by its deadline, answer
each audit and each records request with only the data it covers, and keep
access to personal data to the people who need it. Every answer to a
personal or sensitive question, every signature on a policy, a filing or
an audit response, every decision to withhold, correct or delete a record,
and every decision about a breach of personal data stay with named people,
and health, investigation and background check records open only to the
named people who must see them.
