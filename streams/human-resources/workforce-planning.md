# Value stream: Workforce planning

`vs/hr/workforce-planning v1`

**position:** 1
A run starts when the business plan for the year is set and the leaders
have to decide how many people each team needs, when a manager asks for a
new or replacement position, when a leader proposes a change to how teams
are organized, or when a reporting period closes. The processes here build
the year's workforce plan with each team's headcount, roles and labor cost,
check each position request against that plan and place the job at a level
with its pay range before anyone is recruited, draw a reorganization as
costed options and record the chosen structure on one date, and report the
workforce against the plan each period with no figure shown for a small
group. The agents work with positions, roles and totals, and every decision
about a person goes to a named leader. The approval of the plan and of each
position, the choice of a new structure, what happens to each person in a
reorganization, and the release of each report always stay with named
people.
