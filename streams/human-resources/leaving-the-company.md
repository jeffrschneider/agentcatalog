# Value stream: Leaving the company

`vs/hr/leaving-the-company v1`

**position:** 7
A run starts when an employee resigns or retires, when a manager proposes
to dismiss someone, or when leaders open a reduction in roles. The
processes here check a proposed dismissal against the record, the policy
and the local rules with counsel, fix a layoff's selection criteria before
any name is applied and check the selection for unfair outcomes across
groups, draft and negotiate separation terms within the policy, and carry
every departure from the confirmed last day to the closed record, with the
work handed over, the property returned, access ended on the last day,
final pay worked out under the local rule, and each benefit ended or
continued. The reasons people give for leaving reach the workforce report
without their names. Every dismissal, every selection in a layoff, every
term past the separation policy, every signature and every release of
final pay always stay with named people, and the agents gather, draft,
check, schedule, record and remind.
