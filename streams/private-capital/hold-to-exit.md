# Value stream: Hold-to-Exit

`vs/pc/hold-to-exit v1`

**position:** 7
**friendly name:** Exits
A run starts when a holding is ready to be sold, or when there is nothing
left in it. The processes here work out the route, the timing and the
likely buyers with the company's management and its board, and take the
start of an exit to the investment committee. They run a sale through an
adviser, the buyers' diligence and a signed agreement, and they sell or
distribute the fund's shares in a company that has listed as the lock-up
and the trading rules allow. A company that has failed is closed out at
zero, documented for tax, and its lessons are written against the original
investment memo. Every sale is settled against the cap table, so the fund
knows what each class of share was paid. What comes out is cash or shares
the fund can distribute, or a write-off on the record, and paying each
investor its share belongs to Commitment-to-Statement.
