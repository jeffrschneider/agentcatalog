# Value stream: Committee approval and closing

`vs/pc/recommendation-to-close v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a deal team brings a finished investment memo to the
investment committee. The processes here put the memo to the committee and
record its vote with the conditions and any dissent, write the approved terms
onto a term sheet and agree it with the company, offer the share the fund
will not take alone to co-investors under the allocation policy, negotiate
the definitive agreements against the signed term sheet, and run the closing
until every condition is met, the documents are signed, the money has gone to
verified accounts and the new position is on the fund's books. A run can also
end in a decided no, recorded with its reason so that the next look at the
company starts from it. The committee's vote, every signature and every
release of money stay with people, and the agents do the drafting, checking
and tracking that bring each of them to a person who is ready to decide.
