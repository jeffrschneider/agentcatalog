# Value stream: Quarter-to-Mark

`vs/pc/quarter-to-mark v1`

**position:** 6
**friendly name:** Quarterly valuations
A run starts when a quarter closes and every company in the portfolio owes
the fund its numbers. The processes here collect each company's reports on
one template and read them against plan, the prior period and its loan
covenants, raising a covenant at risk or a short cash runway the day it is
found. They propose a fair value for every holding under the firm's
valuation policy, with the method, the inputs and the judgments written
down, and take the marks to the valuation committee, which approves them.
The partners then go through each company's condition, update the watch
list, and set the reserves the fund holds back for each company's later
rounds or add-on acquisitions. What comes out each quarter is a set of
approved marks the auditor can test, a current watch list and a reserve for
every company. The investor statements built from those marks belong to
Commitment-to-Statement.
