# Value stream: Due diligence

`vs/pc/opportunity-to-recommendation v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when a partner takes an opportunity on and names the deal
team. The processes here test the company's market, customers, product,
numbers, contracts and ownership against evidence, model what the fund
would earn at each outcome, and check the deal against the fund's
limits, its excused investors, the capital it has left and its
conflicts. Each strand of work leaves rated findings with an owner. The
stream closes with an investment memo that recommends investing on
stated terms, with every claim resting on a diligence finding, or with a
recorded pass and its reason. The vote on the memo belongs to the
investment committee in the next stream.
