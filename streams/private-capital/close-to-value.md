# Value stream: Close-to-Value

`vs/pc/close-to-value v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts when a deal closes and the company becomes a holding the firm
has to help grow. The processes here pass what the deal team learned and
promised to the people who will work with the company, agree a value creation
plan with management in which every change has an owner, a measure and a
date, prepare the firm's director for each board meeting and follow up what
the board decides, help fill the roles the plan depends on, and work a
company that is missing its plan or running short of cash through to a
decision by the partners. A follow-on round in a venture holding, or an
add-on acquisition in a buyout holding, starts here and then runs through the
diligence and committee processes like a new deal. A director's vote on a
portfolio company's board always stays with that director, and the agents
make sure the director walks in having read everything the vote rests on.
