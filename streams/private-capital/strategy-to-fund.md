# Value stream: Strategy-to-Fund

`vs/pc/strategy-to-fund v1`

**position:** 1
**friendly name:** Raising a fund
A run starts when the partners decide to raise a new fund. The processes
here write down what the fund will invest in, how large it will be and
what it will offer investors, set up the fund's entities and draft its
legal documents, and build the track record and the materials that carry
it. They then work each prospective limited partner (LP) from a first
approach to a commitment or a decline, answer the questionnaires
investors send, negotiate the partnership agreement and each side letter,
and hold each close through to the last one. What leaves the stream is a
fund with a written strategy, its legal documents in force, and signed
investor commitments at final close. The money those commitments promise
is called, accounted for and reported in Commitment-to-Statement, and the
search for companies to invest it in starts in Lead-to-Opportunity.
