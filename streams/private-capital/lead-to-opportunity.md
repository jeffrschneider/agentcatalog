# Value stream: Lead-to-Opportunity

`vs/pc/lead-to-opportunity v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts when a company comes to the firm's attention, whether a
founder pitched it, a contact referred it, a banker offered it in a sale
or the firm found it while mapping a sector. The processes here write
down the firm's thesis for a sector and watch the companies that fit it,
approach a company through the warmest path the firm has, screen what
arrives unasked against the fund's mandate, and answer a banker's sale
process by its deadline. Every company that reaches the firm gets a
recorded screening decision and an answer inside the time the firm
promised, including the ones it passes on. Each company worth pursuing
leaves as an opportunity in the deal pipeline with a named owner, and
the diligence that tests it belongs to the next stream.
