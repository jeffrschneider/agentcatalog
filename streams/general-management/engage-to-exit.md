# Value stream: Engage-to-Exit

`vs/gm/engage-to-exit v1`

**position:** 2
An organization brings an outside agent into its work much as it brings
in a new employee or contractor, and takes it out again with the same
care. A run starts when somebody asks for an outside agent to do work
the organization will rely on, and the stream ends when that agent is
out of the work with nothing of the organization's still open to it.
The processes here check and interview the agent before anyone signs,
put an NDA in force before anything confidential reaches it, form the
engagement, grant a mandate no wider than the work needs, and admit the
agent with grants that end when the engagement ends. When the
engagement ends, they hand over the work in flight, revoke the mandate,
withdraw and try every grant, settle the money, get the agent's owner to
confirm what happened to the data, and close the run with the owner's
signature over every record it left. The work the agent does while it
is in is owned by the departments that contracted it; this stream owns
bringing it in and taking it out.
