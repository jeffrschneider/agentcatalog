# Value stream: Plan-to-Commitment

`vs/prt/plan-to-commitment v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when both sides sit down to agree what the relationship is for
over the coming year. The processes here set the targets each side is
working to, name the accounts or segments to go after together, agree what
each side will put in by way of people, money and time, write it where both
sides can read it, and review it on a cadence with the numbers in front of
them. The review is what makes this a stream rather than a meeting: a joint
plan nobody looks at again is a document, and the partnerships that grow are
the ones where somebody keeps score.
