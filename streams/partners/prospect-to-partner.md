# Value stream: Prospect-to-Partner

`vs/prt/prospect-to-partner v1`

**position:** 1
A run starts when the company wants to reach a market, a customer set or a
capability it cannot reach alone. The processes here work out what kind of
partner would close that gap, find the candidates, qualify them on what they
can actually deliver rather than what they claim, negotiate the agreement
that governs the relationship, and sign it. A signed agreement is where this
run ends and where several others begin. The strategic arrangements that
change what the company itself can offer are a different stream, because
what comes out of those is a product rather than a route to one.
