# Value stream: Partner-to-Productive

`vs/prt/partner-to-productive v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts at a signed agreement and ends when the partner can sell or
deliver without being carried. The processes here grant access to the
portal, the materials and the systems, train and certify the people who will
do the work, walk the first deal or the first delivery alongside them, and
confirm they can do it again alone. A partner signed and never onboarded is
the most common way a partner program produces nothing at all, which is why
this is a stream rather than a checklist at the end of recruiting.
