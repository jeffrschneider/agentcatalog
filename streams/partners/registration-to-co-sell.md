# Value stream: Registration-to-Co-sell

`vs/prt/registration-to-co-sell v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a partner registers a deal or a joint pursuit begins. The
processes here check and approve the registration, protect it for the period
the agreement promises, decide who leads and who supports, keep both sides
working the same deal without the customer being approached twice, resolve
the conflicts when a partner's deal meets a direct one, and record the
outcome against the partner. The customer-facing work runs through the
ordinary sales streams. What is particular here is that two organizations
are trying to close one deal, and the rules that keep it from becoming a
fight.
