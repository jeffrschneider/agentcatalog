# Value stream: Process-to-Adoption

`vs/rev/process-to-adoption v1`

**position:** 10
A run starts when a way of working is written or changed, and ends when it
is what people actually do. The processes here document it, communicate it
to the people who have to follow it, instrument the systems so that
departure from it is visible, review what the instrumentation says, and
change either the process or the enforcement. This stream is the reason the
rest of the catalog is worth writing down. A documented process nobody
follows is a description of an intention, and the difference between the two
is measurable.
