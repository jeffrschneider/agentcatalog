# Value stream: Pipeline-to-Forecast

`vs/rev/pipeline-to-forecast v1`

**position:** 7
A run starts on the reporting cadence and produces the numbers the
commitment conversation argues over. The processes here enforce the hygiene
that makes a pipeline readable, roll deals up by whatever dimensions
leadership needs, compare against history and coverage ratios, and publish a
single view everybody is looking at at the same time. What this stream must
not do is make the call. It produces an arithmetic anybody can reproduce,
and the judgment about what is committed belongs to the people who carry the
number.
