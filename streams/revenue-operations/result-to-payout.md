# Value stream: Result-to-Payout

`vs/rev/result-to-payout v1`

**position:** 6
A run starts when a plan period opens and ends when everybody has been paid
what the plan says. The processes here design the plans against what
leadership wants to happen, model what they will cost across several
outcomes, publish them so each person can read their own, calculate what
each closed deal earns, work through the disputes that follow, and pay. A
compensation plan is a set of instructions people will follow exactly, which
is why the modelling matters more than the arithmetic. Finance owns money
leaving the building; this stream owns what the number should be and why.
