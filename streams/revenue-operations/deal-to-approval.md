# Value stream: Deal-to-Approval

`vs/rev/deal-to-approval v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts when a deal wants terms the standard ones do not cover: a
discount past the threshold, a non-standard term, an unusual structure, an
exception to policy. The processes here review what is being asked, price
the risk, get the approval of whoever actually carries that risk, record the
decision with its reason, and hand back either an approved shape or a
refusal that says what would change it. Speed is part of what this stream
produces. A desk that takes a week teaches sellers to work around it, and
the exceptions that never reach it are the ones that cost money.
