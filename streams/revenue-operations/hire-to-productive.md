# Value stream: Hire-to-Productive

`vs/rev/hire-to-productive v1`

**position:** 9
A run starts when somebody joins a revenue team and ends when they can do
the job unaided. The processes here run the onboarding, teach the product
and the market, teach the process the organization actually uses rather than
the one it wrote down, certify that a person can do the parts that matter
before they are let near a customer, and keep the material current as the
product and the market move. It also covers what changes for everybody at
once: a launch, a repricing, a new competitor. A launch nobody can sell is a
marketing stream that ended too early, and this is where it lands.
