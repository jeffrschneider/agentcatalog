# Value stream: Proposal-to-Signature

`vs/sls/proposal-to-signature v1`

**position:** 6
A run starts when an offer is in the buyer's hands and ends at a signature
or a decided loss. The processes here run the negotiation, carry redlines
back and forth with legal, keep a close plan naming every step both sides
still owe, work through the buyer's own procurement process, and get the
agreement executed. A loss is an outcome this stream produces properly: a
decided no, with a reason, closed on the record, because a deal left open is
worse than one lost. What the signature obliges the company to do begins in
the next stream.
