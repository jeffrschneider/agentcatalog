# Value stream: Solution-to-Proposal

`vs/sls/solution-to-proposal v1`

**position:** 5
A run starts when the shape of a solution is agreed and somebody has to turn
it into an offer. The processes here configure what will actually be sold,
price it against the rate card and whatever the customer already bought,
assemble the quote, write the proposal that says what is included and what
is not, and answer the security, legal and procurement questionnaires that
arrive with any serious purchase. Anything non-standard goes for approval
before it leaves. The approval machinery itself, who may discount what and
on what evidence, belongs to operations; what leaves this stream is an offer
the customer can say yes to.
