# Value stream: Account-to-Plan

`vs/sls/account-to-plan v1`

**position:** 1
A run starts when an account is named as one worth a plan of its own: a
target account from a territory carve, an existing customer the company
wants more of, or an account leadership has decided to pursue. The processes
here research what the account is trying to do, map who decides and who
influences, work out where it already spends and with whom, write the plan
that says what to sell, to whom and in what order, and keep that plan
current as people move and priorities change. A plan commits nobody outside
the account team, and the money it hopes for appears in a forecast only once
a deal is open. Territories and quotas are cut in a different stream, and so
is the machinery that holds the account record.
