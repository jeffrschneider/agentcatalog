# Value stream: Inquiry-to-Qualified

`vs/sls/inquiry-to-qualified v1`

**position:** 3
A run starts when an inquiry lands with an owner: a form somebody filled in,
a trial they started, an event they came to, a referral from a partner. The
processes here answer it inside the clock the organization has promised, ask
the questions that separate a real evaluation from a browse, record what was
learned in a shape the next stage can read, and end in a decision to work
it, to send it back for nurture, or to say no and say why. The decision is
what this stream produces. The routing that delivered the inquiry is
operations, the demand that created it is marketing, and the discovery that
follows a yes is the next stream here.
