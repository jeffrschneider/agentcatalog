# Value stream: Prospect-to-Meeting

`vs/sls/prospect-to-meeting v1`

**position:** 2
A run starts when somebody decides to reach people who have not asked to
hear from us: a list built from a territory, the accounts named in a plan,
or a trigger somebody noticed. The processes here build the list and check
it, decide what is worth saying to each part of it, run the sequence across
mail, phone and social, keep every message inside the consent and
suppression rules, and hand over a meeting that actually happened along with
what was said and what the person wanted. A run ends at the meeting rather
than at the reply, because a booked meeting nobody attended is a run that
failed. Reaching people who did ask to hear from us is marketing, and the
rules that decide who owns an answer belong to operations.
