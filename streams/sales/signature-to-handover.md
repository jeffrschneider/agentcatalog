# Value stream: Signature-to-Handover

`vs/sls/signature-to-handover v1`

**position:** 7
A run starts at signature and ends when somebody other than the seller owns
the relationship. The processes here book the order against the right entity
and terms, check that what was sold is what can be delivered, introduce the
team who will deliver it, hand over everything learned during the sale so
the customer does not have to repeat themselves, and schedule the start.
Invoicing and collections are finance, and this stream stops at the booking.
A handover that never happens is how a signed customer becomes an unhappy
one, which is why it is a stream rather than a step at the end of closing.
