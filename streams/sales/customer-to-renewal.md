# Value stream: Customer-to-Renewal

`vs/sls/customer-to-renewal v1`

**position:** 8
A run starts well before an agreement ends, on a clock the agreement itself
sets. The processes here watch what the customer uses against what they
bought, surface the risks early enough that somebody can still act on them,
agree what the next term should be, price it, and get it signed. A renewal
that comes back smaller is still a completed run, and one that ends in a
departure produces a reason the rest of the organization can learn from.
Growing an account is a different stream because it is a different
conversation: this one is about continuing.
