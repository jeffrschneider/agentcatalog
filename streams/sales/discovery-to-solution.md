# Value stream: Discovery-to-Solution

`vs/sls/discovery-to-solution v1`

**position:** 4
A run starts when a qualified opportunity has to be understood before
anything can be offered. The processes here interview the people who feel
the problem, record what they need in their own words, test what would have
to be true for a purchase to make sense, bring in the specialists who can
say whether it is buildable, and arrange the references and evaluations a
buyer needs when description is not enough. A run ends at an agreed shape of
what would fix the problem, and nothing in it is priced. The offer is the
next stream, and a discovery that ends in no viable shape has closed an
opportunity properly rather than failed.
