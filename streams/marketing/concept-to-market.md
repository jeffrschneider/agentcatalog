# Value stream: Concept-to-Market

`vs/mkt/concept-to-market v1`

**position:** 4
A new product, feature or price is made ready for the market, so that
customers hear about it and the people selling it can answer for it. A run
starts when a launch date is committed, when a feature reaches customers,
when a pricing decision is approved, when sales names a gap it cannot close
in a live deal, or when somebody reports a move by a competitor. The
processes here settle what one product stands for and which claims it can
make, coordinate a full launch across every channel, announce the smaller
feature releases, move the published prices and every existing customer
onto new terms, build the deck and the objection answers reps carry into a
deal, rewrite the battlecards a competitor move has put in doubt, and
recruit and train the partners who sell on the organization's behalf. What
these runs have in common is a market date somebody outside marketing has
already fixed, and that date sets every deadline inside them. A run
finishes when the story is settled, the signed material is in the hands of
the people who need it, every channel is carrying the announcement, and the
first weeks have been reported.
