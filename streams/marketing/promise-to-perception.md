# Value stream: Promise-to-Perception

`vs/mkt/promise-to-perception v1`

**position:** 3
The organization decides what its brand stands for, how it looks and how it
sounds, and then holds the published material to that standard. A run
starts when a change in strategy or in the market is big enough that
leadership reopens the positioning, when an agreed change to the identity
or the voice has to be written into the guidelines, when the audit or the
brand tracker falls due, or when something needs a name. The processes here
settle the position and the promise, build the visual system and the logo,
write the voice as numbered rules a scoring seat can pass or fail material
against, publish the guidelines and the asset kit, and carry a new identity
across every page, template and registration the organization already
holds. The brand audit samples what has already been published and names
every place it has moved away from the standard, and the brand tracker asks
people outside the organization what they have heard of and what they
believe the brand stands for. A run finishes when the brand owner, legal
and leadership have signed a numbered version and every process that scores
material has been told which version now applies.
