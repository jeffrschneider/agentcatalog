# Value stream: Story-to-Coverage

`vs/mkt/story-to-coverage v1`

Something the organization wants said in public is put in front of
reporters, analysts and audiences. A run starts when leadership confirms a
piece of news and clears it to be said, when somebody judges a story angle
worth telling, when a research firm opens an evaluation cycle, when a call
for papers or an award opens, when a show or an event is booked, or when an
incident that will become public is reported to the escalation channel. The
processes here write and issue a release, offer a story to the reporters
who cover the subject, put executives in front of an industry analyst and
read what that firm later publishes, issue a holding line during a crisis
and drive the wording through legal and leadership, get a speaker accepted
and rehearsed, file an award entry before its deadline, build a stand at a
trade show, fill the room at a field event, and run the annual user
conference. Somebody outside the organization decides the outcome of most
of these runs, so the middle of one is a series of offers and follow-ups
waiting on an answer nobody here controls. The runs stay open long past the
day, because the coverage, the judges' decision or the recording arrives
weeks later and somebody still owes a report on it.
