# Value stream: Customer-to-Advocate

`vs/mkt/customer-to-advocate v1`

**position:** 9
Customers are brought up to speed, kept in touch with, and some of them
agree to speak for the organization in public. A run starts when a customer
begins, when a publishing cycle or a duty period opens, when a churn risk
signal fires, when somebody asks for a reference, or when reporting
identifies a segment that has gone quiet. The processes here walk one new
customer to the milestones that count as active, send the newsletter, work
one duty period of the community and hand on whatever is still open, ask
eligible customers for reviews inside the rules each review site publishes,
find a customer who will act as a reference and record what their
permission covers and when it runs out, seat an advisory board under signed
agreements, answer a churn signal with an intervention a person approved,
and try to bring back the contacts who have stopped responding. Nothing
here reaches a customer until the consent record has been read and the
opt-outs suppressed, and no customer is named in public until that customer
has agreed in writing. A run ends with an outcome recorded against the
account, or with a permission written onto the standing list that the seats
producing customer material read before they use anybody's name.
