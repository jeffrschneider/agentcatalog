# Value stream: Idea-to-Asset

`vs/mkt/idea-to-asset v1`

**position:** 5
A content or creative need becomes a finished asset that somebody has
approved and published. A run starts when a planning period opens and the
department has to settle what it will publish, when a topic or a creative
brief is approved, when a campaign asks for a page to send people to, when
somebody asks for a change to a page that is already live, or when a
content audit comes due. The processes here agree the slate for the period,
write the articles and guides, produce the copy, images and video a
campaign asks for, cut a video with its captions written and its rights
cleared, build and measure a landing page, edit pages that are already
live, write a customer story inside the terms that customer agreed to, and
go back through everything already published to update it, merge it or take
it down. The approval process is called by all the others: it takes
finished material at a version, runs the brand, legal and accuracy checks
over it, and hands back either a cleared version with its record or a list
of failures with the rule behind each one. A run finishes when the asset is
live on its date and the seat that made it has reported how it did against
what the request asked for.
