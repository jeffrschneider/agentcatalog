# Value stream: Market-to-Audience

`vs/mkt/market-to-audience v1`

The organization works out who it sells to and writes that down where every
other process can read it. A run starts when a go-to-market question is
opened in planning, when the review date on a recorded profile, set of
segments or persona arrives, or when somebody needs a number for how large
a market is. The processes here divide the market into named segments,
agree the profile of the account worth selling to, describe the person
inside that account who actually buys, map how that person buys and what
they ask at each stage, size the market, read the organization's own record
of who bought and who stayed, and go out and ask customers the questions
that record cannot answer. What each run produces is an agreement written
down at a version, carrying the evidence under it and the names of everyone
who agreed and everyone who did not. A run is finished when that version is
in the hands of the seats that qualify accounts, plan content and target
campaigns, with the date it comes back up already set.
