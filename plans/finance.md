# Finance

A first pass at the catalog's department for a company's own finance function, at a company of a few hundred people or more. It follows the company's money from the budget that sets what each department may spend, through buying, paying, billing and collecting, to the closed books, the tax returns and the audit, and it covers the controls that hold that work together. It has 8 value streams, 45 processes and 47 agents (21 new, 26 reused). Activity tables come later.

## 1. Decisions

I made these calls so the work could start. Jeff can override any of them, and the documents that follow are written to them.

1. **Scope.** The department models a company's own finance function at a company of a few hundred people or more, which may have several legal entities in several countries. It does not model an accounting firm, a bank or an outsourced finance provider that serves other companies, though an outside accountant, payroll provider, tax adviser or auditor may hold a role for the company in a roster, and the roster says which.
2. **Payroll stays in Human resources.** HR runs payroll from the changes to the payments in `ref/hr/run-payroll` and `ref/hr/close-the-payroll-year`, and the finance controller's check of each register and the posting of the period's payroll journal stay inside those processes as HR wrote them. Finance picks up from the approved register and the posted journal: Fund the Payroll moves the money into the payroll account before the approver releases the payments, Book the Period-End Accruals accrues pay earned and not yet paid, and Reconcile a Balance Sheet Account reconciles the payroll accounts each month. These reuse the `payroll-administrator` and `wire-check` agents and never run a second payroll. The labor cost in the budget comes from HR's workforce plan through the `workforce-planner` agent, and a leaver's company card and open expenses are closed from the record that `ref/hr/offboard-a-leaver` keeps.
3. **A fund's accounting stays in Private capital.** Capital calls, distributions, the fees and expenses charged to a fund, the capital accounts and the investor statements belong to `vs/pc/commitment-to-statement`, and Finance covers only the company's own books. An investment firm's own management company, which has its own suppliers, payroll and ledger, would use this department for those books, and its funds would use Private capital. The `wire-check` agent serves both departments, and nothing in Finance reaches into a fund's capital accounts.
4. **Revenue operations and Sales keep commissions, quotes and discounts.** Commissions are calculated, approved and disputed in `vs/rev/result-to-payout`, and the signed file reaches payroll from `ref/rev/calculate-commission`. Finance accrues the commission earned and not yet paid at each period end from the `commission-calculator` agent's figures, and pays it through payroll. Quotes, discounts and non-standard terms stay in Sales and in `vs/rev/deal-to-approval`. Finance invoices from the booking the `order-check` agent made from the signed agreement, a customer's credit limit is an input the deal desk reads, and each reforecast reads the revenue forecast Revenue operations publishes without building a second one.
5. **Finance steps inside other departments stay there.** Where another department's process already names a finance person, such as the finance partner who signs a commission run, the finance lead who checks the workforce plan, finance signing marketing's verified media spend or finance committing the money for a revenue tool, that step stays in that process, and the Finance processes take its output. Marketing keeps dividing its own budget with the `budget-keeper` agent, inside the total the company budget gives it.
6. **Purchasing belongs here.** At a company of this size purchasing usually reports to the chief financial officer (CFO), and the purchase order is the first control on what the company spends, so the purchase request, the purchase order and the choice of a supplier for a large purchase sit in the Buying and paying stream beside the invoice and the payment. A department of its own for procurement makes sense at a larger company, and it is left for later together with strategic sourcing, category plans and supplier performance reviews. A department's own evaluation of a tool or an agency, such as `ref/rev/choose-a-tool`, still runs in that department, and the purchase it leads to runs through Issue a Purchase Order.
7. **Written for any country.** Accounting frameworks and tax rules differ by country, so no document states one country's rule as the rule. Where a local rule decides a step, such as how revenue or a lease is accounted for, what an invoice must show, how long an invoice is kept, when a tax return is due or what a supplier must provide for tax, the document leaves a blank such as `<your accounting framework>`, `<your jurisdiction's indirect tax rules>` or `<your jurisdiction's rule on invoice retention>`, and the adopting company's accountants and advisers fill it in. A company with entities in several countries fills each blank once for each entity.
8. **People decide about money.** A named person approves and releases every payment, signs off the close and the financial statements, approves each manual journal entry above `<your threshold>`, writes off a debt, sets a customer's credit limit, approves a budget, a reforecast and an investment case, takes each tax position and signs each tax return, approves every change to a supplier's bank details once a person has confirmed it through a channel already on file, and approves anything reported outside the company, such as a covenant certificate or a response to an auditor or a tax authority. The phases that hold these decisions carry `automation: never`. Agents gather, match, reconcile, draft, check, schedule, record and remind.
9. **Duties stay separate.** Whoever prepares a payment, a journal or a reconciliation never approves it, every payment is released by two named people under `<your dual control rule>`, and no agent moves money itself or holds the access to do so. A request to change bank details that arrives by email alone is treated as an attempt at fraud until a person has confirmed it through a contact already on file, and the message that asked for the change is never used to confirm it. Review Access to Finance Systems checks each period that nobody can both prepare and approve the same thing.
10. **One delegation of authority.** Every threshold and every approver in these processes is read from the company's delegation of authority at the version in force, which the new `delegation-keeper` agent holds. Each process shows where a number goes, and the board or the people it names set the number.
11. **Reused agents.** Twenty-six existing agents take work here. Ten of them need their purpose or Not line widened in place (`budget-keeper`, `wire-check`, `dispute-coordinator`, `payroll-administrator`, `close-plan-keeper`, `disclosure-check`, `filing-coordinator`, `policy-keeper`, `statistician` and `supplier-check`), following the Private Capital and Human Resources decisions to widen in place and make no copies. The other sixteen fit as they stand. Section 6 quotes each change.
12. **Stream names.** Following Jeff's rule, each stream's title is a friendly descriptor in a buyer's words, its slug is the kebab form of that title, and its document has no friendly name line.
13. **Left for later.** Each of these is left for a later pass, for the reason given.
   - Mergers and acquisitions: deals are rare, run with outside advisers, and need diligence and integration work of their own, which the Private capital diligence processes already cover from the buyer's side.
   - Investor relations and reporting to a securities regulator: for a listed company this runs under disclosure rules that differ by market, and a private company reports to its shareholders through the board.
   - Equity and stock plans: a committee of the board decides grants on its own cycle, and finance, HR and legal share the plan administration, which the Human Resources plan also leaves for later.
   - Insurance: risk or legal usually owns it, with renewals and claims on a cycle of their own.
   - The long-range plan: the executive team and the board set it with the strategy, and Set the Annual Budget starts from its first year.
   - Hedging currency and interest rates: it needs a hedging policy the board approves and specialist treasury work that few companies of this size run themselves.
   - Inventory and product costing: only companies that hold stock need it, and operations shares the work.
   - Transfer pricing between entities: each country sets its own rules, and the company usually sets the prices with outside advisers.
   - A department of its own for procurement: see decision 6.

## 2. Department name

**Recommended: Finance.** It is the name most companies use for the function the CFO leads, it reads plainly beside Sales, Marketing and Human Resources, and it covers accounting, planning, treasury and tax without naming any one of them.

- **Finance and Accounting.** This name says outright that the controller's work is included, but it is longer, and buyers already read accounting as part of finance.
- **Office of the CFO.** Some companies use this name for the whole function, but it names the leader, and a catalog list reads more clearly when each entry names the work.

I set aside "Accounting" because it leaves out planning, treasury and tax, which most buyers count as finance work.

Proposed ids, following the catalog's pattern: folders `streams/finance/` and `processes/finance/`, ids `vs/fin/<slug>` and `ref/fin/<slug>`, and a row in `departments.md` that reads `finance | Finance | Department`.

## 3. Value streams

| # | Stream | Slug | Outcome it produces | Main human roles |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Planning and forecasting | `planning-and-forecasting` | An approved budget for the year at a version, a forecast redone on a set cadence that the leaders plan against, a monthly report that explains every difference from the plan that matters, and every investment above the threshold approved by the people the delegation of authority names. | CFO, head of financial planning, executive team, board, department heads and budget owners, head of HR |
| 2 | Buying and paying | `buying-and-paying` | Everything the company buys is bought on an approved order from a supplier whose record and bank details were checked, every invoice is matched before it is approved, and every supplier is paid the right amount on its due date by people who did not prepare the payment. | requesters and budget owners, purchasing lead, payables lead, the named approvers and payment releasers, treasurer, suppliers |
| 3 | Travel and expenses | `travel-and-expenses` | Employees travel and spend inside the policy, every expense claim and card transaction is backed by a receipt and a business reason and approved by the employee's manager, and every employee is paid back on time. | employees and card holders, managers, travel and expense lead, payables lead, the card issuer and the travel agency |
| 4 | Billing and collecting | `billing-and-collecting` | Every customer is invoiced correctly and on time for what it signed for and used, every payment is applied to the invoices it pays, overdue money is collected, disputes are answered with evidence, and only named people set credit and write off debt. | billing lead, credit and collections lead, controller, account owners, customers and their payables teams, collection agencies |
| 5 | Cash and banking | `cash-and-banking` | The company knows each morning how much cash it holds and can see weeks ahead whether it will have enough, money moves only under dual control, every bank account and signer is current and approved, and every loan covenant and lender report is met. | treasurer, CFO, controller, payroll manager, authorized signers and payment releasers, banks and lenders |
| 6 | Closing the books | `closing-the-books` | Every month closes on its calendar with every journal supported and approved and every balance sheet account reconciled and reviewed, revenue, assets and leases are accounted for under `<your accounting framework>`, the group is consolidated, and the named officers sign the financial statements. | controller, accountants, revenue accounting lead, the reviewers and approvers of journals and reconciliations, entity controllers, CFO |
| 7 | Filing and paying tax | `filing-and-paying-tax` | Every tax return is filed and paid by its date in each jurisdiction, the tax in the accounts is calculated and supported each quarter, every tax position is taken by a named person, and every notice and audit is answered on time. | head of tax, CFO, controller, the named signers, outside tax advisers, tax authorities |
| 8 | Controls and audit | `controls-and-audit` | Every approval in the company reads one current delegation of authority, the finance policies are current and acknowledged, the key controls are tested and their failures fixed, nobody can both prepare and approve the same thing, and the external audit finishes on its timetable. | CFO, controller, audit committee, board, control owners, controls lead or internal audit, external auditor |

Some decisions stay with people in every process: approving and releasing every payment, approving a budget, a reforecast and an investment case, setting a customer's credit limit, writing off a debt, approving a manual journal entry above the threshold, signing off the close and the financial statements, taking a tax position and signing a tax return, approving a change to a supplier's bank details, and approving anything reported outside the company.

**Later.** Mergers and acquisitions, investor relations, equity and stock plans, insurance, the long-range plan, hedging, inventory and product costing, transfer pricing and a department of its own for procurement are left for a later pass, for the reasons in decision 13.

## 4. Processes (45)

A slug marked * is an existing catalog agent. Every other slug is a new agent from section 5. Where a process holds a money decision, its description names who decides, and the phase that holds that decision carries `automation: never`. Every threshold and approver named below is read from the delegation of authority at its version, so `<your threshold>` in a description is a number the adopting company sets there.

### Planning and forecasting

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Set the Annual Budget | Each year finance sets the calendar and the assumptions, each department head drafts a budget inside them with the labor cost from the workforce plan, finance puts the drafts together and tests them against the company's targets, the executive team settles the trade-offs, and the people the delegation of authority names approve the budget before it is issued at a version. | planning-analyst, workforce-planner*, budget-keeper*, decision-coordinator* |
| Reforecast the Year | On the company's cadence the outlook for the rest of the year is rebuilt from the results so far, the revenue forecast Revenue operations publishes, the hiring plan and the changes already known, every movement from the last forecast is explained, and named people approve the reforecast before anyone plans against it. | planning-analyst, budget-keeper*, decision-coordinator* |
| Report the Monthly Results | Once the month is closed, the results are set beside the budget and the latest forecast, each budget owner explains every difference above `<your threshold>`, and the leaders receive one report with every figure traced to the ledger. | reporting-manager*, planning-analyst, budget-keeper* |
| Approve an Investment Case | A proposal to spend above `<your threshold>` on a project, an asset or a commitment outside the budget is written up with its cost, its return and its risks on assumptions finance checks, and the people the delegation of authority names approve or refuse it before anything is bought. | planning-analyst, forecaster*, delegation-keeper, decision-coordinator* |

|---|---|---|

### Billing and collecting

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Set a Customer's Credit Limit | Before a customer buys on credit, or when a customer needs more, its payment history, its accounts and outside credit reports are read against the credit policy, a limit and terms are proposed with the evidence behind them, and a named person sets them. | credit-analyst, researcher*, delegation-keeper |
| Issue a Customer Invoice | Each invoice is built from the booking, the billing terms of the agreement and, where the customer pays for what it uses, the metered usage, checked for what `<your jurisdiction's invoice rules>` require, and sent on its date in the form the customer asked for. | billing-administrator, order-check*, usage-monitor* |

| Resolve a Billing Dispute | A customer's claim that an invoice is wrong is logged with the date it will be answered, the invoice is checked against the agreement, the booking and the usage, a named person decides, and any credit note or replacement invoice is issued once the people the delegation of authority names approve it. | dispute-coordinator*, billing-administrator, collections-coordinator |
| Write Off a Bad Debt | A debt that collection has not recovered is set out with everything tried and the amount still owed, the people the delegation of authority names decide whether to write it off, and the write-off is posted with its approval while the account stays open for any later recovery. | receivables-keeper, collections-coordinator, decision-coordinator* |

### Cash and banking

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Report the Daily Cash Position | Each morning the balance in every bank account and the payments and receipts expected that day are gathered into one position, and anything unexpected or below `<your minimum cash>` goes to the treasurer the same morning. | treasury-analyst, receivables-keeper, payables-processor |
| Forecast Cash | Each week the cash the company will hold over the coming weeks and months is forecast from what customers owe, what the company owes, payroll, tax and debt, set beside the last forecast and what actually happened, and any shortfall is raised early with options for the people who decide. | treasury-analyst, planning-analyst, receivables-keeper, payables-processor |

| Fund the Payroll | Once Human resources has an approved pay register, its total and its payday are confirmed, the payroll account is funded in time with a transfer that named people approve and release, and the funding is matched to the payments that leave. | treasury-analyst, payroll-administrator*, wire-check* |
| Change a Bank Account or Signer | Opening or closing a bank account, or adding or removing a person who may sign or release payments, is approved by the people the delegation of authority names, signed on the bank's own forms, confirmed with the bank and recorded, and a leaver's authority ends on the last day. | treasury-analyst, delegation-keeper, signature-manager* |

### Closing the books

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Post a Manual Journal Entry | A journal that does not come from a system feed is prepared with its support and checked for its accounts, its period and its balance, a named person who did not prepare it approves it, with an entry above `<your threshold>` approved by the people the delegation of authority names, and it is posted. | ledger-accountant, delegation-keeper |
| Book the Period-End Accruals | At each period end the costs incurred and not yet invoiced, the commissions earned and not yet paid, the pay owed for days already worked and the prepaid costs to spread are worked out from the orders, the receipts and the records, a named person reviews them, and each is posted and reversed when it should be. | ledger-accountant, purchasing-coordinator, commission-calculator* |
| Reconcile a Balance Sheet Account | Each account on the reconciliation list, including the bank, payroll and intercompany accounts, is reconciled at period end to its statement, its sub-ledger or its schedule, every difference is explained and cleared, and a named reviewer who did not prepare the reconciliation signs it off. | account-reconciler, ledger-accountant, payroll-administrator* |
| Recognize Revenue | Each new or changed customer agreement is read for what was promised, at what price and when it is delivered under `<your accounting framework>`, a named person decides each judgment the agreement raises, the revenue for the period is worked out from the billing and the delivery, and the deferred and unbilled revenue is reconciled. | revenue-accountant, order-check*, billing-administrator |
| Account for Fixed Assets | Each purchase above `<your capitalization threshold>` is recorded as an asset with its useful life and its owner, depreciation posts each period, each disposal or impairment goes to a named person, and the register is checked against what physically exists. | asset-register-keeper, purchasing-coordinator |
| Account for a Lease | Each lease signed or changed is read for its term, its payments and its options and entered in the lease register under `<your accounting framework>`, a named person decides each judgment, such as whether an option will be used, and the entries post each period. | asset-register-keeper, ledger-accountant |
| Close the Month | Each month the close calendar gives every task an owner and a day, the sub-ledgers are closed, the journals, accruals and reconciliations are done and reviewed, the results are checked for movements nobody has explained, and the controller signs off the close. | close-plan-keeper*, ledger-accountant, account-reconciler, reporting-accountant |
| Consolidate the Group Accounts | Each entity's ledger is brought into the group in one currency at dated rates, the balances between entities are matched, settled and eliminated, and a named person reviews the consolidated trial balance. | reporting-accountant, account-reconciler |
| Prepare the Financial Statements | Each quarter and year the statements and the notes are drafted from the consolidated trial balance under `<your accounting framework>`, checked against the disclosures that framework requires and against the last period, and signed off by the named officers before any of it is reported outside the company. | reporting-accountant, disclosure-check* |

## 6. Widened agents

Ten of the twenty-six reused agents needed their purpose or their Not text changed, the ten decision 11 names. The last of them, `supplier-check`, was found while the agents were written: its purpose listed only investors and target companies among the parties it screens. Each change is the smallest one that removes the contradiction with the Finance work, and each agent keeps its meaning for the departments it already served. The other sixteen reused agents took their Finance processes and activities with no change to either text. Each entry quotes the wording before and after.
