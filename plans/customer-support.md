# Customer support

A first pass at the catalog's department for a company's own customer support function, at a company of a few hundred people or more that supports its own customers, whether they are businesses or consumers. It follows each request from the moment a customer asks for help on any channel, through the answer, the escalation and the fix, to what the company learns from it, and it covers outages, the help center, account and billing requests, the quality of the work and the running of the support desk itself. It has 8 value streams, 40 processes and 43 agents (16 new, 27 reused). Activity tables come later.

## 1. Decisions

I made these calls so the work could start. Jeff can override any of them, and the documents that follow are written to them.

1. **Scope.** The department models a company's own support function at a company of a few hundred people or more, supporting its own customers, whether businesses or consumers, by email, chat, phone, a web form and inside the product. It does not model an outsourced support provider that serves other companies, though an outside provider may hold the support specialist's role or the after-hours cover for the company in a roster, and the roster says which. The documents call the people who answer customers support specialists, so the word agent always means an agent in the catalog's sense.
2. **Sales keeps the account.** Renewals, expansion, working an account at risk of leaving (`ref/sls/work-a-churn-risk`) and the handover after the sale (`ref/sls/hand-over-to-delivery`) stay in Sales. Support spots the signs in its cases, such as a customer who says it is looking elsewhere, a champion who has left or a team asking for what a larger plan includes, and Flag an Account Signal passes each one with its evidence to the account owner through the `risk-watcher` agent at `ref/sls/work-a-churn-risk` or the `expansion-spotter` agent at `ref/sls/spot-an-expansion`. Support never runs the save, prices anything or sells, and its part in a save plan is the fixes the plan names for the support lead. A customer who asks to cancel is told how, the request goes to the account owner the same day in the customer's own words, and support never delays or conditions a cancellation to make room for a save, following `<your jurisdiction's rules on cancelling a subscription>` where they apply.
3. **Marketing keeps the customer programs and the public voice.** Customer marketing, references, case studies, reviews, the newsletter, the onboarding email program and the community the company runs stay in Marketing's Customer marketing and references stream. When the `community-manager` agent sends on a support question from the community or a social inbox, it arrives at Take In a Request like any other request. A question from a reporter goes to Marketing's press work, and an outage or a security event that will become news goes to `ref/mkt/crisis-communication` for what the company tells the press. The status updates support posts say the same thing as that statement, and the same named person approves both.
4. **Finance keeps the money.** Invoices, billing disputes (`ref/fin/resolve-a-billing-dispute`), credit notes, refunds as money and write-offs stay in Finance. Support answers a question about a bill from `<your billing system>`, which it can read and never change, and a customer's claim that an invoice is wrong goes to Finance's dispute process through the `dispute-coordinator` agent. A refund or a goodwill credit is a support request: a named person in support decides it within `<your refund limit>`, the person the delegation of authority names decides above it, and Finance then issues the credit note through the `billing-administrator` agent and pays any refund in `ref/fin/pay-approved-invoices`. That process lists where approved refunds come from, and when the support processes are written the list gains Decide a Refund Request, the one change this department needs in a Finance document.
5. **Human resources keeps employment.** Hiring support staff, their performance reviews, their pay, discipline and the working time policy stay in Human resources. Support's own case reviews, the calibration of its reviewers and coaching on how cases are handled are in scope, and so is the schedule, which is built inside HR's working time policy and `<your jurisdiction's working time rules>`. Agents score cases. No agent scores, ranks or rates a support specialist, a specialist's case scores go only to that specialist and their team lead, and nothing from a case review reaches a performance review unless the team lead, as a person, brings it there. The `staffing-planner` agent reports coverage against the service levels by interval, and no agent reports a named person's time for discipline.
6. **Revenue operations keeps the customer record and the revenue tools.** Clean customer data, the definition of an account and the choice of revenue tools stay in Revenue operations. Support owns the day-to-day setup of its own desk: its routing rules, its saved replies and its service level policies. It reuses the `routing-keeper` and `response-clock-monitor` agents for that work, and a case that shows a wrong field in `<your CRM>` is reported to the owner of that field through Revenue operations' own processes.
7. **Legal, Product and Engineering have no department yet.** Anything legal, such as a legal threat, a regulator's letter or a request from a court or the police, goes to `<your legal team>` (person) the same day, and the reply waits for them. A confirmed bug goes to `<your engineering team>` and a feature request to `<your product team>` (people) through `<your issue tracker>`, and support follows each one up and tells the customers linked to it. A security or safety report goes to `<your security team>` (person). Engineering runs an outage itself, and support's part is the customer side: noticing it, saying so in public once a named person approves the words, telling the customers it affects and following up. When those departments are written, these processes hand over to theirs.
8. **Written for any country.** Laws on privacy, consumer rights and record keeping differ by country, so no document states one country's rule as the rule. Where a local rule decides a step, such as how fast a data request must be answered, how a formal complaint must be handled, what a customer must be told about talking to an agent, when a call may be recorded, how long a case is kept or how a cancellation must work, the document leaves a blank such as `<your jurisdiction's deadline for answering a data request>` or `<your jurisdiction's rule on keeping customer records>`, and the adopting company's counsel fills it in. A company that serves customers in several countries fills each blank once for each country.
9. **What an agent may say to a customer.** An agent may answer a customer directly on a routine question, but only from approved content in `<your knowledge base>` and the customer's own records, and every answer says that it comes from an agent and offers a person. A named person reviews and sends any reply that admits fault, promises a fix or a date, offers money (a refund, a credit or a goodwill gesture) or any change to what the contract says, answers a formal complaint, a legal threat, a security or safety report, a regulator or the press, or answers a request about a person's own data. The new `reply-check` agent checks every reply drafted on a case for these and holds each one it finds for Approve a Sensitive Reply. A routine answer an agent sends itself is checked by that agent against `<your reply rules>` and the content it cites, and any question in one of these categories goes to a person before anything is drafted. An agent that finds no approved answer says so and hands the case to a person, and it never writes an answer from general knowledge.
10. **People decide.** A named person decides every refund and credit, within `<your refund limit>`, and above it the person the delegation of authority names. A named person closes a customer's account, hands control of an account to someone after the identity checks, approves what the company says in public about an outage, and decides anything that touches a support person's employment. The phases that hold these decisions carry `automation: never`. Agents take in, sort, look up, answer from approved content, draft, check, schedule, record and remind.
11. **Duties stay separate.** Whoever proposes a refund or a credit never approves it, and no agent moves money or changes a bill itself: an agent proposes, a named person decides, and Finance books. A request to recover access or to change the email or phone on an account that arrives with urgency or pressure is treated as a possible takeover until the identity checks pass, and the contact details the request itself gives are never used to confirm it, which follows the rule Finance applies to a supplier's bank details.
12. **Customer success stays out for now.** Onboarding a customer onto the product, health scores, success plans and business reviews belong in a department of their own, left for a later pass. At a company of this size customer success usually works beside sales with targets for renewal and growth, and today that work is spread across Sales (renewals, expansion, the churn risk and the handover), Marketing (the onboarding email program) and the `account-monitor`, `usage-monitor` and `risk-watcher` agents, which already read an account's health. Putting it here would give support a commercial target, which pulls against answering every customer by the same rules. Support feeds it through Flag an Account Signal and through the support history the `account-monitor` agent reads.
13. **One delegation and one set of support policies.** Refund and credit limits are read from the company's delegation of authority, which the `delegation-keeper` agent holds, and support's own policies, such as the refund policy, the identity policy, the reply rules and the service level policy, are held at versions by the `policy-keeper` agent. Each process shows where a number goes, and the people the delegation names set it.
14. **Reused agents.** Twenty-seven existing agents take work here. Fifteen of them need their purpose or Not line widened in place (`analytics`, `accessibility-check`, `billing-administrator`, `consent-manager`, `curriculum-manager`, `delegation-keeper`, `editor`, `forecaster`, `in-product-producer`, `meeting-scheduler`, `onboarding-manager`, `policy-keeper`, `routing-keeper`, `search-optimizer` and `statistician`), following the earlier departments' decision to widen in place and make no copies. The other twelve fit as they stand. Section 5 says what each change is, and the wording before and after is quoted when the agents are written.
15. **Stream names.** Following Jeff's rule, each stream's title is a friendly descriptor in a buyer's words, its slug is the kebab form of that title, and its document has no friendly name line.
16. **Left for later.** Each of these is left for a later pass, for the reason given.
   - Customer success: see decision 12.
   - Field service: sending a technician to a site needs dispatch, parts and travel planning that operations usually owns.
   - Returns and repairs of physical goods: they need a warehouse, carriers and warranty rules that differ by country, and only companies that ship goods need them.
   - Community forums: Marketing already runs the community at `ref/mkt/moderate-community`, and support answers what the `community-manager` agent sends on.
   - Support sold as a paid service, such as a premium plan or a named technical contact: Sales sells it, its contract sets its own service levels, and these processes read those levels from the account's entitlement.
   - Support in several languages and the translation of the help center: each language needs its own review before anything is published, and the rules on which languages a company must offer differ by country.
   - Choosing or replacing the support system: it happens once every few years, and a company can run it through `ref/rev/choose-a-tool` with the head of support as its owner until a later pass covers it.
   - Managing an outsourced support provider: buying the service runs through Finance's purchasing, and the provider's people hold roles in the roster like anyone else.

## 2. Department name

**Recommended: Customer support.** It is the name most companies of this size use for the team that answers customers, it reads plainly beside Sales, Marketing and Finance, and it covers every channel and the help center without naming any one of them.

- **Customer service.** Consumer businesses often use this name, but buyers also read it as including order taking, delivery and returns, which this department leaves out.
- **Customer experience.** Some companies give the support team this name, but it usually means the whole of a customer's dealings with the company, including marketing and the product, and it reads as a program more than a department.

I set aside "Help desk" because it names a kind of tool, and many buyers read it as the internal desk that helps employees with their equipment.

Proposed ids, following the catalog's pattern: folders `streams/customer-support/` and `processes/customer-support/`, ids `vs/sup/<slug>` and `ref/sup/<slug>`, and a row in `departments.md` that reads `customer-support | Customer support | Department`.

## 3. Value streams

| 8 | Running the support desk | `running-the-support-desk` | The desk has enough people on every channel and in every interval for the service levels it promised, the routing rules and saved replies are current and readable at their versions, every service level is set by named people, and each period the head of support sees every queue against its promise. | support operations lead, head of support, team leads, support specialists |

Some decisions stay with people in every process: every reply that admits fault, promises a fix or a date, offers money or a change to the contract, or answers a formal complaint, a legal threat, a security or safety report, a regulator, the press or a request about a person's own data; every refund and credit; closing an account; handing control of an account to someone; what the company says in public about an outage; and anything that touches a support person's employment.

**Later.** Customer success, field service, returns and repairs of physical goods, community forums, support sold as a paid service, support in several languages, choosing the support system and managing an outsourced provider are left for a later pass, for the reasons in decision 16.

## 4. Processes (40)

A slug marked * is an existing catalog agent. Every other slug is a new agent from section 5. Where a process holds a reply or a decision that stays with a person, its description names who, and the phase that holds it carries `automation: never`. Every limit and approver named below is read from the delegation of authority or the support policy at its version, so `<your refund limit>` in a description is a number the adopting company sets there.

### Answering customers

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Take In a Request | Each request that arrives by email, chat, phone, the web form or inside the product, or that the community-manager agent sends on, is matched to the customer and the account, joined to any case already open on the same matter, sorted by urgency and topic under `<your routing rules>`, given the service level the customer's plan carries, and placed in the right queue with what is already known about the account. | request-sorter, account-monitor* |
| Verify a Customer's Identity | Before anyone reads out, changes or acts on anything in an account, the person asking is checked under `<your identity policy>` against what `<your identity system>` and the account already hold, with the checks the action's risk calls for, and a request that fails the checks or pushes to skip them is held and goes to a named person as a possible takeover. | identity-check, case-manager |
| Answer a Routine Question | A question that approved content in `<your knowledge base>` answers is answered directly by an agent from that content and the customer's own records only, inside the service level, in a reply that says it comes from an agent and offers a person, and a question with no approved answer, or in a category a person must answer, goes to a support specialist with the case as it stands. | customer-question-responder, knowledge-keeper, case-manager |
| Resolve a Case | A case that needs a person is held by one named support specialist from assignment to close: the facts are gathered from the customer's records, each reply is drafted and checked before it goes, every promise made to the customer is kept on the case with its date, and the case closes once the customer has the answer or the fix and reopens if the customer says it did not work. | case-manager, customer-question-responder, reply-check |
| Approve a Sensitive Reply | A drafted reply that admits fault, promises a fix or a date, offers money or a change to the contract, answers a formal complaint, a legal threat, a security or safety report, a regulator or the press, or answers a request about a person's own data is held until the named person for its category has read it with the case beside it, and that person sends it, changes it or passes it to `<your legal team>`. | reply-check, case-manager |
| Cover After-Hours Requests | Outside the team's hours the agents answer what approved content covers, tell every other customer when a person will pick the case up, and page the named person on call under `<your after-hours rota>` for anything urgent, such as an account takeover, an outage or a safety report, and the morning team receives every case held overnight in order of urgency. | request-sorter, customer-question-responder, staffing-planner |

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Escalate a Case to a Specialist | A case the first support specialist cannot resolve is handed to a named specialist in `<your escalation tiers>` with its full history, what was tried and what the customer was told, the customer is told who now holds it and when they will hear, and a call with the customer is booked when the specialist needs one. | escalation-coordinator, case-manager, meeting-scheduler* |
| Report a Confirmed Bug | Once a support specialist has reproduced a fault, it is checked against the bugs already filed, filed in `<your issue tracker>` with the steps, the evidence and every account affected or joined to the bug already there, followed up with `<your engineering team>` on the cadence its severity sets, and each linked customer is told what is known, with a date only when a named person has approved one. | escalation-coordinator, account-monitor*, case-manager |
| Log a Feature Request | A customer's request for something the product does not do is written in the customer's own words, matched to the requests already logged, counted with the accounts that asked and what they pay, sent to `<your product team>` through `<your issue tracker>`, and the customer is told how requests are handled, with no promise that it will be built. | feedback-keeper, account-monitor* |
| Tell Customers about a Fix | When `<your issue tracker>` shows a bug fixed or a requested feature released, every customer linked to it is told in the approved wording, each case still open on it is checked and closed or kept open, and a customer the fix did not help comes back as a case of its own. | feedback-keeper, escalation-coordinator, customer-question-responder |
| Escalate a Security or Safety Report | A customer's report of a security weakness, a suspected exposure of their data or a product that may cause harm goes to `<your security team>` or the named safety owner within the hour with the report in the customer's own words, nothing about it is discussed in a public channel, and every reply to the person who reported it is approved and sent by a named person. | escalation-coordinator, reply-check |

### Outages and incidents

| Process | What it does | Agents |
|---|---|---|
| Raise a Suspected Outage | When cases on one symptom arrive faster than `<your threshold>`, or customers start saying in public that something is broken, the cases are grouped, the symptom, the time it was first seen and the accounts affected are set out, and the engineer on call in `<your engineering team>` is told at once and confirms or rules out an incident. | outage-coordinator, request-sorter, coverage-monitor* |
| Post a Status Update | While an incident is open, each update on `<your status page>`, and any notice inside the product, is drafted from what the incident lead has confirmed, approved by the named person before it is posted, posted on the cadence `<your incident policy>` sets even when nothing has changed, and kept in step with any statement Marketing issues. | outage-coordinator, in-product-producer* |

| Follow Up an Outage | Once the incident closes, the summary of what happened and what changes is written from engineering's review and approved by a named person before any customer reads it, it is sent to the affected customers, every linked case is closed or kept open with its reason, the credits an agreement owes for the outage are proposed for a named person to decide, and what support learned is recorded. | outage-coordinator, refund-coordinator, reply-check, case-manager |

## 6. Widened agents

Fifteen of the twenty-seven reused agents needed their purpose or their Not text changed: the fourteen decision 14 names, and `consent-manager`, which was found while the agents were written, because its Not text said it deletes no record while Answer a Data Request has it carry a granted deletion to every copy of a person's contact details. Each change is the smallest one that removes the contradiction with the Customer support work, and each agent keeps its meaning for the departments it already served. The other twelve reused agents took their Customer support processes and activities with no change to either text. Each entry quotes the wording before and after.

1. **Accessibility check (`accessibility-check`), Not.** It named only the makers of marketing material as the ones who fix a failure.
   - Before: "... the web-producer changes the page, and this agent scores the result again."
   - After: "... the web-producer changes the page, the article-writer agent changes a help article, and this agent scores the result again."
2. **Analytics (`analytics`), purpose.** Its examples named only marketing and workforce readings.
   - Before: "... audits the scoring model behind lead routing, and reads the headcount, hiring, leaving and labor cost behind a workforce plan and report."
   - After: "... audits the scoring model behind lead routing, reads the headcount, hiring, leaving and labor cost behind a workforce plan and report, and reads the cases, the complaints and the help center's searches and reads behind the support desk's reports and its forecast of requests."
3. **Billing administrator (`billing-administrator`), purpose.** It drafted a credit note only for a disputed invoice.
   - Added after the sentence on a disputed invoice: "For a refund or a goodwill credit a named person in support decided, it drafts the credit note for exactly what was decided and issues it once that person has approved it."
4. **Billing administrator (`billing-administrator`), Not.** It named only the billing lead and the credit note approver as the people who decide.
   - Before: "Does not approve an invoice or a credit note, decide a dispute or decide how a rejected invoice is put right, which are the billing lead and the credit note approver (people)."
   - After: "Does not approve an invoice or a credit note, decide a dispute, a refund or a credit, or decide how a rejected invoice is put right, which are the billing lead, the credit note approver and the named person in support who decided the refund or the credit (people)."
5. **Consent manager (`consent-manager`), Not.** It said the agent deletes no record, and a person's data request now has it carry a granted deletion to every copy.
   - Before: "Does not standardize fields, merge records, enrich them, delete them or rebuild segments, which is the audience-operator agent."
   - After: "Does not standardize fields, merge records, enrich them, delete them or rebuild segments, which is the audience-operator agent, though for a person's data request it carries the deletion the privacy lead granted to the owner of each system that keeps a copy of their contact details and confirms each one."

Each reused agent also got the access its Customer support activities need and the people it escalates to in Customer support. No grant gives an agent the right to approve a refund or a credit, to move money or to change a bill, and none lets an agent switch a routing rule or a clock on in live without the approval its process names.
