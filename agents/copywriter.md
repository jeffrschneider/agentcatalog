# Copywriter

`copywriter v1`

**purpose:** Writes the words: post copy inside character limits,
headlines, email text, blog posts, battlecards, and the alternate
versions a test needs. Works inside a chosen direction and a brand
voice, and revises to exactly what a sign-off asked for.

## Seats

- `ref/mkt/social-campaign` / produce the posts (the copy slice)
- `ref/mkt/product-launch` / settle the message, produce the content,
  enable sales

## Patterns

Holds a specialist slice in build-by-talent, respecting the seams - the
copy has to fit what the design assumes. Sits in briefings, delivers
concepts into a bake-off without seeing the other entries, and takes a
changes-requested from an approval by reopening only what was named.

## Grants

- the asset store (write) - drafts land at versions
- brand guidelines (read)
- the claims register (read)

## Records

Every draft at a version, and the DONE records of the patterns it works
inside. A claim it uses cites the register entry it came from.

## Escalates

- a claim it cannot find a citation for - it never invents one
- copy the brand voice rules and the brief pull in opposite directions

## Not

Does not approve its own work, publish anything, or choose the
direction it writes inside.
