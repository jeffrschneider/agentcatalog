# cap-table-keeper

## rebuild-the-capitalization

Rebuilds every share, option, warrant and convertible from the
company's own records, checks each issue was approved, paid for and
recorded, and reads the rights and incentive plans behind every share.
It hands the rebuilt table and its gaps on as findings.

covers:
- Check the Share History
- Hand the Findings On
- Read Employment and Incentive Plans
- Read the Shareholder Rights
- Rebuild the Capitalization

## model-what-each-class-takes

Models the fund's fully diluted ownership and runs a value through the
debt, the preferences, conversion and participation to each share
class, whether that value is an exit case, a quarter's mark, a buyer's
final bid or what is left of a failed company.

covers:
- Allocate Value across the Share Classes
- Compare the Final Bids
- Confirm Nothing Is Left for the Fund
- Hand the Model On
- Model the Ownership
- Model the Preferences

## hold-the-funds-position

Keeps the fund's position in the cap table record from the closing
date to the close at zero: the shares, class, price and rights it
bought, its position at each measurement date, its listed shares after
a listing, and what it still holds or owes when a company fails.

covers:
- Book the Investment
- Close the Fund's Position
- Confirm the Capitalization at Closing
- List What the Fund Still Holds or Owes
- Make the Shares Ready to Trade
- Read the Fund's Position in Each Company
- Record the Close in the Cap Table Record
- Record the Fund's Position
- Take in the Listed Position

## settle-money-against-the-cap-table

Sets the cap table against the money: the funds flow lines at a
closing, the payout of a sale holder by holder, and each trade of a
sell-down matched to its instruction and confirmation. It then hands
the proceeds or the shares on and records the result against the plan.

covers:
- Build the Funds Flow
- Check the Proceeds against the Cap Table
- Hand Over the Proceeds or the Shares
- Record the Result
- Record the Result against the Plan
- Settle and Reconcile Each Sale
