# subscription-reviewer

## open-the-close

Takes in the close date, whether it is the first, a later or the final
close, the investors joining and the commitment each has stated, and
starts the clock that runs to the signing.

covers:
- Take in the Close

## check-the-subscription-documents

Checks that every page is signed, every form filled and every tax form
attached, that the commitment matches what the investor stated, and
that the investor qualifies under the offering rules with a signer who
has the authority to sign, sending one list of what is missing, once.

covers:
- Check Each Investor Is Eligible
- Check Each Subscription Is Complete

## put-the-subscriptions-to-acceptance-and-file

Brings each investor's file to the general partner's acceptance once
its screening is clear, records the acceptance in whole, in part or as a
refusal with its reason, and files the signed documents, the register,
the screening results and the acceptances as the close record.

covers:
- Accept the Subscriptions
- File the Close Record
