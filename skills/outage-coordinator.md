# outage-coordinator

## raise-a-suspected-outage

Opens one suspected outage for a symptom, reads whether an incident is
already open and what known cause may match, groups the cases, sets out
the symptom in the customers' words, lists the accounts reporting it,
pages the engineer on call with the evidence, and hands on or sends back
the cases once the engineer rules.

covers:
- Check for a Known Cause
- Check for an Incident Already Open
- Group the Cases
- Hand On a Confirmed Incident
- List the Accounts Reporting It
- Open the Suspected Outage
- Page the Engineer on Call
- Send Back a Ruled-Out Alarm
- Set Out the Symptom

## draft-from-confirmed-facts

Opens the status record, takes in what the incident lead confirmed and
engineering's review, and drafts each update, correction, resolved
update and summary at a version from those facts alone, set beside
Marketing's statement once the press has it.

covers:
- Draft a Correction
- Draft the Resolved Update
- Draft the Summary for Customers
- Draft the Update
- Open the Status Record
- Set the Draft Beside Marketing's Statement
- Take In Engineering's Review
- Take In What the Incident Lead Confirmed

## post-on-the-cadence

Posts exactly the approved version on the status page with the time of
the next update, keeps the cadence even when nothing has changed, and
passes the approved wording to the support team as the only words anyone
in support uses.

covers:
- Keep the Cadence
- Pass the Wording to the Support Team
- Post the Resolved Update
- Post the Update

## tell-the-affected-accounts

Opens the customer side of the incident, adds the accounts behind the
linked cases, sends each account owner their affected accounts, sends
each approved version to every open linked case and the approved summary
to every affected account, joins each new case to the incident, and
confirms that every account was told by the route it was owed.

covers:
- Add the Accounts Behind Linked Cases
- Confirm Who Has Been Told
- Join Each New Case to the Incident
- Open the Customer Side of the Incident
- Send Each Later Update
- Send the Account Owners Their Accounts
- Send the Resolved Update
- Send the Summary
- Send the Update to Each Open Case

## close-out-the-incident

Hands the accounts, the cases, the notices and the wording sent to the
follow-up, opens the follow-up from that record, and closes or keeps
open each linked case with its reason and its owner.

covers:
- Close or Keep Open Each Linked Case
- Hand Over to the Follow-Up
- Open the Follow-Up

## record-what-the-incident-taught

Records the time from the first case to the page, the first update and
the summary, the cadences missed, the corrections, the notices sent late
and whether the threshold was right.

covers:
- Record What Support Learned
- Record What the Run Taught
