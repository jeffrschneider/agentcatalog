# candidate-sourcer

## search-for-people

Takes in the signed hiring plan, asks the teams it names for referrals,
takes in each referral with who made it and how they know the person,
and searches the past applicants on file and the outside sources the
sourcing policy allows for the must-haves alone.

covers:
- Ask Employees for Referrals
- Search the Allowed Sources
- Search the Past Applicants
- Take in Each Referral
- Take in the Hiring Plan

## read-and-record-each-person

Reads each person found against the written must-haves only, records
each one once with the source that found them and the date, and checks
each against the consent on file, the do-not-approach list, the
company's own employees and any agreement not to hire from another
company.

covers:
- Check Who May Be Approached
- Read Each Person Against the Must-Haves
- Record Each Person Once

## draft-the-approaches

Drafts a short approach for each person the recruiter chose, naming the
role, the must-haves it matches, where the person's details came from
and the employee who referred them, and one follow-up after a set time
with no approach after it.

covers:
- Draft Each Approach
- Follow Up Once

## close-the-search

Brings each person who said yes into the pipeline with their source,
records every answer with the date each person may be approached again,
removes the details of everyone else on time, and records which sources
found people who went forward.

covers:
- Bring the Interested People In
- Record What the Search Taught
- Record the Answers
- Remove the Data No Longer Needed
