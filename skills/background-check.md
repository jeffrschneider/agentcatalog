# background-check

## set-up-the-checks

Takes in the chosen candidate, confirms only the checks the screening
policy sets for the role in each location and the point the rules let
each one run, and gives the candidate a plain notice of each check with
a request for written consent.

covers:
- Ask for Written Consent
- Confirm When the Checks May Run
- Confirm the Checks the Role Requires
- Take in the Chosen Candidate

## run-the-checks

Orders each consented check from the provider with only the details it
needs, chases each one to a result by its date, and holds the results
apart where only the named people can open them, with every opening
logged.

covers:
- Hold the Results Apart
- Order the Checks
- Track the Checks

## give-the-candidate-a-say

Sends the candidate a copy of what a check found and the time the rules
allow to respond, takes the candidate's explanation or sends an error
back to the provider, and tells the candidate the named person's
decision in writing with any notice the rules require.

covers:
- Send the Candidate the Finding
- Take the Candidate's Response
- Tell the Candidate the Decision

## close-the-checks

Hands the offer only the outcome, clear or decided, destroys the results
and references when the retention period ends with a record that they
were, and records how long each check took and what the disputes found.

covers:
- Destroy the Results on Time
- Hand the Outcome to the Offer
- Record What the Checks Taught
