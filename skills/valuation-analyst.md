# valuation-analyst

## gather-the-evidence

Opens each valuation period with its measurement date, its holdings and
the policy version, takes in the confirmed reports or the facts of a
failure, and gathers rounds, offers, sales and listed peers, each with
its source and its date.

covers:
- Gather the Market Evidence
- Open the Valuation Period
- Take in the Company Reports
- Take in the Failure

## propose-the-mark

Chooses the method the policy sets for each holding, calibrates it to
the last price, and proposes the company's value with every input and
judgment written down. It runs that value through the share classes
with the cap-table-keeper agent, and proposes a mark at zero when
nothing is left.

covers:
- Allocate Value across the Share Classes
- Calibrate to the Last Price
- Choose the Method for Each Holding
- Propose the Company's Value
- Propose the Mark at Zero

## write-up-and-defend-the-mark

Writes one file per holding and puts it in front of each reviewer in
turn: the outside adviser, the chief financial officer (CFO), the
valuation committee and, at year end, the auditor. A mark that moves
comes back as a new version for the committee to approve.

covers:
- Approve Any Change to a Mark
- Get an Outside View of Named Holdings
- Present the Marks to the Committee
- Review the Marks as CFO
- Support the Valuations under Test
- Write Up Each Mark

## carry-the-approved-mark-on

Hands each approved mark or write-off to the controller who posts it,
to the auditor's file, and to the quarterly report with the plain
reason it moved, and records where marks moved most against the prices
later realized.

covers:
- File the Work for the Auditor
- Hand the Marks to the Quarterly Report
- Hand the Write-Off to the Quarterly Report
- Post the Marks to the Fund's Books
- Post the Write-Off to the Fund's Books
- Record What the Quarter Taught

## close-out-a-write-off

Fixes the event that realizes a loss with the tax adviser, builds the
tax file with the event, its date, its evidence and the fund's cost,
and keeps the decision, the mark, the documents and the lessons
together on the holding's record.

covers:
- Build the Tax File
- File the Write-Off Record
- Fix the Date the Loss Is Realized
