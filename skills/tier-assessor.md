# tier-assessor

## open-a-tiering-round

Starts a round, reads the tiering rules at the version in force with
the date they were published, and checks that the same evidence is
present for every partner in scope. A gap is marked as a gap rather
than counted as a zero.

covers:
- Check the Evidence Is Complete
- Open the Tiering Round
- Read the Rules in Force

## apply-the-rules-and-list-the-exceptions

Lets the rules produce a tier for each partner from the evidence, and
writes down every proposal that differs from that result as an
exception with a reason, a person asking and a date it expires.

covers:
- Apply the Rules
- List Every Exception

## close-the-tiering-round

Puts the tier set and every exception to the named signers, then
records where the rules gave a result nobody could defend and what the
next version of the rules should say.

covers:
- Approve the Tiers and the Exceptions
- Record What the Rules Got Wrong

## score-a-partner-against-its-plan

Scores each partner against the plan it agreed to, writes the finding
as on plan, behind or at risk, and refers any result that would move a
tier into the tiering round, where the published rules decide it.

covers:
- Draft the Finding per Partner
- Refer the Tier Changes
- Score Each Partner Against the Plan

## take-down-what-a-tier-gave

Removes the entitlement a tier carried when a relationship ends, and
records what was taken down and when.

covers:
- Close Off the Access
