# curriculum-manager

## work-out-where-the-teaching-starts

Writes down what each role has to prove, as testable lines at a
version, and reads what the people already hold so teaching starts from
what they can be excused. The same work runs for somebody joining a
revenue team and for the people a partner names.

covers:
- Check What They Already Know
- Set What Each Role Must Prove

## plan-what-is-learned-and-when

Builds the plan of which courses each role takes, in what order and in
which week, and books the dates, the trainers and the rooms in diaries
on both sides.

covers:
- Book the Sessions
- Build the Curriculum
- Build the Learning Plan

## read-the-handover-before-anybody-is-taught-from-it

Reads material handed over from a launch, a price change or a process
change against the questions a buyer actually asks, and names the gaps
before anybody is taught the old answer.

covers:
- Check What Was Handed Over

## make-the-material-at-a-version

Builds the session, the exercise and the short version of a change, and
publishes the decks, the labs and the recordings at a version at one
address, so the session and the library never differ.

covers:
- Build the Teaching
- Publish the Course Materials

## teach-a-subject

Runs the session itself: what the product does and what it costs, who
else the buyer is looking at and why, the stages, the gates and the
systems of record, the pitch and the objections for the people who
sell, and the build and the common failures for the people who deliver.

covers:
- Run the Delivery Training
- Run the Sales Training
- Run the Sessions
- Teach the Market and the Competition
- Teach the Process
- Teach the Product

## run-a-practice

Puts a person through the thing itself before a customer sees it,
whether that is building the product once in a lab, saying the pitch
out loud and getting it back, playing a discovery conversation end to
end, or rehearsing the hard answers alongside a partner.

covers:
- Give the Hands-On Lab
- Practise the Pitch
- Rehearse Before the Customer Sees It
- Run the Discovery Exercise

## build-the-check-of-what-was-taught

Builds the questions, the exercises and the scenarios a sitting uses,
each item naming the part of the standard it tests, and the short check
that a change was understood. It builds these and never scores the
person taking them.

covers:
- Build the Assessment
- Check That It Landed

## teach-a-named-gap-again

Reads what a sitting found and says what passed, what did not and what
to do next, then runs the session again for that one named gap and
nothing else.

covers:
- Give the Feedback
- Retrain Whoever Failed
