# investor-relations-manager

## write-the-fundraising-materials

Reads the strategy and the fund documents, writes the deck and the case
studies from the one frozen set of figures, with the cases chosen by a
rule written down first, updates the offering memorandum with counsel,
briefs the team on what may and may not be said, releases the approved
set at its version, and keeps the records the rules require.

covers:
- Brief the Team on What May Be Said
- Keep the Records the Rules Require
- Release the Materials
- Take in the Strategy and the Fund Documents
- Update the Offering Memorandum
- Write the Case Studies
- Write the Deck

## work-an-investor-to-an-answer

Takes in the fund and the investor, makes the first approach through
the path named, writes every contact into the customer relationship
management system the same day, opens
the data room under a signed confidentiality agreement, records the
commitment or the decline the partners' ask produced, and hands a
committed investor on with every promise made to it written down.

covers:
- Ask for the Commitment
- Hand On the Commitment
- Make the First Approach
- Open the Data Room to the Investor
- Record the Answer
- Record What the Investor Said
- Take in the Fund and the Target

## answer-an-investor-questionnaire

Takes in a due diligence questionnaire, holds every answer until the
investor's confidentiality agreement is signed, routes each question the
library cannot answer to the person who owns the fact, sends the
reviewed response in the investor's own format by its deadline, answers
the follow-ups through the same checks, and records what went to whom
at which version.

covers:
- Answer the Follow-Up Questions
- Check the Confidentiality Agreement
- Record the Response
- Review the Whole Response
- Route the Unanswered Questions
- Send the Response
- Take in the Questionnaire

## place-a-co-investment

Works out what is left to place and who is entitled to an offer, gets
confidentiality signed with each candidate, sends one offer and one set
of answers to every candidate alike, collects the amounts by the
deadline, allocates them in the policy's order with the mandate-check
agent, confirms each commitment, tells those cut back why, and hands the
co-investors to the closing.

covers:
- Agree the Offer List
- Allocate Under the Policy
- Answer the Candidates' Questions
- Collect the Amounts
- Confirm Each Co-Investor's Commitment
- Hand the Co-Investors to the Closing
- List Who Is Entitled to an Offer
- Record Each Allocation and Its Reason
- Send the Offer
- Sign Confidentiality with Each Candidate
- Take in the Deal and the Amount to Place
- Tell Those Cut Back or Left Out

## carry-a-request-to-the-advisory-committee

Sends the same consent request to every member of the advisory
committee of the fund's limited partners at the same time, answers each member's question to all of
them alike, and tells the deal team, the investors and anyone the
clause names what the committee decided.

covers:
- Answer the Members' Questions
- Send the Request to the Committee
- Tell Those Who Must Know
