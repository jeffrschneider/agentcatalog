# collections-coordinator

## remind-on-a-schedule

Sets each reminder's date, wording and contact from the follow-up level,
sends the reminders to the payables contact on the customer's record,
and brings in the account owner at the stage the schedule sets.

covers:
- Bring In the Account Owner
- Send the Reminders
- Set the Reminder Schedule

## keep-track-of-promises

Records each promise to pay with its amount, its date and who made it,
and checks it against the receipts on its date.

covers:
- Check Each Promise Was Kept
- Record Each Promise to Pay

## ask-about-a-payment

Asks the customer once what each held receipt or shortfall is for, and
reads the answer as the invoices it paid, a deduction claimed, a payment
meant for another customer or money sent in error.

covers:
- Ask About Each Held Receipt
- Read the Customer's Answer

## route-a-dispute

Sends each claim that an invoice is wrong, and each deduction a customer
claims, to the dispute process in the customer's own words, pauses the
reminders on the amount claimed, and restarts them on what is still owed
once the answer is given.

covers:
- Pause Collection on the Amount Claimed
- Restart Collection on What Is Owed
- Send a Claimed Deduction to Its Own Process
- Send a Dispute to Its Own Process

## bring-an-account-to-a-decision

Sets out an account that still does not pay with everything tried,
everything owed and the choices open, gathers the history for a
write-off, and follows the named people's decision through in the
reminders, the payment plan and the agency.

covers:
- Bring Collections in Line with the Decision
- Follow the Decision Through
- Gather Everything Tried
- Set Out the Account for a Decision

## record-what-collection-taught

Records which reminders brought payment, which promises were broken,
which warning signs came first, and what the follow-up level for the
customer should be.

covers:
- Record What the Debt Taught
- Record What the Run Taught
