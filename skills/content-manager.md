# content-manager

## take-in-the-request

Takes a request for a piece and settles what it has to do, who it is for, what it asks a reader to do, and the date it is due.

covers:
- Open the Issue
- Set the Parameters
- Take in the Request
- Take in the Topic

## settle-what-the-piece-carries

Settles what the piece argues, the order it argues it in, and which items it carries.

covers:
- Agree What the Story Argues
- Agree the Outline
- Settle the Lineup

## brief-every-agent-at-once

Gives every agent that has to research, write, design or check the work the same request at the same time, so that nobody is working from a different account of it.

covers:
- Brief the Agents that Write to It
- Brief the Department
- Brief the Makers
- Brief the Pages in the Cycle
- Brief the Piece
- Brief the Story

## hand-the-work-to-the-agents-that-act

Passes a settled thing to each agent that has to act on it and takes back what each one says it will change.

covers:
- Hand It to the Campaign
- Hand over the Approved Topics
- Hand over the Commitments
- Hand over the Map
- Hand over the Persona
- Hand the Strategy to the Work that Follows
- Tell Every Function What It Has

## collect-what-every-area-puts-forward

Sends one request in one format with one due date to every area or agent, and gathers what comes back into a single list.

covers:
- Ask Each Area What It Would Change
- Collect What Each Function Asks For
- Collect What the Plan Must Carry
- Collect the Candidate Initiatives
- Collect the Topic Candidates

## build-the-calendar

Lays the agreed work across the period with a format, an owning agent and a date on each item, and publishes the schedule where every agent reads it.

covers:
- Build the Calendar
- Draft the Calendar
- Publish the Calendar

## work-out-what-the-makers-can-carry

Works out how much work the makers can take on in the period. It breaks the demand into the runs it is actually made of, counts what is already committed, sizes what each run costs to make, says what is still free, and names what in flight has to stop or wait when something new takes its place.

covers:
- Break the Work into Activities
- List What Is Already Committed
- List the Committed Work
- Settle What Gets Displaced
- Size the Production Capacity
- Work out What Is Free

## handle-a-change-to-a-live-page

Takes a change against a page that is already live, works out whether it is a small edit or needs a bigger run and who has to sign it, makes the change the request or the numbers call for, and closes it when the requester agrees.

covers:
- Close out the Change
- Fix What the Numbers Show
- Route the Change

## publish-when-the-owner-says-go

Puts the work live or sends it out once the named owner says go, and records what went out and when.

covers:
- Publish
- Publish the Change
- Publish the Changes
- Send the Issue

## proof-it-before-it-goes-out

Reads test copies the way a reader will get them and finds what is wrong while it can still be fixed.

covers:
- Proof the Issue

## report-how-it-did

Sets the results against what the work promised and writes the account of it, then has the agents it names confirm the account is accurate before it goes out.

covers:
- Account for the Published Pieces
- Check the Account
- Report How the Piece Did
- Report the Results

## watch-what-happens-after-it-is-live

Reads what happens once the work is live, covering how many people arrive, what they do next, and how that compares with before, and keeps reporting it against the goal that was set.

covers:
- Watch It Convert
- Watch Performance
- Watch What the Changes Did
- Watch the Results

## inventory-what-is-already-published

Goes across what is already published and what readers keep asking, and pulls it into one list, so it is clear which piece answers which question and what has nothing behind it.

covers:
- Group the Recurring Questions
- Inventory the Content against the Map

## repurpose-published-material

Turns material that already exists into new pieces, clips, posts and pages.

covers:
- Repurpose the Content

## record-what-was-learned

Writes down what is worth repeating next time and what to avoid, so the next run starts from it.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
