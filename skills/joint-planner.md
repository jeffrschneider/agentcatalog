# joint-planner

## settle-what-each-side-signs-up-to

Settles the numbers each side works to and what each side puts in by way
of people, money and time, with the person on each side who confirmed
it.

covers:
- Set the Targets Each Side Works To
- Agree What Each Side Puts In

## pick-the-accounts-and-the-plays

Picks the accounts and segments both sides go after together, with the
reason written on each one and the conflict or the owner behind every
account removed, then names the plays run against them.

covers:
- Pick the Accounts and Segments
- Name the Plays

## price-our-side-of-the-plan

Prices what our share of the plan costs and holds that cost against the
budget line it is paid from.

covers:
- Price the Plan

## write-the-plan-at-a-version

Puts one named owner and one date on every milestone and writes the plan
at a version both sides read from the same place, with every blank
filled or marked open.

covers:
- Set the Milestones and the Dates
- Draft the Plan

## amend-a-plan-at-review

Names the gaps out loud at review with the person closest to each,
carries what the sponsors decided into an amendment, and marks the
changed lines against the version they replace.

covers:
- Name the Gaps Out Loud
- Decide What Changes
- Write the Amendment
