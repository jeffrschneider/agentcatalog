# creator-relations

## match-creators-to-an-audience

Searches the creator record for people whose following matches the audience a campaign wants to reach, and returns a candidate list with the audience figures that put each name on it.

covers:
- Draw up the Candidate List

## vet-a-creator

Checks a named creator before anybody is offered money. It reads the audience figures against the measurement sources to see whether the reach is real, reads what the creator has already published, and records what each check found and the date it was run.

covers:
- Vet the Candidates

## get-the-shortlist-cleared

Takes the creators who survived the checks to the people who have to accept who the organization pays, and records the answer against each name.

covers:
- Clear the Shortlist

## agree-a-deal

Makes the approach to a creator, finds out whether they are interested, and works through the terms until both sides hold the same deliverables, dates, usage rights, disclosure wording and fee. Every exchange lands in the contact log on the day it happened.

covers:
- Approach the Creators
- Settle the Terms

## brief-a-creator

Tells a creator what the campaign is for and what the work has to say, and hands over the material they are allowed to use.

covers:
- Brief the Creators

## chase-what-the-deal-owes

Tracks what a creator still owes against the dates in the deal and goes back to them for it, whether that is a deliverable that has not arrived or a fix to something already published. It records the date it asked, what it asked for, and what the creator did.

covers:
- Collect the Drafts
- Correct What Failed

## review-a-draft

Reads a creator's draft against the rules the deal set and says whether it can run as it is or what has to change first.

covers:
- Review the Drafts

## see-the-posts-go-live

Confirms each agreed post goes out on the date it was promised, and keeps a copy of what actually ran, where it ran and when.

covers:
- Publish the Posts
- Record the Live Posts
