# web-producer

## build-a-page

Assembles approved copy and approved design into a working page, using the templates and components the site already has, and wires up whatever the page needs to do its job, such as a form or the tracking behind it.

covers:
- Build the Page
- Build the Pages and the Notice
- Build the Partner Portal
- Build the Registration Page
- Open Registration
- Ready the Landing Pages

## change-a-published-page

Puts an approved change into a page that is already live and leaves it as a draft for the people who have to look at it. The change may be new wording, new visuals, a new figure, or a fix to the part of the page people are leaving from.

covers:
- Fix What the Numbers Show
- Make the Change in the Page
- Make the Changes in the Pages
- Restate the Price Everywhere It Is Published
- Update the Product Pages

## rebuild-the-templates

Remakes the templates the rest of the material is built from, so that everything produced after them comes out right.

covers:
- Rebuild the Templates

## repurpose-existing-material

Turns material that already exists into new pages and pieces, which includes cutting a recording, adding captions and building the page it sits on.

covers:
- Prepare the Recording for Reuse
- Repurpose the Content

## stage-changes-before-the-date

Builds and previews everything ahead of a date without any of it serving, so the people who have to look at it can, and so it all goes live together.

covers:
- Prep the Channels
- Stage the Changes

## set-the-addresses-and-the-redirects

Sets the address a page lives at and points merged and retired pages somewhere sensible.

covers:
- Set the Addresses and the Redirects

## take-it-live-on-the-owners-word

Waits for the named owner to say go, takes the staged work live on the date it was set for, and records what went live, at which version and who said go.

covers:
- Change the Public Price
- Issue the Release
- Issue the Response
- Launch the Joint Campaign
- Publish
- Publish the Change
- Publish the Changes
- Publish the Material
- Publish the Version
- Switch the Properties

## run-a-split-test

Builds each version of a page and sets up how the traffic divides between them, confirms every version serves and its numbers arrive, starts the split when the owner says go, and afterwards puts one version in front of everyone or takes the change out.

covers:
- Build the Variants
- Carry out the Decision
- Check the Setup
- Start the Test

## check-every-destination

Opens the page behind every live link and confirms that it loads and that the visit is recorded.

covers:
- Check Every Destination

## close-out-a-change

Gets the requester to agree that the change is done, and writes the record of what changed, when, and on whose word.

covers:
- Close out the Change

## brief-the-agents-that-make-it

Gives every agent that has to produce something the same account of what is wanted, at the same time.

covers:
- Brief the Agents that Remake the Material
- Brief the Makers
