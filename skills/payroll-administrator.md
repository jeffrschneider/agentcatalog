# payroll-administrator

## prepare-the-pay-period

Opens the pay period with its cutoff and payday, gathers every change
approved since the last run, collects the time and variable pay each
manager approved, and applies the benefit and other deductions in the
order the rules set, starting each new deduction in the first period the
plan allows.

covers:
- Apply the Deductions
- Collect Time and Variable Pay
- Gather the Approved Changes
- Open the Pay Period
- Start the Deductions

## work-out-pay

Works out each employee's pay from gross to net under the tax and
deduction rules for the place they work, and the amounts that run across
periods: back pay from a past effective date, a catch-up or refund, pay
during a leave and its return to normal, and a leaver's final pay.

covers:
- Restore Normal Pay
- Set the Leave Pay in Payroll
- Work Out Any Back Pay
- Work Out Any Catch-Up or Refund
- Work Out Gross to Net
- Work Out Pay During the Leave
- Work Out the Final Pay

## check-the-pay

Compares the register with the last period and traces every movement to
an approved change, confirms the first pay at a new rate and a leaver's
final pay against the record with the finance controller, and corrects
an error off cycle once a person releases or approves it.

covers:
- Check the Final Pay
- Compare the Register with the Last Period
- Confirm the First Pay at the New Rate
- Correct an Error Off Cycle

## pay-and-file

Builds the payment file from the approved register to each employee's
verified account, issues the payslips on payday, pays and files the
withholding by its date, posts the period's journal to the ledger, and
records what the run taught.

covers:
- Build the Payment File
- Issue the Payslips
- Pay and File the Withholding
- Post the Payroll to the Ledger
- Record What the Run Taught

## close-the-payroll-year

Takes in the last adjustments, sets up the new year's rates for the
payroll manager to confirm, totals each person's year, reconciles it to
the filings and the ledger, corrects every difference, and produces,
issues and answers questions on each year-end statement.

covers:
- Answer Questions on the Statements
- Check Each Person's Details
- Correct the Differences
- Issue the Statements
- Produce the Year-End Statements
- Reconcile the Year to the Filings
- Reconcile the Year to the Ledger
- Record What the Year Taught
- Set Up the New Year's Rates
- Take in the Last Adjustments
- Total Each Person's Year
