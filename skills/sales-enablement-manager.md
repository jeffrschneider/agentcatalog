# sales-enablement-manager

## take-in-what-the-material-must-answer

Records what sellers cannot answer today and in which deals, or what a competitor has done and where it was seen, and settles what the material has to answer and by when.

covers:
- Take in the Gap
- Take in the Move

## read-what-happens-in-deals

Goes to the sellers for what they are running into inside live deals, including what the competitor is saying in front of a customer and what the won and lost deals turned on.

covers:
- Hear What Reps Are Meeting
- Read What Happens Inside Deals

## find-the-material-a-change-touches

Searches the material already in reps' hands for the pieces and the claims that a change puts in doubt, and lists what has to be revisited.

covers:
- Find the Cards It Touches

## settle-the-obligations

Writes down what each side has to deliver, which covers what this agent owes, what sales owes back, and what a partner has to be able to demonstrate before it sells.

covers:
- List What the Partner Must Demonstrate
- Settle What Each Side Owes

## settle-the-objection-answers

Pairs each objection a seller meets with the answer it gets, and holds the proof that stands behind that answer.

covers:
- Settle the Answers

## brief-the-agents-that-make-it

Gives every agent that has to write, design or check the material the same request at the same time.

covers:
- Brief the Makers

## produce-the-selling-material

Assembles what a seller or a partner uses in front of a customer, working from copy and design that have already been approved and keeping each claim tied to the proof behind it.

covers:
- Build the Partner Selling Kit
- Make the Sales Material
- Produce What Customers and Sellers Read
- Produce the Partner Material
- Write the Card

## try-the-material-on-reps

Puts the finished material in the hands of a few reps to use in a real conversation, and records what held up and what did not.

covers:
- Try the Kit on Reps

## release-the-material-to-sales

Puts the signed version where a rep can find it while a deal is open, walks the people who will use it through what to say, and tells every one of them which version is current and from when.

covers:
- Brief Support and Sales
- Hand the Kit to Sales
- Hand the Story to Sales
- Issue the Version
- Notify Sales

## answer-the-questions-that-come-back

Answers what comes back after a session or a release, and files each answer with the material it belongs to.

covers:
- Answer the Partner's Questions

## report-how-the-material-did

Reads who opened the material, who used it and which deals it touched, and reports that against the gap it was made to close.

covers:
- Report against the Gap
- Watch What Sales Does with It

## record-what-was-learned

Writes down what is worth repeating next time and what to avoid, so the next run starts from it.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
