# study-manager

## take-in-what-the-study-must-settle

Reads the request that started the study and works out what it has to
settle and by when. That covers the question a piece of research must
answer, the period a review reads back over, and what a proposed change
is meant to do and to whom.

covers:
- Take in the Question
- Take in the Request
- Take in the Period
- Take in the Hypothesis

## set-what-the-study-covers

Writes down the boundary of the study before anything is asked, which is
the channels, markets and periods in scope, the things it will measure,
the other organizations asked about beside this one, and the earlier
runs this one gets compared to.

covers:
- Set What the Audit Covers
- Settle What the Wave Measures
- Set the Competitor Set
- Open the Cycle

## choose-the-method-and-draw-the-sample

Picks the method that will answer the question and says why that method
rather than another, then decides who or what gets sampled, how the
sample is drawn and how many responses it needs. Where a run reads back
over existing records, it pulls the population those records describe
first.

covers:
- Choose the Method
- Plan the Study
- Design the Buyer Study
- Define the Sample
- Draw Both Samples
- Size the Test
- Pull the Closed Deals

## write-the-questions

Writes every question that gets asked and every piece of material that
gets shown, and holds them at a version before anybody sees them. It
tries the wording on a handful of people first, and keeps a question
that has run before word for word so the two readings stay comparable.

covers:
- Write the Questions
- Write the Interview Guide
- Try the Questions on a Few People
- Carry the Questions Forward

## brief-everyone-on-the-study

Puts the question, the method, the scope and the dates in front of every
agent involved at the same time, so everyone works from one account of
what the study is for.

covers:
- Brief the Study
- Brief the Audit
- Brief the Wave
- Brief the Makers

## field-the-study

Puts the study to the sample, which means the invitations going out, the
survey opening and the interviews being arranged. Every response lands
as it was given, marked with the version of the questions it answered.

covers:
- Field the Study
- Field the Wave
- Invite the Buyers
- Ask the Audience

## watch-the-responses-and-close-the-field

Tracks how many responses have come in against how many the study needs,
half by half where the design has more than one half. When the sample is
complete or the date arrives, it stops the asking and fixes the
responses.

covers:
- Watch the Sample Fill
- Watch Both Halves Fill
- Close the Study
- Close the Wave
- Close the Interviews

## hold-the-interviews

Talks to buyers, customers and the people in the field, and takes down
what each of them said as they said it. It reports what these people
weigh when they choose and what they needed answered along the way.

covers:
- Hold the Interviews
- Talk to the Buyers
- Collect the First-Hand Accounts
- Hear from Buyers and the Field
- Collect the Questions

## sort-what-was-said-into-themes

Files every remark under a theme at a version and keeps the quotations
under each one, including the words people used for the problem and for
the answer.

covers:
- Sort What Was Said into Themes
- Record the Words This Person Uses

## read-the-results

Reads what the responses say once the field is closed, and sets that
reading beside the baseline and the earlier runs in the same series so
that a real movement can be told from noise. It also sets what people
believe against what the organization claims and against what its own
records say.

covers:
- Read the Result
- Compare with the Earlier Waves
- Set Perception against the Promise
- Set the Themes against the Record
- Read What People Recognize Today
- Read What People Recognize Now

## write-and-deliver-the-findings

Writes each finding with the responses recorded under it, so the finding
and its evidence are read in one place, and delivers the report to
whoever asked for it and to the people who have to act on it. It records
what worked in the run and what should change next time.

covers:
- Write the Findings
- Deliver the Findings
- Deliver the Themes and Recommendations
- Report Awareness, Perception and Preference
- Record What Was Learned

## run-a-controlled-test

Sets the terms of a test, which are the change, the audience it runs on
and the measure that decides it. It checks that nothing else is due on
the same visitors, starts the split once the owner says go, and watches
delivery and the guardrail measures while the test runs.

covers:
- Set the Terms of the Test
- Clear the Traffic
- Start the Test
- Watch the Test Run

## settle-and-carry-out-the-test-decision

Takes the result to the named person who says roll it out or put it
back, then carries that decision out so that everyone sees one version
or the change comes out.

covers:
- Decide Roll out or Revert
- Carry out the Decision

## audit-what-is-published

Lists every place an outsider meets the organization, pulls each of
those as it is actually served, and reads the set as a whole against the
standard in force. It names every place the material and the standard do
not agree.

covers:
- List Where the Brand Appears
- Draw the Sample
- Read the Set as a Whole
- Read the Promise the Brand Makes
- Name the Gaps

## dispose-of-the-gaps

Takes each gap the audit found to a decision about whether it gets fixed
now, fixed later or accepted in writing, and hands the ones being fixed
to the agent that can close them.

covers:
- Decide What Happens to Each Gap
- Hand the Gaps to the Owners

## describe-the-buyer

Writes down what a buyer is trying to get done, what gets in the way and
what that costs them, what starts them looking, and the steps they move
through. Each of those rests on what people said rather than on what the
organization assumes.

covers:
- Name the Jobs
- Name the Pains and What They Cost
- Name the Buying Triggers
- Draft the Stages

## try-material-on-outsiders

Puts a message or a candidate name in front of people outside the
company and reports what they took from it.

covers:
- Try the Messages on Buyers
- Try the Names on Outsiders
