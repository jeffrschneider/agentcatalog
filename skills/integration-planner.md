# integration-planner

## test-another-company-technology

Runs the other company's product against our own real cases and reads
what that product depends on, naming the licenses and the third parties
behind it, so a dependency that would block embedding or reselling is
found while the deal can still change.

covers:
- Read the Dependencies
- Test the Technology

## take-in-the-terms-and-set-the-scope

Reads the signed agreement for what it obliges technically and when
each obligation comes due, then sets what has to connect, how deep it
goes and what deliberately does not connect.

covers:
- Set the Technical Scope
- Take in the Signed Terms

## design-the-connection

Designs the interfaces, the data, the identity and the limits each side
agreed to, and plans the test systems and test accounts on both sides
and what data they hold.

covers:
- Design the Integration
- Plan the Environments and Data

## build-it-and-prove-it-on-a-customer

Sees the connection built and run end to end with what passed and what
did not written down, then proves the offer on one named customer with
both sides watching, before anybody announces it.

covers:
- Build and Test the Connection
- Run a Joint Pilot

## hold-the-readiness-gate

Works the readiness list item by item and puts the gate itself to a
person, with any waiver naming who waived it and the risk they
accepted, then hands the running alliance on to partner management.

covers:
- Check Readiness Before Launch
- Hand On to Partner Management
