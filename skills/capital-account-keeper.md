# capital-account-keeper

## keep-the-capital-accounts

Opens each investor's account at its commitment once the total is
checked against the hard cap, reads what each investor has left to
give, posts every call, charge and distribution to the accounts, and
builds each investor's statement from its own account.

covers:
- Build Each Investor's Statement
- Check the Total Against the Hard Cap
- Open Each Capital Account
- Post the Call to the Capital Accounts
- Post the Charges to the Capital Accounts
- Post the Distribution
- Read What Each Investor Has Left

## allocate-by-the-agreement

Splits every amount among the investors as the partnership agreement
sets it: the later investors' true-up, each share of a call or a
distribution summing to the cent, the amount to distribute, the
waterfall and the carried interest with its holdback, the net asset
value and the quarter's results.

covers:
- Allocate the Quarter's Results
- Approve the True-Up
- Calculate the Later Investors' True-Up
- Run the Waterfall
- Set the Amount to Distribute
- Work Out Each Investor's Share
- Work Out the Carried Interest
- Work Out the Net Asset Value

## run-calls-and-distributions

Takes in a call request or cleared proceeds, checks a call is allowed
and takes out the excused investors, drafts one notice per investor,
chases a late payer with investor relations, hands the cash to its use,
and reconciles each call and distribution to the cent.

covers:
- Chase a Late or Short Payment
- Check the Call Is Allowed
- Draft the Distribution Notices
- Draft the Notices
- Hand the Cash to Its Use
- Reconcile the Call
- Reconcile the Distribution
- Record What the Call Taught
- Record What the Run Taught
- Take in the Call Request
- Take in the Proceeds
- Take Out the Excused Investors

## charge-fees-and-expenses

Sets each period's fee basis, works out the management fee and the
offsets from company fees, classifies every expense by the clause that
allows it, splits the shared and broken-deal costs by the policy, tests
the caps, and bills the firm and the companies for what they owe the
fund.

covers:
- Allocate the Broken-Deal Costs
- Bill What Others Owe the Fund
- Check the Charges against the Caps
- Classify Each Expense
- Collect the Fees Paid by Companies
- Gather the Period's Expenses
- Open the Fee Period
- Record What the Period Taught
- Set the Fee Basis
- Split the Shared Costs
- Work Out the Fee Offsets
- Work Out the Management Fee

## close-the-books-for-audit

Closes the quarter's and the year's books, drafts the fund's financial
statements, answers every auditor request from the books in one log,
supports the capital accounts and fees, gets the cash and the holdings
confirmed, and works the auditor's findings with the chief financial
officer.

covers:
- Answer the Auditor's Requests
- Close the Quarter's Books
- Close the Year's Books
- Confirm the Cash and the Holdings
- Draft the Financial Statements
- Open the Year-End Close
- Record What the Year Taught
- Settle the Auditor's Findings
- Support the Capital Accounts and Fees

## prepare-the-tax-documents

Characterizes each distribution and works out the tax to withhold,
works out each investor's tax share and applies its tax status, sends
estimates marked as estimates, and answers the investors' tax
questions with any correction issued as a new version.

covers:
- Answer the Investors' Tax Questions
- Apply Each Investor's Tax Status
- Characterize the Proceeds
- Send Tax Estimates to Investors
- Work Out Each Investor's Tax Share
- Work Out the Tax to Withhold
