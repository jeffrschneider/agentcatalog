# case-manager

## hold-one-owner

Gives each case one named owner, one state and one clock, takes in each
new case or billing question, takes back a case a person must answer,
and moves an escalated case to its new owner with its clock and its
promises carried over.

covers:
- Assign the Case to One Specialist
- Hand the Case to a Person
- Move the Case to Its New Owner
- Take In the Billing Question

## gather-the-case

Gathers the account, the case history, the customer's past cases, the
replies, the steps tried and the promises, and sets them beside the
person who must decide or send.

covers:
- Gather the Case History and the Records
- Gather the Case as It Stands
- Gather the Customer's Records
- Set the Case Beside the Reply

## keep-the-identity-check-on-the-case

Sends each action that would read out or change anything in the account
to the identity check, records the result with the level reached, holds
a possible takeover with the account untouched, and ends the pass when
the case closes or its window runs out.

covers:
- Confirm the Asker Was Verified
- Confirm the Person Where the Case Needs It
- Hold a Possible Takeover
- Let the Result Lapse
- Record the Result on the Case
- Start the Identity Check Where Needed
- Take In the Action Asked For

## keep-each-promise

Records each promise made to the customer, by a reply, a sensitive
reply, an outage summary or a complaint's answer, with its owner and its
date, keeps each linked case open with its next update due, and raises
each promise before its date passes.

covers:
- Keep the Case Open Until Finance Answers
- Keep the Linked Customers Updated
- Keep the Promises the Answer Makes
- Raise a Promise Before Its Date
- Record Each Promise with Its Date
- Record What the Reply Commits To
- Record the Promises the Summary Makes

## record-what-the-customer-was-told

Keeps the case beside the person who tells the customer something that
matters, such as who now holds the case, what is known about a bug, a
credit or a complaint's answer, and records who approved and sent it and
when.

covers:
- Record the Approval on the Case
- Send the Answer
- Tell Each Customer Its Credit
- Tell Each Linked Customer What Is Known
- Tell the Customer Who Holds It
- Tell the Customer Who Holds It Now

## hand-on-what-belongs-elsewhere

Opens what belongs to another process there with the case and the
customer's own words, such as a claim that an invoice is wrong, a
request for money back or a change of plan, a bug or what an outage
follow-up does not cover, and links the case to it.

covers:
- Hand On What Belongs Elsewhere
- Hand the Claim to Finance
- Link Each Case to the Bug
- Pass On What the Follow-Up Does Not Cover
- Send a Refund or Plan Request On

## pass-an-account-signal

Writes up a sign that an account may leave or buy more in the customer's
own words, sorts it with the support specialist, joins it to anything
Sales already holds, confirms the account owner has it, and records
where it went and what Sales decided.

covers:
- Check Whether Sales Already Holds It
- Confirm the Account Owner Has It
- Record Where the Signal Went
- Sort the Signal
- Take Back What Sales Decided
- Write Down the Customer's Own Words

## close-and-reopen

Checks each linked case against the customer's own account, closes a
case once the customer has the answer or the fix and every promise is
kept or handed on, reopens it with the same specialist when it did not
work, and starts the survey.

covers:
- Check Each Linked Case
- Close or Keep Open Each Linked Case
- Close the Case
- Close the Question on the Case
- Close the Run on the Case
- Reopen a Case That Did Not Work
- Start the Survey

## record-what-cases-taught

Records the cause of each case, the content that was missing or wrong,
the promises missed and the time it took, the bills that confused
customers, and which account signals held.

covers:
- Record What the Case Taught
- Record What the Question Taught
- Record What the Signal Taught
