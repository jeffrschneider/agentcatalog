# fund-modeler

## read-the-last-fund

Reads the last fund's partnership agreement for when a successor may be
raised, with the chief compliance officer, and sets what the last fund
planned beside what it actually did, with the reason for each gap.

covers:
- Check the Last Fund Allows a New One
- Read the Last Fund's Results

## build-the-portfolio-construction

Turns the mandate the partners set into the number and size of deals,
the ownership or control each takes and the pace of investing, sets the
reserve from how the last fund used its own, and sizes the fund and the
partners' commitment from the construction and what investors will
back.

covers:
- Model the Portfolio Construction
- Set the Fund Size
- Set the Mandate and Limits
- Set the Reserve for Later Rounds
- Set the Team and the Firm's Commitment

## price-the-economics-and-test-the-returns

Prices the management fee and its step-down, the carried interest, the
hurdle, the catch-up and the fee offsets in the model, and tests the net
return to investors in the base, low and high cases with the deal
outcomes each case assumes.

covers:
- Set the Fees and Carried Interest
- Test the Returns the Strategy Needs

## put-the-strategy-to-the-partners

Sets two or three options before the partners, each with its size,
construction, economics, net return and what it gives up, and freezes
the chosen model beside the written strategy at the same version.

covers:
- Put the Options to the Partners
- Write the Strategy Down

## measure-a-deal-against-the-fund

Shows a proposed deal's share of commitments alone and combined, the
capital left after fees, reserves, excused investors and the pipeline,
and what the deal does to the fund's diversification, reserves and
expected return.

covers:
- Check the Capital Left
- Model the Deal's Effect on the Fund
- Test the Concentration Limits

## set-the-reserves-in-the-fund-model

Proposes each company's reserve from the deal lead's ask, models the
fund at each reserve level, checks the reserves against uncalled
capital, recycling and the fund's terms, and writes the reserves the
partners agree into the fund model at a new version.

covers:
- Check the Fund Can Carry the Reserves
- Model the Fund with the Reserves
- Propose Each Company's Reserve
- Set the Reserves
- Update the Fund Model
