# performance-review-coordinator

## gather-the-evidence

Opens a review cycle or a promotion round with its people, dates and
scale, asks each employee for a self-review and each chosen peer for
feedback, takes in the nominations, sends each manager the objectives
and the evidence in one place, and gathers the record of the work behind
an improvement plan, with nothing from a health, leave or case record.

covers:
- Ask for Peer Feedback
- Ask for Self-Reviews
- Gather the Record of the Work
- Open the Promotion Round
- Open the Review Cycle
- Send Each Manager the Evidence
- Take In the Nominations

## check-for-examples

Reads each manager's draft review, each promotion case and each written
gap for an example of the work behind every rating, expectation or
point, and flags every remark about personality with its line named,
leaving the words and the judgment to the manager.

covers:
- Check Each Case for Evidence
- Check Each Draft for Evidence
- Check the Written Gap for Evidence

## prepare-the-panel-packs

Sets each team's proposed ratings or each promotion case beside its
evidence, and its proposed pay for a promotion, with nothing about pay
in a calibration pack and nothing about health, a leave's reason, a case
or a protected characteristic in any pack.

covers:
- Prepare the Calibration Packs
- Prepare the Panel Pack

## record-what-people-decide

Records the final ratings the calibration panel agreed and the promotion
panel's decisions with their reasons and dissent, the employee's
response to the review, the next period's objectives the manager and the
employee agreed, and the feedback given to each nominee not promoted.

covers:
- Agree the Next Period's Objectives
- Give Feedback to Those Not Promoted
- Record the Decisions
- Record the Employee's Response
- Record the Final Ratings

## hand-on-and-close

Hands the final ratings to the pay review and each promotion on to the
record, and closes each cycle and round with what finished on time, what
was sent back and why, and what the next one does differently.

covers:
- Close the Cycle on the Record
- Close the Round on the Record
- Hand On Each Promotion
- Hand On the Ratings
