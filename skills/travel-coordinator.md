# travel-coordinator

## read-the-trip-against-the-policy

Takes in the trip request, reads it against the travel policy at its
version for the classes, the hotel rates, the booking notice and the
destinations that need approval, and offers the options that fit with
the lowest first.

covers:
- Offer the Options Inside the Policy
- Read the Trip Against the Policy
- Take In the Trip Request

## book-the-trip

Books each part of the trip at the option the traveler chose or the
approver approved, sends the itinerary with every reference and
cancellation term, and books each change inside the policy or sends it
to the approver again.

covers:
- Book the Trip
- Change or Cancel a Booking
- Send the Itinerary

## keep-the-traveler-reachable

Keeps where the traveler is each day of the trip, open only to the named
people who respond to an emergency, and gives the travel and expense
lead who is where when an event hits a place on the itinerary.

covers:
- Keep the Traveler's Whereabouts
- Reach the Traveler in an Emergency

## close-the-trip

Passes each booking's cost to the expense record, records each unused
ticket and credit once the traveler is home, deletes the whereabouts
under the retention rule, and records what the trip taught.

covers:
- Close the Trip
- Pass the Costs to the Expense Record
- Record What the Trip Taught
