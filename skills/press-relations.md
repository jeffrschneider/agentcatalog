# press-relations

## settle-what-the-approach-must-do

Takes in what an outside contact asked for and by when, and settles what the organization wants out of the exchange, which means the result worth having, who will speak, and what the story says and why it is news.

covers:
- Set What the Briefing Must Do
- Settle What the Briefing Will Say
- Settle the Story
- Take in the Request

## judge-how-far-a-story-will-travel

Reads a story and says how much outside attention it will draw, whether that is deciding a reporter would care about the angle or working out who is affected and how far the thing will spread.

covers:
- Size up the Exposure
- Test the Angle

## set-the-announcement-date

Sets the date the news goes public, the embargo that holds until then, and the order in which each audience hears it.

covers:
- Set the Announcement Date

## draft-the-words-and-the-versions

Writes the words the organization will say and holds them at a version, then produces the version each audience gets.

covers:
- Draft the Statement
- Tailor for Each Audience

## choose-who-to-approach

Reads the contact record for the reporters, editors and analysts a given story actually fits, and says who gets approached and who is offered it early.

covers:
- Choose the Reporters
- Line up the Reporters

## pitch-and-follow-up

Sends each contact the version written for them, asks again when nothing comes back, and records what every one of them said.

covers:
- Follow up on the Pitches
- Send the Pitches

## set-up-the-briefing

Books the people who will be in the session, both the outside contacts and the customers who agreed to speak, and holds the date and time each one is expected.

covers:
- Invite the Press and the Analysts
- Line up the Customer References

## prepare-the-people-who-speak

Works through the questions the speakers are going to be asked, gives everyone who might be asked the same answers, and runs them through it once with the hard questions included.

covers:
- Brief the Speakers
- Prepare the Spokesperson
- Rehearse the Briefing

## hold-the-session

Runs the live session while it is happening, keeps it to the plan, and fixes what breaks in it.

covers:
- Hold the Briefing
- Run the Show

## answer-what-comes-back

Answers the questions that come back from reporters, analysts, customers and the public, and sends the extra data, the fact check or the further interview that was asked for. Every exchange is logged with what was asked and what was given.

covers:
- Answer What Reporters Ask
- Answer What Was Left Open
- Answer the Press
- Answer the Questions

## issue-the-announcement

Sends the approved words out on the agreed date across every channel that was named, and records what went out where.

covers:
- Issue the Release
- Issue the Response
- Say It in Public

## hand-work-to-the-agent-that-owns-it

Passes an item to the agent or the person who owns it and records where it went, and puts the same request to every agent that has to act on it at once.

covers:
- Brief the Makers
- Hand the Result On
- Route the Escalations

## track-what-outside-contacts-publish

Reads what each outside contact has already published about the organization, logs whatever they publish afterwards, and reports what they now say against what the organization set out to have them say.

covers:
- Read Where the Analyst Stands
- Report Whether the Positioning Landed
- Watch What Gets Published

## ask-for-a-correction

Reads a published piece against the facts, names each error, and asks the outlet to correct it.

covers:
- Check the Published Note

## record-what-was-learned

Writes down what is worth repeating next time and what to avoid, so the next run starts from it.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
