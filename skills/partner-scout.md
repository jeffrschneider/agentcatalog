# partner-scout

## set-the-bounds-of-a-search

Reads the written gap and the test that says when the gap is closed, and
turns them into the bounds the search runs inside, including the
companies it may not touch.

covers:
- Set the Search Bounds

## look-for-candidates

Looks for companies that could close the gap. Reads the partners,
customers and suppliers already on the books first and goes out to the
market only after that, so a partner we already have is not missed in
favour of a new name.

covers:
- Look at Who We Already Know
- Search the Market

## read-and-screen-each-candidate

Reads what each candidate sells, to whom and where, then takes out the
ones that cannot work and writes the reason against every removal.

covers:
- Read Each Candidate
- Screen Out the Impossible

## score-the-survivors-against-the-closing-test

Scores every candidate that survives screening against the test the gap
set, with the evidence line behind each score and the date that evidence
was read.

covers:
- Score the Candidates

## approach-a-candidate-and-record-the-answer

Makes first contact on the approach a person set, then keeps what came
back: who replied, who declined, who never answered, and the reason each
one gave.

covers:
- Make First Contact
- Record Interest and Refusal
