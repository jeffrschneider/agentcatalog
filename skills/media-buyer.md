# media-buyer

## take-in-what-starts-a-piece-of-work

Takes in whatever starts a piece of work and records what it covers,
whether that is creative arriving with the plan lines it has to fill, a
report that an ad ran somewhere it should not have, or a signal that a
line is off its pace and by how much.

covers:
- Take in the Concern
- Take in the Creative and the Plan
- Take in the Pacing Signal

## collect-what-each-area-asks-for

Asks every area what it wants to run or change, in one format and against
one due date, and puts the answers into a single list.

covers:
- Ask Each Area What It Would Change
- Collect What Each Function Asks For
- Collect the Candidate Initiatives

## put-numbers-on-what-is-being-asked-for

Puts cost and expected return on each request, initiative or proposed
change, with the assumptions written underneath and what it would
displace. It also reads the terms behind money already placed, so a
proposal to pull that money out carries the notice, the minimums, the
rate tiers and the cancellation cost.

covers:
- Check What a Move Would Cost
- Price the Requests
- Put Numbers on Each Initiative
- Put Numbers on Each Request
- Put Numbers on the Proposed Changes

## read-what-is-committed-and-what-is-free

Reports what the period has already committed, what has been spent
against it, and what is still free to place.

covers:
- List the Committed Work
- Read the Current Position
- Work out What Is Free

## read-what-a-channel-costs-and-delivers

Gathers the evidence behind a buying decision: what each channel has
cost, delivered and returned, what the creative in flight is delivering
by placement, and what people search for and what a click on those terms
costs.

covers:
- Gather the Channel Evidence
- Read the Current Performance
- Research the Search Demand

## draft-the-options-for-moving-the-money

Puts up two or three ways the money could be split across channels or
moved between lines, saying for each one which lines give money up and
which lines take it.

covers:
- Draft the Candidate Mixes
- Name the Candidate Moves

## lay-out-what-runs-when

Lays the agreed work across the calendar: what runs on which dates and
channels, at what weight, and who owes what when more than one party is
paying.

covers:
- Build the Calendar
- Plan the Joint Campaign

## decide-who-the-buy-goes-after

Decides what the buy targets: the audience each channel is bought
against, the terms to bid on and how they are grouped, and who is kept
out or must not overlap.

covers:
- Build the Keyword Plan
- Name the Exclusions
- Set the Channel Audience Targets

## set-how-the-buy-delivers

Sets the dates each line runs, the money it may spend a day, how the
versions rotate, and how often one person may see an ad and for how long.

covers:
- Set the Exposure Rules
- Set the Flights, Budgets and Rotation

## build-the-campaigns-in-the-platforms

Builds the buy in the systems that will run it: campaigns, ad sets and
ads named to the plan line they answer to, the audiences loaded, the
schedule set, and each piece of creative sized to what its placement
takes.

covers:
- Build the Ad Units
- Prep the Channels
- Read the Placement Specifications
- Set up Each Side's Distribution
- Set up the Paid Buy
- Set up the Practical Side
- Set up the Sending

## wire-up-the-tracking

Puts the measurement on before anything runs: the parameters every ad
carries, the codes that separate one source or one variant from another,
and the conversions that count, each one fired once and tested.

covers:
- Build the Tracking Parameters
- Set up the Tracking
- Wire up Conversion Tracking
- Wire up the Measurement

## line-up-the-page-the-ad-points-at

Makes sure the page behind an ad answers what that ad was bought for, and
gives the campaign the address and the tracking that go with it.

covers:
- Hand It to the Campaign
- Ready the Landing Pages

## load-an-audience-list-into-the-platforms

Formats an audience list for each system it is going to, delivers it,
confirms it arrived, and reports how much of the list each one could
actually match and reach.

covers:
- Confirm the Match
- Hand the List to the Platforms
- Test Whether Each Segment Can Be Reached

## read-back-what-is-set-against-what-was-agreed

Reads what is actually set in the systems and compares it with what was
decided, covering caps, pools, exclusions, splits, tags, variants and
budgets, then reports anything that does not match.

covers:
- Check the Buy Before Switch-On
- Check the Change Took
- Check the Test Before Switch-On

## switch-the-buy-on

Starts the buy once the named owner says go, at the bids, caps and splits
already set, and confirms that everything named is serving.

covers:
- Build the Campaigns and Launch
- Launch the Campaigns
- Launch the Test
- Start the Touches
- Switch the Ads On

## record-what-is-running

Writes down at a version what is live: which creative runs on which line,
and the pools, caps and exclusions in force behind it.

covers:
- Record What Is Live
- Record What Is in Force

## promote-a-run-with-paid-media

Runs the paid push behind an event, a session or a piece of content,
pointed at whatever counts as a response, such as a registration or a
read.

covers:
- Promote the Conference
- Promote the Event
- Promote the Piece
- Promote the Session

## watch-what-is-running

Watches a live buy against what it was supposed to do and reports the
gap: spend and pace, clicks and conversions, whether a test split still
holds, and what came back from the people it reached.

covers:
- Watch Delivery
- Watch Pacing and Results
- Watch the Response

## adjust-a-live-buy

Changes a buy that is already running. It moves money between lines and
systems, resets daily limits so each flight lands on its date, moves
bids, swaps out creative that has gone stale, and turns wasted terms and
unwanted inventory into exclusions.

covers:
- Apply the Exclusions
- Correct the Pacing
- Move the Money in the Platforms
- Optimize
- Tune the Bids and Terms

## design-a-creative-test

Designs a test of one change: what is being changed and what it should
improve, how many variants there are, how the traffic splits, where they
run, and how much exposure has to build up before the result counts.

covers:
- Design the Comparison
- Name the Change to Test
- Set the Evidence Bar

## call-a-test-and-roll-out-the-winner

Reads a finished test against the bar that was set and says whether there
is a winner, no difference, or more time needed, then puts the winning
version in place of what was running and turns the rest off.

covers:
- Call the Winner
- Roll out the Winner

## brief-the-agents-that-will-spend

Opens a run by telling every agent that will spend the same thing at
once: what the plan is, what each of them owes against it, and by when.

covers:
- Brief the Build
- Brief the Department
- Hand the Plan to the Buying Agents

## hand-a-decision-to-the-agents-that-act

Passes a settled decision to each agent that has to act on it, including
the money it has been given and the rules that come with it, and takes
back what each one says it will change.

covers:
- Hand over the Commitments
- Hand over the Profile
- Hand over the Segments
- Hand the Strategy to the Work that Follows
- Tell Every Function What It Has

## set-what-a-close-or-an-audit-covers

Says which accounts, campaigns, channels and dates a close or an audit
covers, before any records are pulled.

covers:
- Set What the Audit Covers
- Set What the Close Covers

## pull-the-record-of-what-actually-ran

Pulls the record of what happened out of each system: what was ordered
and at which rates, what was recorded as delivered and spent, and every
site, app and channel the ads actually ran on.

covers:
- Pull the Delivery Record
- Pull the Order Record
- Pull the Placement Record

## match-the-records-against-the-books

Sets the spend the systems recorded against what the books say and clears
the differences the buy's own records already explain, leaving only the
ones that need an answer from outside.

covers:
- Clear the Explained Differences
- Reconcile the Spend

## settle-what-happens-to-each-finding

Decides what to do with each thing a close or an audit turned up, whether
to exclude it, contest it or accept it and say why, claims back what was
spent on impressions that failed, and follows every query through to a
credit, a fix, a make-good or a refusal.

covers:
- Claim Back the Wasted Spend
- Decide What Happens to Each
- Track the Answers

## account-for-what-was-spent

Reports what the paid buy spent by campaign and channel and what came
back from it, and confirms the figures that name the buy in another
agent's report before that report goes out.

covers:
- Account for the Paid Spend
- Check the Account

## change-a-published-price

Finds every live page, ad and asset that names a price and changes it,
puts the new pages up, and points the old addresses at them.

covers:
- Change the Public Price
- Restate the Price Everywhere It Is Published

## record-what-was-learned

Closes a run with what to check first the next time, naming the lines and
the issuers that caused the most work.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
