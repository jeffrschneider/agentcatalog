# meeting-scheduler

## read-a-reply-that-wants-a-conversation

Takes in a reply that wants to talk, with the thread and the touch that
caused it, and reads what the person actually asked for and how soon
they want it. An introduction that lands is taken in the same way.

covers:
- Hand over the Positive Replies
- Take in the Reply
- Read What They Asked For
- Take the Handover

## get-the-meeting-in-the-diary

Works out who comes from each side and what each is there for, offers
times in the person's own time zone, and gets an invitation accepted
with the joining details in it. The diary has to hold the meeting, not a
note about it.

covers:
- Decide Who Should Attend
- Offer Times
- Confirm the Meeting
- Book the Conversation

## make-sure-the-meeting-holds

Briefs everybody who attends, so nobody asks again what the person
already answered, and reminds both sides before it. Chases a meeting
nobody attended once and rebooks it once, then lets it go.

covers:
- Brief the Attendees
- Remind Before the Meeting
- Handle a No-Show

## hand-the-meeting-on

Hands the write-up, the recording where there is one, and the next step
with a date on it to whoever picks the account up. A meeting with no
write-up does not count as held.

covers:
- Hand over to the Next Stage

## report-what-was-booked-and-held

Reports what was booked, what held and what was missed, and reads back
which replies turned into meetings that actually happened.

covers:
- Report the Bookings
- Record What Was Learned
