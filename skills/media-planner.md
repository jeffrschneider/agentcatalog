# media-planner

## take-in-what-the-plan-must-do

Takes in what the money has to achieve and by when, and collects the launches and campaigns the plan has to carry.

covers:
- Collect What the Plan Must Carry
- Take in the Objective and the Budget

## settle-the-audience-to-buy-against

Settles who the plan buys against and how many of them, then sets the audience each channel is bought against.

covers:
- Set the Channel Audience Targets
- Settle the Audience to Reach

## settle-what-counts-as-a-result

Says what the plan is judged on and where that result is counted, so every channel is read the same way afterwards.

covers:
- Settle What Counts as a Result

## work-up-the-candidate-mixes

Builds two or three ways of splitting the money across the channels and works out what each split would reach, what it would cost and what it would return.

covers:
- Draft the Candidate Mixes
- Model Each Mix

## get-the-money-committed

Puts a split and an amount in front of the people who own the money, and records what they committed and when.

covers:
- Choose the Mix
- Commit the Test Budget
- Get the Plan Approved

## set-the-flighting-and-the-weights

Writes the running detail for each line of the plan: the dates it runs, the money it gets in each period, where it runs, and how much weight it carries against the other lines.

covers:
- Lay out the Flighting
- Set the Flights, Budgets and Rotation
- Set the Parameters
- Set the Tiers and the Weight

## version-the-plan

Writes a new version of the plan whenever the split changes, and holds the reason for the change, the figures behind it, the assumptions it rests on and what would reopen it.

covers:
- Amend the Media Plan
- Record What the Plan Rests On
- Record Why the Money Moved

## read-the-current-position

Reads the running position of the plan and answers what is committed, what has been spent and what is still free.

covers:
- Read the Current Position

## name-the-candidate-moves

Works out which lines of the plan could give money up and which lines could take it, and puts those moves forward as candidates.

covers:
- Name the Candidate Moves

## test-a-proposed-move

Works out what a proposed move would cost to make, reading the notice periods, minimums, rate tiers and cancellation terms behind it, and scores the move against the plan and the terms the buying is held to.

covers:
- Check What a Move Would Cost
- Check the Move against the Rules

## hand-the-plan-to-the-agents-that-spend

Gives every agent that will spend against the plan the same version at the same time, and tells the agents already working to an older split what changed.

covers:
- Brief the Makers
- Hand the Plan to the Buying Agents
- Tell the Agents that Read the Plan
