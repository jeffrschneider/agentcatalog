# advocacy-manager

## take-in-the-request

Reads a request for a customer to do something in public and works out
what it is for and by when, whether that is a reference somebody needs
for a live deal or a goal for how many reviews on which sites.

covers:
- Take in the Reference Request
- Take in the Review Goal

## set-the-profile-and-the-rules

Writes down which customer would fit the ask, what the ask may offer,
and what it has to disclose. It reads each site's own policy on asking
and on incentives first, because those policies differ and some forbid
an incentive outright.

covers:
- Set the Reference Profile
- Set the Rules for Asking
- Read the Site Rules

## pick-the-customers-to-ask

Pulls the accounts that match the profile, orders them by how well they
fit and how ready they are, and settles who actually gets asked this
cycle.

covers:
- Draw up the Candidate List
- Choose the Customers to Ask
- Rank the Shortlist
- Take in the Candidate
- Settle the Membership

## clear-the-ask-with-the-account-owner

Puts the shortlist in front of the people who own those relationships
and gets their word on whether each customer may be asked now. A name
comes off the list when the owner says so.

covers:
- Clear the Ask
- Clear the List with Account Owners

## write-the-ask

Puts the ask in writing so the customer can read exactly what is being
requested, together with the reminder that follows it and any line the
rules require it to carry.

covers:
- Write the Ask
- Write the Request

## put-the-ask-to-the-customers

Sends the ask to the cleared list on its schedule, sends one reminder to
whoever has not answered, and records every reply. It sets up the
practical side first, which is the list, the dates and the link each
customer follows.

covers:
- Make the Ask
- Send the Ask
- Send the Reminder
- Invite the Members
- Line up the Speakers
- Open the Call for Sessions
- Set up the Practical Side

## agree-and-record-the-terms

Settles what the customer has agreed to in their own words, which is
what may be used, in which channels, under which name and title, and
until when. It clears whatever paperwork goes with that and records the
permission with the date it runs out.

covers:
- Agree What the Customer Will Do
- Clear the Paperwork
- Record the Reference

## get-the-customers-approval

Goes back to the customer to have them clear the finished material at a
version, including their own words and the sign-off of their own legal
and communications people where the customer requires it.

covers:
- Get the Customer's Agreement
- Get the Customer's Approval
- Get the Customer's Approvals

## get-the-internal-sign-offs

Runs the material past the people inside the organization who have to
clear it before a customer is named, which covers brand, legal and
rights, and records what each of them cleared.

covers:
- Get Sign-Offs
- Get the Internal Sign-Offs
- Clear the Customer Story

## hand-the-customer-to-whoever-asked

Hands a cleared and willing customer to whoever needs them, with what
may be used and what may not, and briefs the agents that will build
something around that customer.

covers:
- Hand the Reference Over
- Hand the Story to Sales
- Line up the Customer References
- Brief the Story

## stay-in-touch-with-the-customers

Answers what comes back after an ask, which is the questions, the
complaints and the people asking to be left alone. It replies on the
site where a customer wrote, tells participants what happened with what
they said, and passes on the names of the people worth recognizing.

covers:
- Answer What Comes Back
- Reply to the Reviews
- Follow up with the Members
- Name the Members Worth Recognizing

## close-the-run

Sets what the run actually landed beside what was asked for, and writes
down what to repeat and what to change next cycle.

covers:
- Report against the Goal
- Record What Was Learned
