# mandate-check

## read-the-limits-in-force

Takes in the deal or the matter as proposed, and reads the partnership
agreement, every side letter, the offering memorandum's strategy, the
allocation policy and the advisory committee decisions in force, each at
its version, down to the clause that governs it.

covers:
- Find the Clause That Requires Consent
- Read the Allocation Policy
- Read the Limits That Apply
- Take in the Proposed Deal

## test-against-the-fund-terms

Tests a deal, a fund share or a sell-down plan against the investment
limits, the concentration limits alone and combined, the exclusions,
the excused investors, the investment period and the fund's terms, with
the clause behind each result and no limit read generously.

covers:
- Check the Deal Against the Mandate
- Check the Fund's Own Share
- Check the Investment Period
- Check the Plan against the Fund's Terms
- Find the Excused Investors
- Test the Concentration Limits
- Test the Exclusions
- Test the Investment Limits

## find-the-conflicts

Checks the company, its parties, each candidate and each sale against
the restricted list, finds conflicts with the firm's other funds, its
portfolio companies and its own people, sets out who gains and who
could lose, and brings each one to the chief compliance officer to
decide.

covers:
- Check Each Candidate for Conflicts
- Check the Restricted List
- Clear Each Conflict
- Clear Each Sale with Compliance
- Find Conflicts with Other Funds
- Find Conflicts with Portfolio Companies
- Find Interests of the Firm's People
- Set Out the Conflict

## issue-the-result-and-its-consents

Names every consent the deal would need with its clause, issues one
dated result as clear, clear with conditions or blocked, hands it to
the memo, the consent process and the deal lead, and tracks each
condition a consent carried to closed with its evidence.

covers:
- Hand the Result On
- Issue the Result
- Name the Consents Needed
- Track the Conditions to Closed

## allocate-under-the-policy

Lists who is entitled to a co-investment offer in the policy's order,
sets the amounts within its order and caps so they add up to the amount
to place, checks each allocation against the policy and the side letter
rights, and records each one with the policy line behind it.

covers:
- Allocate Under the Policy
- Check Each Allocation
- List Who Is Entitled to an Offer
- Record Each Allocation and Its Reason
