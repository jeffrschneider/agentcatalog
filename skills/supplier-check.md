# supplier-check

## hear-the-requirements

The agent sits in the briefing that opens a selection, so it hears what
the organization needs at the same time as the agents writing the
requirements down.

covers:
- Brief the Selection

## set-the-criteria-before-any-candidate-arrives

The agent writes down how a candidate or a proposal will be judged and
fixes that list at a version before the first candidate is seen. Every
candidate is then scored against the same list in the same order,
whenever it arrived.

covers:
- Set the Scoring Criteria
- Set the Selection Criteria
- Set the Criteria for Each Provider
- Write the Renewal Criteria

## take-in-the-candidate-material

The agent sits through each candidate's presentation and takes in what
was submitted, recording the date the material arrived and which
requirements it speaks to.

covers:
- Hear the Pitches

## score-against-the-requirements

The agent scores every candidate against every requirement and returns a
verdict on each one, naming the requirement it was scored against and
the evidence behind the verdict. A requirement it could not test is
recorded as not tested, with the reason.

covers:
- Score the Proposals
- Score the Shortlist
- Score the Candidate Providers
- Choose the Sell-Side Adviser

## compare-the-scored-field

The agent sets the scores side by side with the trial results and the
cost, and says which candidates miss a requirement and come off the
list.

covers:
- Compare the Finalists
- Cut to a Shortlist

## arrange-an-outside-provider

Where the process needs somebody outside the organization to do the
work, the agent scores the providers available against the requirements
and arranges the one that meets them.

covers:
- Arrange the Outside Interviewer
- Arrange the Outside Sample

## check-the-company-on-the-public-record

The agent reads the registries for a company's registration, its good
standing and who owns it, and reads the court and regulator records for
suits, threats, regulator letters and settlements beside outside
counsel. Each result names the record it came from and the date it was
read.

covers:
- Check the Company Is Real
- Read the Litigation and Claims

## screen-against-the-lists

The agent screens investors and their owners, target companies and
their leaders, and the firm's own people and providers against
sanctions lists, the anti-money laundering rules, adverse records and
the events that would disqualify an offering. Every hit goes to the
chief compliance officer with what was found and where, and the agent
records the decision it is given without clearing any hit itself.

covers:
- Screen Each Investor and Its Owners
- Screen the Company and Its Leaders
- Check No One Disqualifies the Offering
- Clear the Screening Hits
