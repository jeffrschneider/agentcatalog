# deal-screener

## take-in-and-clear-the-material

Logs a teaser or an information memorandum as it arrives with its
timetable and the people allowed to read it, and checks that a pitch
may be read, with no terms the firm will not accept and no reader who
sits on a competing portfolio company's board.

covers:
- Check the Pitch May Be Read
- Receive the Information Memorandum
- Take in the Teaser

## match-and-read-the-company

Matches the company to past pitches, meetings, bids, passes, sector
maps and owners, reads what it does, its numbers and its ask with every
claim marked as a claim, and weighs who referred it and what ties them
to it.

covers:
- Match It to What the Firm Holds
- Read the Information Memorandum
- Read the Pitch
- Weigh the Referral

## test-against-the-mandate-and-the-theses

Tests the company against the fund's limits and exclusions at their
version, with the clause behind each result, and against the firm's
written theses, saying which one it fits and what would have to be true.

covers:
- Test It Against the Mandate
- Test It Against the Theses
- Test the Teaser Against the Mandate

## write-the-screen-note-for-a-partner

Writes one page with the case, the fit, the gaps and a proposed answer
with its reason, sees it routed to the partner who decides, brings it to
each decision to engage, bid or answer a pitch, and drafts the one list
of what is missing.

covers:
- Ask for What Is Missing
- Decide the Pitch
- Decide Whether to Bid
- Decide Whether to Engage
- Route the Note to a Partner
- Write the Screen Note

## answer-the-bank

Writes the indication of interest in the form the process letter asks
for, with the range the partners decided, its basis, its financing and
its conditions, or tells the bank in writing that the firm withdraws and
sees the material returned or destroyed.

covers:
- Withdraw in Writing
- Write the Indication of Interest

## record-the-outcome

Opens the opportunity with its named owner when a partner decides to
meet, and records the decision, its reason, the time the answer took,
and for a sale the bank's reply and the price the company sold for.

covers:
- Open the Opportunity
- Record the Decision and the Time
- Record the Outcome
