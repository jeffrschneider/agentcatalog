# close-plan-keeper

## open-a-plan-from-the-agreed-position

Takes the agreed position as it comes across from the negotiation and
opens the plan on it, with the signature date both sides are working
toward written at the top.

covers:
- Hand On the Agreed Position
- Open the Close Plan
- Open the Closing Checklist

## open-the-formation-checklist

Takes the decided strategy and the fund model in at their version and
opens one formation checklist on them, listing every step the fund
needs before it can take a commitment, each with one owner and one
date.

covers:
- Take in the Decided Strategy
- Open the Formation Checklist

## name-the-steps-and-put-an-owner-and-a-date-on-each

Lists every step still owed as one set rather than two, walks the
buyer's own approval path committee by committee, puts one named owner
on each step and counts the dates back from signing.

covers:
- Name the Steps Each Side Owes
- Confirm the Buyer's Own Process
- Put an Owner on Every Step
- Date Every Step
- Put an Owner and a Date on Each

## work-the-steps-that-take-the-longest

Runs the buyer's security review and the procurement steps as dated
steps inside the plan, which means the vendor forms, the supplier setup
and the order rather than a wait nobody owns.

covers:
- Work the Security Review
- Work the Procurement Steps

## work-each-owned-step-to-done

Holds each step that somebody else owns as a dated step inside the
checklist, and works it with its owner until it is done with its
evidence. In forming a fund these are the entities counsel forms, the
partnership agreement and subscription documents counsel drafts, the
filings the compliance officer makes, and the books and portals the
fund opens. In a closing they are the capital call the fund needs, the
consents, filings and approvals the closing waits on, and what the
agreements still owe once the deal has closed.

covers:
- Form the Entities
- Draft the Partnership Agreement
- Draft the Subscription Documents
- Make the Regulatory Filings
- Open the Fund's Records
- Request the Capital Call
- Work the Closing Conditions
- Track the Post-Closing Items

## build-the-funds-flow

Builds one funds flow for a closing with every payment, payee, amount
and account on it, beside the cap-table-keeper agent, so that a payment
the sheet does not carry is never made. Each line goes to the
wire-check agent to be verified, and this agent releases no money.

covers:
- Build the Funds Flow

## chase-what-slips-and-re-plan

Chases a step past its date with its owner, re-dates it with the buyer
or sends it up, and moves every step behind it when a date moves.
Reports where the deal stands against its own plan.

covers:
- Chase the Slipping Steps
- Re-Plan When a Date Moves
- Report the Plan

## close-the-plan-out

Checks every condition owed before signature as done with the evidence
behind it, and closes the plan on the record with what slipped, why,
and what to plan differently next time.

covers:
- Confirm the Close Conditions
- Close the Plan on the Record
- Confirm Every Condition Is Met
- Close the Formation Checklist

## wind-a-dead-deal-down

Stops the trials, pilots and pending approvals when a deal is called
lost, and gives back or deletes what the buyer gave us.

covers:
- Stop the Open Work
- Return the Buyer's Material
