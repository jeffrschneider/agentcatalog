# close-plan-keeper

## open-a-plan-from-the-agreed-position

Takes the agreed position as it comes across from the negotiation and
opens the plan on it, with the signature date both sides are working
toward written at the top.

covers:
- Hand On the Agreed Position
- Open the Close Plan

## name-the-steps-and-put-an-owner-and-a-date-on-each

Lists every step still owed as one set rather than two, walks the
buyer's own approval path committee by committee, puts one named owner
on each step and counts the dates back from signing.

covers:
- Name the Steps Each Side Owes
- Confirm the Buyer's Own Process
- Put an Owner on Every Step
- Date Every Step

## work-the-steps-that-take-the-longest

Runs the buyer's security review and the procurement steps as dated
steps inside the plan, which means the vendor forms, the supplier setup
and the order rather than a wait nobody owns.

covers:
- Work the Security Review
- Work the Procurement Steps

## chase-what-slips-and-re-plan

Chases a step past its date with its owner, re-dates it with the buyer
or sends it up, and moves every step behind it when a date moves.
Reports where the deal stands against its own plan.

covers:
- Chase the Slipping Steps
- Re-Plan When a Date Moves
- Report the Plan

## close-the-plan-out

Checks every condition owed before signature as done with the evidence
behind it, and closes the plan on the record with what slipped, why,
and what to plan differently next time.

covers:
- Confirm the Close Conditions
- Close the Plan on the Record

## wind-a-dead-deal-down

Stops the trials, pilots and pending approvals when a deal is called
lost, and gives back or deletes what the buyer gave us.

covers:
- Stop the Open Work
- Return the Buyer's Material
