# deal-structurer

## price-what-diligence-found

Takes in the diligence result and prices every finding into a term the
agreement has to carry, or calls it a stop. A finding that does not
make it into the paper is raised rather than dropped.

covers:
- Decide What Each Finding Costs
- Take in the Diligence Result

## settle-what-the-arrangement-has-to-do

Writes down what each side must get before any shape is chosen, then
chooses the shape, whether that is a resell, an embed, a joint product,
an investment or a purchase, and says why it beat the others
considered.

covers:
- Choose the Shape
- Set What Each Side Must Get

## set-the-commercial-terms

Settles who charges the customer, who invoices whom and who keeps what,
how long the arrangement runs and what notice each ending needs, and
stands up the ordering, invoicing and split the joint offer will
actually run on.

covers:
- Set the Commercial Machinery
- Set the Money Model
- Set the Term and the Exit

## model-what-it-is-worth

Models what the arrangement would be worth, with the assumptions
written out and the downside case stated beside the upside one, at a
version somebody else can rerun.

covers:
- Model What It Is Worth
- Model the Deal Economics

## take-the-paper-from-a-term-sheet-to-signature

Writes the agreed shape onto a term sheet, closes the other side's asks
one at a time with a date on every position taken, supplies the shape
the definitive agreement is drafted from, and reads every redline back
against the term sheet the two companies agreed.

covers:
- Draft the Definitive Agreement
- Draft the Term Sheet
- Negotiate the Term Sheet
- Review the Redlines

## hand-on-the-obligations

Writes what the signed terms owe out as work with owners and dates, so
the obligations the agreement created have somebody behind them before
anybody starts.

covers:
- Hand On the Obligations
