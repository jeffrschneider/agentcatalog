# reference-finder

## take-in-a-reference-request

Reads the request that starts a run: who is asking and what the deal
turns on. What the prospect actually has to hear is written down before
anybody is searched for, in terms of the industry, the size, the
deployment shape and the specific worry.

covers:
- Take in the Reference Request
- Say What the Prospect Must Hear

## find-customers-who-match-and-are-free-to-ask

Searches only the customers whose written agreement to speak is
current, checks the cool-down clock on each one, and comes back with a
short ranked list carrying a match reason for every name.

covers:
- Search the Reference Pool
- Check When Each Was Last Asked
- Shortlist the Candidates

## put-the-ask-to-the-customer

Puts the ask to a shortlisted customer through the person who owns that
relationship, and records the answer. A no is recorded as a no, and no
reason is demanded for it.

covers:
- Ask the Customer

## brief-both-sides-and-set-the-conversation-up

Tells the reference customer what will be asked and what is off limits,
tells the prospect the same bounds, and books the time and the way both
sides join.

covers:
- Brief the Reference
- Brief the Prospect
- Set the Conversation Up

## close-an-ask-out

Thanks the customer against their own record, puts them back on the
clock with the date they return to the available list, and feeds back
what the prospect took from the conversation.

covers:
- Thank the Reference
- Reset the Cool-Down Clock
- Feed Back What the Prospect Heard
