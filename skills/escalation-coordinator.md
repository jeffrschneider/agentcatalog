# escalation-coordinator

## hand-a-case-up-a-tier

Takes in each escalation, writes one summary of the problem, the steps
tried and what the customer was told, chooses the tier and a named
specialist, sends a case one tier up when this tier cannot resolve it,
hands the outcome back, and tells the first specialist what solved it.

covers:
- Choose the Tier and the Specialist
- Escalate to the Next Tier
- Hand the Outcome Back
- Take In the Escalation
- Tell the First Specialist What Solved It
- Write the Escalation Summary

## prepare-a-bug-report

Takes in each reproduced fault, checks that its steps repeat and its
evidence is present, removes customer data engineering does not need,
and searches the bugs already filed for the same fault under other
words.

covers:
- Check the Report Is Complete
- Remove Customer Data from the Evidence
- Search the Bugs Already Filed
- Take In the Reproduced Fault

## file-or-join-a-bug

Joins a case to the bug already filed with its account and any new
evidence, or proposes a severity from the harm, the accounts and any
workaround and files a new bug with the steps, the evidence and the
accounts.

covers:
- File the Bug
- Join the Case to the Bug Already Filed
- Propose the Severity

## follow-up-with-engineering

Asks engineering or the security team for the status of each bug or
report on the cadence its severity sets, records each answer, and brings
a change in the picture to the person who sets a new severity.

covers:
- Decide a New Severity
- Follow the Bug Up
- Follow the Report Up

## carry-a-fix-back

Hands over each bug marked fixed with its linked cases, takes in the fix
with the version and the plans it reached, lists every customer and case
linked to it with who has the fix, and takes back a reply that says the
fix did not help as a case of its own.

covers:
- Hand Over the Fixed Bug
- List the Customers Linked to the Bug
- Take Back a Fix That Did Not Help
- Take In a Fixed Bug

## get-a-security-report-to-its-owner

Takes in a security or safety report as it was made, restricts it to the
named people who must see it, has it taken out of public view, sorts it,
sends it within the hour to the security team or the safety owner and a
possible exposure of personal data to the privacy lead and the legal
team, joins later reports of the same thing, and records who knew what
and when.

covers:
- Join Later Reports of the Same Thing
- Record the Outcome
- Restrict the Case
- Send It to the Security Team or the Safety Owner
- Sort the Report
- Take In the Report
- Take It out of Public View
- Tell the Privacy Lead and the Legal Team

## record-what-escalations-taught

Records why each case escalated, how long each step took, whether the
summary held, what engineering sent back, which faults were filed twice,
and how long each report took to reach a named person.

covers:
- Record What the Escalation Taught
- Record What the Run Taught
