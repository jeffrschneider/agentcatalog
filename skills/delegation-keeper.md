# delegation-keeper

## draft-a-change

Takes in the reason for a change with the decision behind it, reads the
version in force and how the approvals since it ran against it, drafts
the new lines with the CFO with every change marked, names a stand-in
for every approver, and checks the draft so every kind of decision has
an approver and nobody approves their own work.

covers:
- Check the Draft for Gaps and Clashes
- Draft the Change
- Name a Stand-In for Each Approver
- Read How Approvals Ran
- Read the Version in Force
- Take in the Reason to Change

## publish-the-delegation

Publishes the approved delegation at a version with its effective date,
so every approval asked from that date reads it, and hands each change
that another process carries out to that process's owner.

covers:
- Hand On the Changes Other Processes Make
- Publish the Delegation at a Version

## name-the-approvers

Answers each request for approval in another process with the approvers,
the releasers and the stand-ins the version in force names for that
kind of decision and that amount, leaving out anyone who prepared or
asked for the item.

covers:
- Find the Approvers
- Look Up the Approver
- Name the Approver
- Name the Approver and the Releasers
- Name the Approvers
- Read the Approvers the Delegation Names
- Name Who May Decide

## check-each-approval

Checks each approval against the version in force before it counts, and
holds any approval by someone the version does not name, by an approver
past their limit, or by the person who prepared or asked for the item,
and reads each limit a finance policy names against the delegation.

covers:
- Check Each Approval Counts
- Check Every Limit Against the Delegation
- Check the Approval Counts
- Check the Approval against the Delegation
- Confirm Each Approval Counts
- Check the Decision Counts
