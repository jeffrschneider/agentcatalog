# statistician

## study-briefs

Puts the question, the method, the scope and the dates in front of every
agent that has a part in the work at the same time, so everyone gathers
against the same design.

covers:
- Brief the Audit
- Brief the Makers
- Brief the Study
- Brief the Wave

## study-design

Settles how a study or a read will be done before any data is gathered,
which means the method and why that one, who is sampled or who counts,
what gets asked, the period and the markets covered, and which wording
carries over unchanged from the last time so the two can be compared.

covers:
- Carry the Questions Forward
- Choose the Method
- Design the Buyer Study
- Plan the Study
- Set the Scope of the Read

## data-sizing

Says how much data the work needs before any of it is collected, working
from the size of the difference that would matter, the variation the
measure already carries and the confidence being run to.

covers:
- Say How Many Interviews It Needs
- Say How Much Data It Needs
- Size the Test

## collection-monitoring

Watches the data arrive against what the design asked for and says
whether the work is on track, including whether each version of a test
is serving and its numbers are coming through.

covers:
- Check the Setup
- Watch Both Halves Fill
- Watch the Sample Fill
- Watch the Test Run

## data-fitness

Says which of the data that arrived can be relied on and how far the
figures from it can be read, covering the coverage, the duplicates, the
gaps and the period the data actually spans.

covers:
- Say What the Data Supports
- Say What the Numbers Carry
- Say What the Responses Support

## fieldwork

Puts the questions to people outside the organization and brings back
what they said, including running an earlier reading again later so the
two can be set side by side.

covers:
- Hear from Buyers and the Field
- Read What People Recognize Now
- Read What People Recognize Today
- Try the Names on Outsiders

## figures-and-differences

Works out each figure with the range around it, and says whether a
difference is larger than the noise in the measurement, whether that
difference is between two groups, between this reading and the last one,
or between what was expected and what happened.

covers:
- Compare with the Earlier Waves
- Compare with the Last Read
- Name the Gaps Worth Acting On
- Read the Result
- Test Whether the Segments Hold Apart
- Test that the Groups Hold
- Work out the Figures

## model-validation

Holds a proposed model, stage set or grouping against what actually
happened and reports whether it would have got the real cases right,
which includes testing a suspected drift against data the model has not
seen before anybody rebuilds it.

covers:
- Confirm the Drift
- Test the Candidates against the Record
- Test the Stages against the Record

## method-review

Takes somebody else's figures apart and puts them back together, tracing
each one to its data, redoing the arithmetic and comparing the method
used against the method the work called for.

covers:
- Check the Findings Hold
- Check the Method

## findings-review

Puts a finding in front of the agents that will act on it before it
issues, so anything they cannot use or cannot reconcile is raised while
it can still be changed.

covers:
- Review the Findings

## candidate-comparison

Sets the finalists for a purchase side by side on one sheet, using their
scores, what the trial on real work showed and what each would cost.

covers:
- Compare the Finalists
- Try the Finalists on Real Work

## finding-record

Files the finding with the numbers and the method underneath it, so
somebody else can work it out again later and see whether it still
holds.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
