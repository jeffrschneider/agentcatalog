# case-reviewer

## draw-the-cases

Opens each review period and each calibration round with the rubric
version in force, draws the cases at random at the size the statistician
agent set, with names hidden for a calibration round, and keeps the
cases picked for a reason apart from the random sample.

covers:
- Add the Cases Picked for a Reason
- Draw the Sample
- Draw the Shared Cases
- Open the Calibration Round
- Open the Review Period

## score-cases-against-the-rubric

Scores each case on each part of the rubric with the moment in the case
behind each score and marks each score it is unsure of, scores the
shared cases and the second set alone as one reviewer among the rest,
and scores the part a specialist practises on their next cases.

covers:
- Check the Agreement Held
- Score Each Case Against the Rubric
- Score the Cases Alone
- Score the Practised Part on New Cases

## put-scores-before-a-person

Brings every uncertain score and every failed critical part to a
reviewer, and sends a score the specialist and their team lead question
back for a second reading by a reviewer who did not give it.

covers:
- Check the Scores a Person Must Read
- Send a Questioned Score Back

## raise-what-the-cases-show

Raises the same day any case that shows a missed approval, a skipped
identity check or exposed data, and sends each score that traces to an
article, a saved reply or a routing rule to the owner of the fix.

covers:
- Raise What Cannot Wait
- Send Desk Faults to Their Owners

## keep-results-with-their-owners

Sends each specialist's results only to that specialist and their team
lead, places their reviewed cases ready for coaching in the record only
the two can open, and sends each reviewer their own calibration scores
beside the agreed reading.

covers:
- Hand the Reviewed Cases to Coaching
- Prepare the Reviewed Cases
- Send Each Reviewer Their Own Scores
- Send Each Specialist Their Results

## calibrate-the-reading

Sets out each part where the reviewers' scores split with the moment
each reviewer cited, takes part in the calibration session as one
reviewer, and records each agreed reading with its worked example at the
rubric version.

covers:
- Hold the Calibration Session
- Record the Agreed Readings
- Set Out Each Split

## record-what-reviews-taught

Records the sample and how far it filled, the time spent, the disputes
and which scores changed, the agreement on each part before and after a
round, and what the next period or round does differently.

covers:
- Record What the Review Taught
- Record What the Round Taught
