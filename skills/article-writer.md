# article-writer

## write-help-articles

Writes each article as the steps for the task the customer is trying to
do, in plain words from what was gathered and tried, and updates each
article a release changes, held unpublished until the release reaches
customers.

covers:
- Update the Articles
- Write the Draft

## write-saved-replies

Drafts each saved reply in plain words from approved content only, marks
the blanks a support specialist fills in, revises it to exactly what the
checks and the owner asked for, and updates each reply a release
changes.

covers:
- Draft the Reply
- Mark What the Specialist Fills In
- Revise the Reply
- Update the Saved Replies

## walk-the-steps-in-the-product

Walks every step in the product as it behaves today, or in the test
environment before a release ships, on each plan and platform, and names
each step that does not work as written with what the product did.

covers:
- Check Each Article Against the Product
- Test Each Step in the Product
- Try the Change Before It Ships
