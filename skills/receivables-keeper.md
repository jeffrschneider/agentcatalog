# receivables-keeper

## apply-the-days-receipts

Takes in each receipt on the bank's report with its remittance,
identifies the customer that paid, matches each receipt to its invoices,
applies the matched ones, holds aside anything short, over or
unidentified, and agrees the day to the bank's total.

covers:
- Account for Every Receipt
- Apply the Matched Receipts
- Gather the Remittance Advice
- Hold Aside What Does Not Match
- Identify the Customer That Paid
- Match Each Receipt to Its Invoices
- Take In the Day's Receipts

## settle-a-held-receipt

Reads the customer's answer about a held receipt, and applies the
receipt, keeps it on account or passes it for refund exactly as the
named person decided.

covers:
- Apply What Was Decided
- Read the Customer's Answer

## keep-each-customers-account

Keeps each customer's account showing every open invoice, its age and
anything held or in dispute, confirms that an overdue invoice is still
owed, checks each promise to pay against the receipts, and records the
outcome of each follow-up.

covers:
- Check Each Promise Was Kept
- Confirm the Invoice Is Still Owed
- Record the Outcome
- Take In the Overdue Invoice
- Update Each Customer's Account

## tell-treasury-what-is-expected

Gives the treasury-analyst agent what customers owe with the week each
payment is expected, the receipts expected each day and each payment a
remittance announced, and matches each day's bank movements to them.

covers:
- Gather What Customers Owe
- List the Receipts Expected Today
- Match Each Movement to What Was Expected
- Tell Treasury What Is Expected

## set-out-a-write-off

Takes in a write-off proposal, confirms the amount still owed on each
invoice, lists the security and other routes, and sets out the proposal
with everything tried and the choices open.

covers:
- Confirm the Amount Still Owed
- List the Security and Other Routes
- Set Out the Write-Off Proposal
- Take In the Proposal

## record-a-write-off

Records each approved write-off for exactly the amount approved against
the allowance, passes the tax and the period's write-offs on, and keeps
the account open so that a later payment is applied as a recovery.

covers:
- Keep the Account Open for Recovery
- Pass the Write-Off to Tax and the Close
- Record the Write-Off with Its Approval

## record-what-receivables-taught

Records which receipts failed to match and why, what each customer
should send next time, and which warning signs came before a debt was
written off.

covers:
- Record What the Debt Taught
- Record What the Run Taught
