# comms-manager

## take-in-what-has-to-be-said

The agent records what happened, who it affects, why it matters and when
it can be said, and opens the run that will answer for it.

covers:
- Take in the News
- Take in the Release
- Take in the Incident
- Take in the Story Angle
- Hand the Result On

## confirm-the-facts

The agent establishes what actually happened from the people who know,
and records who established each fact and when.

covers:
- Confirm the Facts

## judge-how-far-it-reaches

The agent works out how big this is: who is affected, how far it will
travel, whether anyone outside would care, and what treatment it earns.

covers:
- Size up the Exposure
- Size the Announcement
- Test the Angle

## settle-what-the-organization-will-say

The agent puts the options in front of the people who decide and records
the single line the organization will take, so everyone says the same
thing for the same reason.

covers:
- Settle the Story
- Settle the Explanation
- Decide the Position

## set-the-date-and-the-order

The agent sets the date, any embargo, and the order in which each
audience hears it, and holds that timeline at a version.

covers:
- Set the Announcement Date
- Set the Timing

## keep-every-audience-version-saying-the-same-thing

The agent holds the press, customer, staff and partner wording at a
version each, and when the facts change it puts every version back
through at a new version so none of them contradicts another.

covers:
- Draft the Statement
- Write the Announcement
- Write the Other Versions
- Update the Response

## get-the-quotes-approved

The agent settles who is quoted and gets each of them to approve the
words that will appear under their name.

covers:
- Gather the Quotes

## produce-the-material-that-goes-with-the-story

The agent puts together the pitch note, the images and the facts a
reporter needs to write the story without asking.

covers:
- Produce the Pitch Materials

## choose-the-outlets-the-story-fits

The agent works out which outlets and writers this particular story is
for, and records why each one was chosen.

covers:
- Choose the Reporters

## get-the-people-who-speak-ready

The agent names who may speak and what they may say on record, works
through the questions they will be asked, and makes sure everyone who
might be approached has the same answers.

covers:
- Decide Who Speaks
- Brief the Speakers
- Prepare the Spokesperson

## send-it-out-on-the-date

Once the sign-offs are in and a person has said go, the agent releases
each version on its channel in the order the timeline set, so staff and
customers hear it before the press does.

covers:
- Issue the Release
- Issue the Response
- Issue a Holding Line
- Post It Publicly
- Say It in Public
- Notify the Staff
- Change the Public Price

## log-what-ran

The agent records every piece that ran in response, what it said, and
where it appeared.

covers:
- Log the Coverage

## close-the-run-and-report-on-it

When a person says the run is over, the agent writes the account of what
happened against what the announcement or the response was meant to do,
including what was known at each point and what to do differently.

covers:
- Close the Response
- Review the Response
- Report on the Announcement
- Record What Was Learned

## run-the-request-and-answer-round

The agent runs a round where every function submits what it is asking
for in one format by one date, and then tells each function what it was
given and the rules that come with it.

covers:
- Collect What Each Function Asks For
- Tell Every Function What It Has
