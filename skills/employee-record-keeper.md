# employee-record-keeper

## create-the-record

Creates one record from the signed offer after checking for an old
record of the same person, files the new hire's forms, keeps the right
to work documents a person examined in the restricted store, and passes
the pay, the tax details and the benefits eligibility on.

covers:
- Check the Right to Work
- Collect the Forms
- Create the Employee Record
- Set Up Payroll
- Start the Benefits Enrollment

## apply-approved-changes

Checks each change for the asker's authority and its evidence, finds
what else it touches, and applies it on its effective date with the old
value kept, taking every decision about a person from the record of the
named person who made it. It tells the employee what changed and closes
the change on the record.

covers:
- Apply the Change on Its Date
- Apply the Change to the Record
- Check the Authority to Ask
- Check the Evidence
- Close the Change on the Record
- Find What Else the Change Touches
- Record the Change on One Date
- Take in the Change
- Tell the Employee What Changed

## pass-changes-on

Passes every approved change to payroll before its cut-off, to benefits
and to access, gathers the changes each pay run needs, and reads each
system back field by field against the record.

covers:
- Confirm Each System Shows the Change
- Gather the Approved Changes
- Pass the Change to Access
- Pass the Change to Benefits
- Pass the Change to Payroll

## track-probation

Confirms the date a probation closes and the steps the contract sets,
records the outcome the manager decided with who decided it and when,
and hands an ended employment to the offboarding.

covers:
- Confirm the Probation Terms
- Hand an Ended Employment to Offboarding
- Record the Outcome

## answer-records-requests

Logs each request, confirms the requester's identity against the
record, finds every record it covers, asks the named holders of
restricted records to search their own, and sets aside other people's
details. It makes each correction and deletion the privacy lead grants,
prepares and sends the approved answer, and records the request.

covers:
- Ask the Holders of Restricted Records
- Confirm the Requester's Identity
- Correct the Record
- Delete What the Rules Allow
- Find Every Record It Covers
- Log the Request
- Prepare the Answer
- Record the Request
- Send the Answer
- Set Aside Other People's Details

## pull-records-for-an-audit

Stops the retention schedule from deleting any record a hold covers,
pulls only the records and fields each audit request covers, and holds
back other people's details and any restricted record the request does
not reach.

covers:
- Hold Back What the Request Does Not Cover
- Pull the Records Asked For
- Put a Hold on the Records

## guard-the-restricted-records

Lists everyone who can open the health, investigation and background
check records, checks each one against the named people allowed, and
reads the log of who opened them without reading what the records say.

covers:
- Check Each Against the Named People
- List Who Can Open Restricted Records
- Read Who Opened Restricted Records
