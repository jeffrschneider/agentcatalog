# credit-analyst

## assess-the-customer

Takes in each request, identifies the legal entity that will be billed
with any parent or guarantor, reads how it has paid the company before,
and reads the risk against the credit policy to a rating with the rule
behind each part.

covers:
- Identify the Entity That Will Be Billed
- Read How the Customer Has Paid
- Read the Risk Against the Credit Policy
- Take In the Request

## propose-a-limit

Proposes a limit, payment terms and any security the policy asks for,
with the evidence behind each figure beside it, and records the account
owner's view beside the proposal.

covers:
- Hear the Account Owner
- Propose a Limit and Terms

## keep-the-customer-under-review

Sets the next review date and the warning signs to watch, sets the
follow-up level for collections and gives it when an invoice is overdue,
reviews the customer's credit when a promise is broken or a debt grows,
and records what each review taught.

covers:
- Read the Follow-Up Level
- Record What the Review Taught
- Review the Customer's Credit
- Set the Follow-Up Level for Collections
- Set the Review Date
