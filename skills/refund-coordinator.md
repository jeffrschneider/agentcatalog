# refund-coordinator

## take-in-a-request-for-money

Takes in each request for a refund or a goodwill credit with who asked,
the account, the case and the customer's own words, and records whoever
asked for a credit as one of its proposers.

covers:
- Take In the Refund Request
- Take In the Request for a Credit

## read-what-happened-and-what-was-paid

Reads the order, the invoice and how the customer paid, sets out the
failure behind a credit with its dates and what the customer bore, and
reads the refunds, credits and goodwill the account already received and
any credit the agreement already owes.

covers:
- Read What the Agreement Already Owes
- Read What the Customer Already Received
- Read the Order and the Payment
- Set Out What Happened

## work-out-what-is-owed

Sets each request against the refund policy and any right a local rule
gives, and reads each agreement against the downtime measured after an
outage for the credit it owes.

covers:
- Set the Request Against the Policy
- Work Out the Credits Owed

## hear-the-people-who-know-the-account

Convenes the support specialist who holds the case, or the account
owner, to say what they know, and counts anyone who recommends an amount
among those who proposed it.

covers:
- Hear the Account Owner
- Hear the Support Specialist

## propose-an-amount

Proposes each refund or credit at a version with the evidence, the rule
behind it, what the customer already received and everyone who proposed
it, for a named person who proposed none of it to decide.

covers:
- Propose the Credit
- Propose the Credits
- Propose the Refund

## pass-decisions-to-finance

Passes each decided refund to Finance's payment run as an approved item,
to be paid back the way the customer paid, and each decided outage
credit to Finance to issue the credit note and book it.

covers:
- Pass the Decided Credits to Finance
- Pass the Refund to Finance's Payment Run

## record-each-decision

Records who asked, who proposed, who decided, the version of the
delegation, the credit note and the reply as sent, and sends the causes
behind refunds and credits to the people who own them.

covers:
- Record What the Credit Taught
- Record What the Request Taught
- Record the Credit
- Record the Decision
