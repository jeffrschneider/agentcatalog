# treasury-analyst

## build-the-daily-cash-position

Gathers every bank's balances and movements each morning, chases a
report that has not arrived, matches each movement to the receipts,
payments and transfers that were expected, holds aside anything nobody
expected, and builds one position by account, entity and currency that
it sets beside the minimum and the forecast and sends to the treasurer.
It also checks each supplier payment run against the cash that must
cover it, and records the released payments in the position.

covers:
- Build the Cash Position
- Chase a Missing Bank Report
- Check the Cash to Cover the Run
- Compare with the Minimum and the Forecast
- Gather the Bank Balances
- Hold Aside Anything Unexpected
- List the Payments Due Today
- Match Each Movement to What Was Expected
- Read the Bank Movements
- Record What the Day Taught
- Record the Day's Position
- Send the Position to the Treasurer
- Update the Cash Position

## forecast-cash

Opens each week's forecast from the last version, adds the paydays, the
tax and the debt service, builds cash by week and then by month for each
entity and currency, explains every change from last week, sets last
week's forecast beside what happened, tests each week against the
minimum and the room in each facility, sets out the options for a
shortfall, and publishes the forecast once it is reviewed.

covers:
- Add Payroll, Tax and Debt
- Build the Forecast
- Compare Last Week with What Happened
- Explain the Change from Last Week
- Open the Week's Forecast
- Publish the Forecast
- Record What the Forecast Taught
- Set Out the Options for a Shortfall
- Test Against the Minimum and the Facilities

## prepare-transfers-and-fundings

Takes in the need for cash or the approved register, works out the
amount each account or each payroll funding needs by the bank's cutoff,
checks that the account the money comes from holds it, sets out the
placements the investment policy allows for surplus cash, and prepares
each transfer for the named people who approve and release it, telling
payroll once the funding has landed.

covers:
- Check the Cash Is There
- Confirm the Total and the Payday
- Prepare the Funding Transfer
- Prepare the Transfer
- Read the Payroll Account
- Set Out the Placements Allowed
- Take In the Need
- Tell Payroll the Account Is Funded
- Work Out the Amount
- Work Out the Funding

## record-money-that-moved

Matches each placement to the bank's confirmation and each payroll
funding to the payments that left, explains what is left in the payroll
account, records each transfer and funding with the approval and the
release behind it for the ledger, records a transfer between two
entities as a balance each owes the other, and records what the run
taught.

covers:
- Explain What Is Left in the Account
- Match the Funding to the Payments
- Match the Placement Confirmation
- Record What the Run Taught
- Record a Balance Between Entities
- Record the Funding
- Record the Transfer

## keep-bank-accounts-and-signers

Takes in each request to open or close a bank account or change a
signer, has a leaver's portal access ended on the last day, reads the
account as it stands, checks that dual control still holds, moves the
payments and the balance of a closing account under their own
approvals, fills in the bank's forms, gathers the bank's confirmation
for the treasurer, tells the people who use a new account, and keeps the
bank account record matched to the bank's own list.

covers:
- Check Dual Control Still Holds
- Check the Bank's List Against the Record
- Confirm the Change with the Bank
- End a Leaver's Access on the Last Day
- Fill In the Bank's Forms
- Move the Balance of a Closing Account
- Read the Account as It Stands
- Record What the Change Taught
- Redirect a Closing Account
- Take In the Request
- Tell the People Who Use a New Account
- Update the Bank Account Record

## test-the-covenants

Opens each covenant test with every facility and report that falls due,
reads each term as the agreement and every amendment define it, works
out each financial covenant with every adjustment shown, checks the
other undertakings, reads the projection forward, raises a breach at
risk to the CFO, drafts the compliance certificate on the lender's form,
and records the test with the lenders' acknowledgment.

covers:
- Check the Other Undertakings
- Draft the Compliance Certificate
- Open the Test
- Project the Covenants Forward
- Raise a Breach at Risk
- Read Each Covenant's Definitions
- Record What the Test Taught
- Record the Test
- Work Out Each Financial Covenant
