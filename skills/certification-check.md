# certification-check

## settle-what-a-sitting-must-prove

Takes in who is put forward, for which job and by when, names the parts
of the job a customer would notice, and checks the assessment item by
item against the standard it claims to test. An item that tests
something the standard does not name is changed rather than scored.

covers:
- Check the Assessment Is Fair
- Name What Must Be Proved
- Take in the Request to Certify

## put-a-sitting-in-front-of-fair-assessors

Picks assessors who are not scoring their own people, briefs all of
them on the same standard, the same scoring sheet and the same worked
examples at once, and sets the date, the order and what the person may
bring in with them.

covers:
- Brief the Assessors
- Name the Assessors
- Schedule the Sitting

## run-the-sitting

Runs the sitting itself: the written exam against the question set at
its version, the product, pricing and market questions answered cold,
and the practical watched while it is done, so somebody is checked
before a customer is the one finding out.

covers:
- Assess the Practical
- Prove the Parts that Matter
- Run the Knowledge Check
- Sit the Written Exam

## score-against-a-written-pass-line

Scores every part against the pass line as it was written before use,
and keeps the evidence beside each score with the assessor who gave it,
so a disputed score can be reread rather than re-argued.

covers:
- Score Against the Standard

## carry-the-result-to-a-person-who-signs-it

Puts the result in front of the named signer as a pass, a conditional
pass, a retake or a fail, and holds that room open rather than deciding
in it. The same signature is what says a partner needs no escort on the
next deal. This agent never grants a pass itself.

covers:
- Decide Pass or Fail
- Decide Pass or Retake
- Decide They Can Do It Unaided

## record-and-issue-what-was-passed

Records the result with the standard version, the assessors, the
evidence and the expiry date, and issues the certificate with its issue
date and its expiry where both sides can see it.

covers:
- Issue the Certificates
- Record the Certification

## check-a-change-reached-the-people-it-names

Calls the roll against the list of who must be told, and runs the short
check that the change is actually understood by the people who sat
through it.

covers:
- Check That It Landed
- Check Who Attended

## report-what-the-standard-predicted

Reports months later whether the people it passed went on to do the
job, because a standard that passes people who cannot sell is not a
gate.

covers:
- Report What Certification Predicted
