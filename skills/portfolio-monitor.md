# portfolio-monitor

## read-the-funds-rights

Reads the executed agreements for what each company owes the fund and
what the fund may do: information rights, loan reporting terms,
consent rights, director rights, preferences and the reserve left.

covers:
- Check the Fund's Consent Rights
- List the Fund's Rights and Duties
- Read the Fund's Rights and Limits
- Read What Each Company Owes

## collect-the-company-reports

Sets up and runs each company's reporting on one template. It agrees
the template, sends it with its due date, takes in packages and board
packs as they were sent, chases a late one once and then with the deal
lead, checks each is complete, and records what arrived late or wrong.

covers:
- Agree the Reporting Template
- Chase a Late Report
- Check the Package Is Complete
- Collect the Board Pack
- Confirm the First Report Arrived
- Open the Reporting Period
- Record What Arrived Late or Wrong
- Send the Template and the Due Date
- Set Up the First Month's Reporting
- Take in the Meeting and the Agenda
- Take in What Each Company Sends

## read-against-plan

Reads results and operating measures against the budget, the value
creation plan, the prior period and the covenants, confirms the facts
with the company's finance lead, and works out how many weeks of cash
are left.

covers:
- Confirm the Facts with Management
- Read Each Company against Its Plan
- Read the Numbers Against Plan
- Read the Operating Measures
- Read the Results against Plan
- Work Out the Cash Runway

## keep-the-watch-list

Raises a warning the day it is found, ranks every company on the watch
list scale, hands a company that needs it to its own process, watches a
company in trouble every week until it comes off the list, and records
what warned first and how early.

covers:
- Hand On the Watch List and Reserves
- Raise a Warning the Same Day
- Raise the Warning
- Rank the Companies by Risk
- Record What Was Learned
- Start the Work a Company Needs
- Take the Company off the Watch List
- Update the Plan and the Watch List
- Watch the Company Every Week

## brief-the-director

Lists each decision a board is asked for and what it commits the
company to, helps write the director's brief and the questions sent
ahead, checks the minutes against what was decided, and reports to the
partners.

covers:
- Check the Minutes Against the Record
- List the Decisions the Board Is Asked For
- Report to the Partners
- Send Questions to Management Ahead
- Write the Director's Brief

## hand-on-the-reading

Writes one page per company with every figure traced to its line, has
the deal lead confirm or correct it, and hands the period's reports and
each company's condition to the valuation and the portfolio review.

covers:
- Confirm the Reading with the Deal Lead
- Gather Each Company's Condition
- Hand the Reports to the Valuation
- Hear the Deal Teams
- Record What the Quarter Taught
- Take in the Company Reports
- Write the Company Summary
