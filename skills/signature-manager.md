# signature-manager

## set-the-signature-path

Takes the agreed text from the people who settled the words and
confirms who signs on each side, in what order, on which document and
under what authority. Nothing goes out to sign until the path is
settled.

covers:
- Confirm the Signature Path
- Hand the Text to Signature
- Confirm Who Signs and in What Order
- Open the Fund's Bank Account

## control-what-is-out-to-sign

Sends the right version to the named signers in the agreed order, and
takes a document back out of signature when it should no longer be out
there, such as when the deal is lost. The signing itself stays a human
step.

covers:
- Execute the Signature
- Stop the Open Work
- Send the Subscription Documents
- Choose and Engage the Providers
- Sign the Formation Documents
- Sign the Close
- Sign the Confidentiality Agreement
- Sign and Send the Indication
- Sign the Closing Documents
- Sign the Adviser's Engagement
- Sign the Sale Agreement
- Get the Offer Signed
- Sign the Agreement

## check-the-executed-document

Takes the executed copy in with every exhibit it names, and confirms
who signed on each side, under what authority, and on what date. An
exhibit that is not there is named as missing rather than assumed.

covers:
- Confirm the Signature and the Signer
- Take in the Executed Agreement
- Collect the Executed Set
- File the Close Record
- File the Executed Agreement

## name-the-parties-and-the-terms-off-the-standard-paper

Matches the buying entity and the selling entity to the entity list the
organization holds rather than to the address block on the paper. Names
every term that differs from the standard paper, with the person who
accepted it and the date.

covers:
- Identify the Buying Entity
- Settle the Non-Standard Terms

## mark-which-promises-the-paper-carries

Reads the promises made during the sale against the executed text and
marks each one carried by the agreement, carried by a side letter, or
said and not binding. Every binding one names the clause behind it.

covers:
- Separate the Contract from the Conversation

## close-the-signature-out

Puts one record in front of everybody who acts on it at the same time,
so nobody works from an older version of what was signed. Writes down
what had to be corrected between signature and booking, and which of it
belongs in the next version of the paper.

covers:
- Record What Had to Be Fixed
- Tell the People Who Need to Know
- Confirm the Close to Every Investor

## hold-the-firm-to-its-confidentiality-agreements

Reads a confidentiality agreement beside the legal reviewer before
anyone signs it, and records its non-solicit, its standstill, its term
and the people it lets see the material where every deal team can read
them. In a sale it makes sure each buyer's agreement is signed before
the information memorandum goes to that buyer, and when the firm
withdraws from a process it tells the other side in writing and records
the return or destruction of the material the agreement covers.

covers:
- Read the Confidentiality Agreement
- Approach the Buyers
- Withdraw in Writing
