# knowledge-keeper

## find-the-approved-answer

Matches each routine question to the article or approved answer that
answers it, confirms its owner's approval, its version, its review date
and its place on the list, and records a question no approved content
answers as a gap.

covers:
- Confirm the Content Is Approved and Current
- Find the Approved Answer
- Record the Gap

## plan-new-content

Takes in each request for an article or a saved reply, reads the replies
already saved on the same question, finds the approved content a reply
will rest on, and plans each article from the customers' own words with
its readers, its plans and its owner.

covers:
- Find the Approved Content Behind It
- Plan the Article
- Read the Replies Already Saved
- Take In the Request
- Take In the Request for a Reply

## publish-approved-content

Publishes each article and saved reply its owner approved at its version
with its review date, marks who may send each reply and which replies go
through approval each time they are used, tells the team what changed,
and records the publication.

covers:
- Mark It for Approval Each Time It Is Used
- Mark Who May Send It
- Publish at a Version
- Publish the Reply at a Version
- Record the Publication
- Tell the Team What Changed

## keep-the-approved-list

Keeps the list of content the agents may answer from, adds each version
once it is published as approved, and takes off any content found wrong
or out of date the moment it is found, so that the list holds only
versions an owner kept or approved.

covers:
- Change the Approved Content
- Correct an Answer Found Wrong
- Mark It as Approved Content
- Take Wrong Content Off the Approved List

## retire-old-content

Takes down each article or saved reply that has been replaced or
retired, sends its address to the closest article, moves every link to
it, and keeps the old version with the dates it was in use.

covers:
- Retire the Version It Replaces
- Take Down Retired Articles
- Take Down What It Replaces

## review-the-articles

Opens each review with the articles due and their owners, reads the
cases that cite each one, sets out the findings with a proposal for each
owner, carries out each decision by setting the next review date or
opening a rewrite, and reports the review.

covers:
- Open a Rewrite
- Open the Review
- Read the Cases That Cite It
- Report the Review
- Set Out the Findings
- Set the Next Review Date

## find-the-gaps

Collects each period's questions the agents could not answer, groups
every source into one gap for each topic, ranks the gaps by the
customers each touches, starts an article for each gap the knowledge
lead chooses, passes on what an article cannot fix, and publishes the
ranked list.

covers:
- Collect the Questions the Agents Could Not Answer
- Group the Gaps by Topic
- Open the Period
- Pass On What an Article Cannot Fix
- Publish the Ranked List
- Rank the Gaps
- Start an Article for Each Chosen Gap

## prepare-content-for-a-release

Takes in what a release changes and Marketing's briefing, lists every
article and saved reply it touches, checks what is ready before the
date, switches the content live as the release reaches each account, and
watches the first days for questions no article answers.

covers:
- Check What Is Ready by the Date
- Find What the Release Touches
- Switch the Content Live
- Take In the Marketing Briefing
- Take In the Release
- Watch the First Days

## record-what-the-help-center-taught

Records what each article, review, gap period and release taught: the
steps that failed, what went out of date and how it was found, which
sources found which gaps, and what was late or missed.

covers:
- Record What the Period Taught
- Record What the Release Taught
- Record What the Review Taught
- Record What the Run Taught
