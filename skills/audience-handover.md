# audience-handover

## hear-the-request-at-the-briefing

The agent sits in the briefing that plans a transfer and says which
fields it will be allowed to send and to which systems, while the plan
can still change.

covers:
- Brief the Build

## settle-what-may-be-sent

The agent settles who gets which records, which fields go with them and
on what basis, reading the agreement covering the transfer and the
consent record together.

covers:
- Settle the Lead Terms

## set-up-the-receiving-systems

The agent sets up the receiving side before anything is sent: the
records and tiers held at the other end, the tracking, the form fields,
and where each record has to land.

covers:
- Set up the Partner Systems
- Wire up the Split

## deliver-the-list

The agent applies the consent record and the field limits to the
approved list, formats it for the receiving system, sends it, and gets a
receipt back saying what was accepted and what was rejected.

covers:
- Hand over the Selling Kit
- Hand the List to the Platforms
- Load the Audiences the Plan Names
- Set up the Paid Buy
- Split and Hand over the Leads

## reconcile-the-counts-after-a-transfer

The agent sets what it sent against what the receiving system recorded,
reports how much of the list matched at the other end, and matches both
sides' counts and costs into one set of figures.

covers:
- Agree the Joint Numbers
- Confirm the Match

## set-the-refresh-schedule

The agent sets how often the list is rebuilt and delivered again, and
records the date the next transfer is due.

covers:
- Set the Refresh Schedule
