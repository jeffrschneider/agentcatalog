# workforce-planner

## build-the-yearly-plan

Opens the year's plan, takes in the business plan and each department
head's requests, names the skills the year calls for, drafts each team's
headcount at a level with its start dates, builds the options, publishes
the chosen plan at a version and briefs the department heads on what
each team has.

covers:
- Ask Each Department What It Needs
- Brief the Department Heads
- Build the Options
- Draft the Headcount by Team
- Name the Skills the Year Calls For
- Open the Year's Plan
- Publish the Plan
- Record What the Plan Rests On
- Take in the Business Plan

## cost-against-the-budget

Works out the labor cost of a plan, a position or an option from the pay
range for each level, with benefits and the employer's taxes on pay, and
sets it beside the budget for the finance lead to check. It supplies
each team's headcount, start dates and labor cost from the approved
plan at its version to the company budget.

covers:
- Take in the Labor Cost
- Check the Budget
- Check the Plan against the Budget
- Cost Each Option
- Cost the Plan
- Cost the Position
- Model the Options
- Write Down the Business Reason

## check-position-requests

Takes in a manager's request for a new or replacement position, checks
it is complete and that the plan holds it, supports the department
head's trade for an unplanned position, records the signed position on
the plan, and hands it to the recruiter.

covers:
- Check the Request Is Complete
- Check the Request against the Plan
- Close the Request
- Hand the Position to Hiring
- Propose a Trade for an Unplanned Position
- Record the Position on the Plan
- Take in the Request
- Tell the Hiring Manager

## model-structure-changes

Draws the structure as it stands and the options for a reorganization,
lists the effect of each option on every role, lists the roles in each
selection pool of a layoff with no names attached, and finds the open
positions a selected person could take.

covers:
- Draw the Current Structure
- Draw the Options
- List the Effect on Each Role
- List the Roles Affected
- Look for Other Roles

## read-the-plan-against-the-year

Takes in the plan for a reporting period, sets each figure beside it
with the gap, hands the gaps the plan cannot absorb to its owner, lists
the key roles for a succession review, and adds the hiring needs that
review finds.

covers:
- Add Hiring Needs to the Workforce Plan
- Hand the Gaps to the Plan
- List the Key Roles
- Set the Figures against the Plan
- Take in the Plan for the Period
