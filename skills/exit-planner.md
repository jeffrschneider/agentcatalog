# exit-planner

## read-what-the-fund-can-do

Writes down the reason to exit and reads what the fund holds and can
force, the fund's term and its extensions, and, after a listing, the
lock-up, the trading windows, the holder limits and the filings each
sale triggers.

covers:
- Read the Fund's Term and Timing
- Read the Lock-Up and the Holder Rules
- Read What the Fund Holds and Can Force
- Take in the Reason to Exit

## plan-the-route

Lists every route open to the fund and the buyers who would pay, names
what a buyer's diligence would find first and plans the work to fix it,
settles management's terms with the deal lead, and writes the exit
paper the committee decides on before handing the approved plan on.

covers:
- Find What Would Lower the Price
- Hand the Plan to the Process That Runs It
- List the Likely Buyers
- List the Routes Open to the Fund
- Plan the Work before Marketing
- Settle Management's Terms for the Exit
- Write the Exit Paper

## run-the-sale

Takes in the approved plan, prepares and checks the company's
information, opens the data room buyer by buyer, helps choose the
second round, answers every buyer's diligence on the same terms, and
works each condition to close.

covers:
- Answer the Buyers' Diligence
- Choose Who Goes to the Second Round
- Meet the Conditions to Close
- Open the Data Room
- Prepare the Company's Information
- Take in the Approved Exit Plan

## sell-down-a-listed-position

Sets the sell-down plan and the choice between a sale and a
distribution with the partners, chooses the broker or the block bank,
follows each execution against its signed instruction, files what the
rules require, and tracks what is left to sell.

covers:
- Choose Between a Sale and a Distribution
- Choose the Broker or the Block Bank
- Execute the Sale or the Transfer
- File What the Rules Require
- Set the Sell-Down Plan
- Track What Is Left to Sell

## record-the-result

Records each exit's price and return against the plan, every amount
held back with its release date, and what the sale or the sell-down
taught.

covers:
- Record the Result
- Record the Result against the Plan
