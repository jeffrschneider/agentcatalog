# training-coordinator

## assign-training-by-role

Takes in each requirement as the learning lead confirms it, reads who
it covers by role and location alone, counts what falls due in the
year, and assigns each person the course at its signed version with a
due date. It records an exemption only on a current completion of the
same version or a named person's written grant.

covers:
- Assign the First Required Training
- Assign the Training
- Assign the Training It Needs
- Count the Required Training
- Read Who It Applies To
- Record Exemptions
- Take In the Requirement

## run-the-sessions

Books people onto live sessions in working time, puts the year's
sessions on the calendar, runs a new hire's first required courses
with the trainer, and takes attendance at each part of orientation.

covers:
- Book the Live Sessions
- Run the First Required Training
- Schedule the Sessions
- Take Attendance

## chase-completion

Reminds each person before the due date, chases anyone overdue, first
the person and then the manager, chases what a new hire still has open
beside the policy-keeper agent, and raises anyone who stays overdue to
the department head, who decides what follows.

covers:
- Chase Overdue Training
- Chase What Is Missing
- Raise What Stays Overdue
- Remind Before the Due Date

## keep-the-training-record

Records each completion with the person, the course version, the date
and any result, reports completion by team and location with each
manager seeing their own team alone, keeps the record ready for an
auditor, and adds what each round showed to the debrief.

covers:
- Keep the Record for Audit
- Record Each Completion
- Record What the Round Taught
- Report Completion
