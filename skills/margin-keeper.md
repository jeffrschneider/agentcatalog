# margin-keeper

## price-a-tier-set

Says what each tier costs the company before anybody is told they hold
it: the margin, the discount, the funds and the support hours behind
the promise. It then sets each tier's discount against the margin floor
at the volume that tier assumes and names the shortfall where there is
one.

covers:
- Check the Margin Holds
- Price What Each Tier Costs

## put-the-priced-terms-into-the-systems

Writes the approved numbers into the price lists and the portal on the
date the round says they take effect, so a partner gets the terms it
was told it has.

covers:
- Change the Systems

## check-what-the-company-owed

Reads the other direction in a performance round, setting what the
company promised each partner against what it actually delivered, and
marking each difference settled or open.

covers:
- Check What the Company Owed

## settle-the-last-accounts

Closes the money out when a relationship ends. It works the settlement
line by line, saying what was earned, what was paid, what is recovered
and what is still open, and puts the amounts to a person to pay and to
recover.

covers:
- Pay What Is Owed and Recover What Is Not
- Settle What the Money Says
