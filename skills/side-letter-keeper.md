# side-letter-keeper

## turn-each-letter-into-obligations

Helps counsel draft the side letter with every clause tied to its closed
ask, records each promise as an obligation with its owner, what sets it
off and its due date, and loads each agreed letter at the close so its
obligations are in force from the signing.

covers:
- Draft the Side Letter
- Load the Side Letters to Be Signed
- Record Each Promise as an Obligation

## run-the-most-favored-nation-elections

Reads which investors already hold which terms and whose most favored
nation right would reach each new ask, marks the terms others may elect
and those the exclusions keep out, and after the final close sends each
holder its terms, collects its elections and records each one as an
obligation.

covers:
- Check What Other Investors Already Hold
- Mark the Terms Others May Elect
- Record the Elections as Obligations
- Run the Side Letter Elections

## apply-the-letters-to-the-fees

Applies each investor's fee discounts, early-closer rates and waivers,
works out the share of portfolio company fees that reduces the
management fee, and tests every charge against the caps in the
agreement and each letter with the running total for the year.

covers:
- Apply the Side Letter Fee Terms
- Check the Charges against the Caps
- Work Out the Fee Offsets

## apply-the-letters-to-a-call

Takes out every investor the agreement or its side letter excuses from
the investment, with the clause that excuses it, and applies the
notice period, caps, currency and delivery each investor was promised.

covers:
- Apply the Side Letter Terms
- Take Out the Excused Investors

## check-the-reporting-against-the-promises

Reads what each investor was promised on content, template, currency,
timing and extra reports, and checks each statement and its package
against those promises before it is published, naming any missing item
with its clause.

covers:
- Check Each Statement against Its Side Letter
- Read the Reporting Promises
