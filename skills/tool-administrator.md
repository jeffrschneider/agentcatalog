# tool-administrator

## take-the-tool-on-and-hold-its-configuration

Takes the tool on with its owner, its licence, its term and the process
it runs, and holds the configuration at a version, so every change can
be seen, dated and taken back.

covers:
- Take On the Tool
- Hold the Configuration at a Version

## hold-who-may-get-in

Sets the roles and what each one may do, opens an account against a job
and closes it against a leaving record, and reads every account again on
its cadence. A leaver who kept an account and an account nobody can
account for both go to the security reviewer.

covers:
- Set the Roles and the Permissions
- Open and Close Accounts
- Read Every Account Again

## give-a-link-its-own-access

Gives a link its own credentials at the reach the agreed flows need and
no wider, switches it on, and takes the running link over with the flow
record and the date it is next checked.

covers:
- Set the Access the Link Is Given
- Switch It On
- Hand It to the Administrator

## work-a-change-through-off-live-first

Takes in what people ask for and what the supplier has changed, tries it
away from live, and applies it with the way back kept. A request that is
not what the tool is for is refused in writing with the process it
belongs to named.

covers:
- Take in Change Requests
- Take in Supplier Changes
- Try the Change away from Live
- Apply the Change

## set-the-sellers-paid-for-against-the-sellers-used

Reads the sellers held against the sellers actually used, names every one
unused past the limit, and takes more licences used than are held to
whoever owns the budget, who says in writing whether licences are bought or
accounts are closed.

covers:
- Keep the Licence Count Right

## bring-the-tool-to-its-standing-review

Brings the use, the cost, the findings and the supplier changes to the
review that asks whether the tool still earns its place, and records the
decision with its date, its evidence and the date the next review falls.

covers:
- Run the Standing Review
- Record the Review

## wind-the-tool-down

Tells the people who use it, stops new work going in, takes the export
while the tool still runs, sees the process to its new home, stops the
links, closes the accounts and the licence on the notice the agreement
requires, and files what the retirement taught.

covers:
- Tell the People Who Use It
- Stop New Work Going In
- Take the Export
- Move the Process to Its New Home
- Turn Off the Links
- Close the Accounts and the Licence
- Record What Was Learned
