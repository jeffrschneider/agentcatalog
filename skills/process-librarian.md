# process-librarian

## open-a-run-and-fix-what-it-covers

Reads why a process is being written down now, and settles with the
named owner where it starts, where it stops and who owns it. Everything
the run watches afterwards sits inside those boundaries.

covers:
- Name the Owner and the Boundaries
- Take in the Request

## gather-every-account-of-the-work

Finds every description that already exists, each carrying its date,
sits with the work while it is done, and asks the people who do it what
they do when the written version does not fit.

covers:
- Ask What People Actually Do
- Find What Is Already Written
- Watch the Work as It Is Done

## draft-the-steps-in-order

Writes the steps in the order they happen, each one carrying its doer,
what starts it and what it hands on, and marks where the work
legitimately differs and what each variant turns on.

covers:
- Draft the Activity Walk
- Mark the Variants

## mark-what-the-accounts-do-not-settle

Marks a step disputed where the accounts of the work do not agree, and
keeps every account attached rather than averaging them into one
sentence that reads finished. What nobody could pin down is written
down as vague.

covers:
- Mark the Disputed Steps
- Record What Stayed Vague

## publish-the-process-at-a-version

Reads the draft back to the people who do the work, publishes it at a
version with a fixed address, marks every document it replaces, and
hands that version on as the one to be measured.

covers:
- Set the Version and Publish
- Take in the Published Process
- Walk It Back to the People

## reopen-a-process-the-review-found-wrong

Reads the reasons people gave for departing and says whether the
process is wrong or the process is ignored, then opens the amendments
for the steps that were wrong so they are documented again.

covers:
- Open the Process Amendments
- Sort the Reasons
