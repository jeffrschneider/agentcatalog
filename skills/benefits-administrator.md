# benefits-administrator

## prepare-the-plan-year

Opens the renewal with its timetable counted back from the first day of
the plan year, sends the broker the enrollment figures the carriers need
with no one's health identified, and sets up and tests the plans, rates
and eligibility rules the named people chose before the window opens.

covers:
- Open the Renewal
- Send the Enrollment Figures
- Set Up the Plans for the New Year

## run-the-election-window

Takes in a new hire or a life event, sets the window the plan rules
allow, tells each employee the options, the costs and the deadline,
records each election as the employee confirms it, reminds those who
have not elected, and applies the plan's default once the window closes.

covers:
- Apply the Default
- Apply the Defaults
- Collect the Elections
- Record the Elections
- Remind Before the Window Closes
- Remind Those Who Have Not Elected
- Set the Election Window
- Take in the Event
- Tell Employees What Changed
- Tell the Employee the Options

## check-eligibility-and-evidence

Checks the employee and each dependent against the plan rules and asks
only for the evidence the plan names, held where only the named benefits
people may open it, with any evidence of good health sent from the
employee to the carrier directly.

covers:
- Ask for the Evidence the Plan Requires
- Check Eligibility
- Check Eligibility and Evidence

## send-and-match-the-enrollments

Sends each enrollment to its carrier and each deduction to payroll
before the cutoff, matches each carrier's confirmation and first bill to
the elections and the deductions, sends the employee a confirmation of
the cover and its cost, and closes the enrollment on the record.

covers:
- Close the Enrollment on the Record
- Confirm the Carriers Hold the Coverage
- Confirm the Carriers Hold the Elections
- Match the First Bills
- Record What the Enrollment Taught
- Send the Deductions to Payroll
- Send the Elections to the Carriers
- Send the Employee a Confirmation

## carry-benefits-through-a-change

Works out how each benefit continues while an employee is on leave and
puts that arrangement in place, restores normal benefits and deductions
on the return, and when someone leaves sends each end date to its
carrier and payroll with the continuation notices by their deadline.

covers:
- End or Continue the Benefits
- Put the Benefits Arrangement in Place
- Restore Normal Benefits
- Work Out Benefits During the Leave
