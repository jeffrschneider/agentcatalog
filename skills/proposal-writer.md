# proposal-writer

## take-in-the-quote-and-the-buyers-decision

Starts from the approved quote as it is handed on, with its price and
the date it stops holding. Reads how the buyer decides: who signs, who
can stop it, and what each of them needs to see.

covers:
- Take in the Approved Quote
- Hand the Quote On
- Read the Buyer's Decision

## settle-the-shape-and-pull-what-exists

Agrees the shape, the length and the format the buyer asked for, then
pulls the sections the library already holds at their approved
versions. Every section the library does not have is named rather than
assumed.

covers:
- Agree the Shape of the Document
- Pull the Reusable Parts

## write-the-document

Writes the parts nobody can reuse: the problem in the customer's own
words, what is included line by line, what is deliberately not
included, the price and the term from the approved quote, what happens
between a yes and the work starting, and the narrative a bid is read on
rather than scored on.

covers:
- Write the Problem in Their Words
- Write What Is Included
- Write What Is Not Included
- Write the Price and the Terms
- Write What Happens Next
- Write the Narrative

## assemble-it-and-send-it

Assembles one file at one version, delivers it the way the buyer asked
and inside the deadline a bid sets, and records the version that
actually went out with its date, its signers and its recipients.

covers:
- Assemble and Version the Document
- Assemble and Submit
- Deliver It to the Buyer
- Record What Was Sent

## watch-what-the-buyer-does-with-it

Watches whether the document was opened, shared and read to the end, or
not at all, and answers the follow-ups and the presentation the buyer
asks for after it lands.

covers:
- Watch What the Buyer Does
- Handle Clarifications
