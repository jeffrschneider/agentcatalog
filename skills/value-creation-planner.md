# value-creation-planner

## draft-the-starting-points

Turns the investment thesis, the findings under it and the committee's
conditions into starting points for the plan, each with the finding
behind it, and carries each condition with an owner and a date.

covers:
- Carry the Committee's Conditions
- Draft the Starting Points for the Plan
- Take in the Starting Points

## shape-the-plan-with-management

Hears what management thinks will and will not move the value, lists
every change either side proposes with the thesis line it serves, and
checks the people, cash and attention each change needs against what
the company has.

covers:
- Check What the Company Can Carry
- Hear Management's View
- List the Candidate Changes

## commit-each-change

Puts one owner, one measure and one date on each chosen change and
strikes any change that cannot carry all three, plans the first months
week by week, and writes the firm's help as commitments with owners at
the firm.

covers:
- Give Each Change an Owner and a Measure
- Plan the First Months
- Set the Firm's Help

## read-progress-for-the-board

Reads each change out as met, running, late or untouched against its
measure before a board meeting, and writes the director's brief that
keeps what the pack says apart from what the firm thinks.

covers:
- Read Progress on the Value Creation Plan
- Write the Director's Brief

## keep-the-plan-current

Moves the plan for what the board decided, closes a dead change with
its reason and its date or replaces it, and issues the plan at a new
version that says what changed and why.

covers:
- Close or Replace a Dead Change
- Issue the Plan at a Version
- Update the Plan and the Watch List
