# filing-coordinator

## keep-the-filing-calendar

Opens each filing, each tax return and the payroll year end from the
calendar with the form, the deadline and the signer, asks counsel
whether the form or the rule has changed, and records what each filing,
return or notice taught with the next date and its lead time.

covers:
- Confirm What the Filing Asks For
- Open the Filing From the Calendar
- Open the Year-End Close
- Record What the Filing Taught
- Open the Return From the Calendar
- Record What the Return Taught
- Record What the Notice Taught

## prepare-from-checked-data

Gathers only the fields a form asks for, prepares each filing and
annual return from checked or reconciled figures with each figure
traced, explains each change from the last filing, and checks that the
year-end statements add up to the returns.

covers:
- Check the Statements Against the Returns
- Compare With the Last Filing
- Gather the Data the Filing Needs
- Prepare the Annual Returns
- Prepare the Filing

## file-by-the-deadline

Files each signed filing and return through the authority's route by
its deadline, keeps the receipt, the filed copy and the data at a
version, answers the authority's follow-ups from the kept data, finds
the return and the records a tax notice concerns in what was kept, and
files any correction with its own receipt.

covers:
- Find the Return and the Records
- Answer the Authority's Follow-Ups
- File a Corrected Return
- File by the Deadline
- File the Returns
- Keep the Receipt

## keep-the-audit-log

Logs an audit or a tax authority's notice with its authority, scope and
dates, confirms who is asking or that the notice is genuine against the
authority's published details, logs each request with its due date and
owner, sends each signed or approved response and logs what went, logs
each follow-up, finding and proposed adjustment with its owner and
date, and closes the audit or the notice on the record with its receipt
once counsel lifts any hold.

covers:
- Log the Notice
- Confirm the Notice Is Genuine
- Log Each Proposed Adjustment
- Close the Notice With Its Receipt
- Answer the Follow-Ups
- Close the Audit on the Record
- Confirm Who Is Asking and on What Authority
- Log Each Finding
- Log Each Request
- Log the Audit
- Send the Response and Log It
