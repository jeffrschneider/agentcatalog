# career-planner

## keep-the-development-plans

Writes into each employee's development plan the actions chosen at a
review, the actions each successor agreed, the feedback an internal
applicant who was not chosen agreed to keep, and the plan a mover
writes again for the new role, each with an owner and a date.

covers:
- Add Development Actions
- Record Feedback in the Development Plan
- Record the Development Plan
- Reset the Development Plan

## prepare-the-succession-review

Asks each manager who could step into each key role, sits beside the
manager who records each readiness view with its evidence, reads each
named person's stated interest, and prepares a review pack with no
pay, health, leave or case record in it. It names the roles with no
ready successor and the development needs a readiness view waits on.

covers:
- Ask Managers for Possible Successors
- Name the Roles with No Ready Successor
- Prepare the Review Pack
- Read Each Person's Career Interest
- Record Readiness with Its Evidence

## match-needs-to-what-opens

Counts the needs the development plans record by skill and by team
with no person named, and tells the people whose plans asked when a
course for their need is there or when an internal opening names the
kind of role they want.

covers:
- Gather Needs from the Development Plans
- Tell the People Who Asked
