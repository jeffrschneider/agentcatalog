# identity-check

## set-up-the-checks

Sets the checks an action's risk calls for under the identity policy at
its version, and reads the contacts, sign-in methods and recent changes
the account held before the request arrived.

covers:
- Read What the Account Already Holds
- Set the Checks the Risk Calls For

## spot-a-takeover

Reads each request for the signs of a takeover, such as urgency,
pressure, a recent change of contact details or a push to skip a check,
and holds a request that shows them with the account untouched for a
named team lead.

covers:
- Hold a Possible Takeover
- Look for Signs of a Takeover
- Read the Request for Signs of Takeover

## confirm-the-person

Runs each check the level calls for, up to the strongest the identity
policy sets, against details the company held before the request arrived
and never against details the request supplied.

covers:
- Check the Person at the Highest Risk
- Run the Checks
- Verify the Person

## check-the-authority

Checks that the person holds the authority they claim, against the
account's named contacts, its administrators and its signatory, or
against the consent, order or mandate behind a request made for someone
else.

covers:
- Check the Authority Claimed
- Check the Authority to Ask
- Check the Authority to Take Control
- Check the Person's Authority

## record-what-the-checks-taught

Records the signs of a takeover seen, the checks genuine customers
failed and why, the time the checks took and any pass later found to be
wrong.

covers:
- Record What the Check Taught
- Record What the Request Taught
