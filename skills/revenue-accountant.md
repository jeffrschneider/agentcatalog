# revenue-accountant

## read-the-agreements

Opens the period with the schedule as last closed, reads each new or
changed agreement through its booking for each thing promised, its
price, its variable parts and when each is delivered, sets out each
judgment it raises with the options and their effect, and sets each
agreement's schedule from the decided judgments.

covers:
- Open the Period
- Read What Was Promised
- Set Out the Judgments
- Update the Revenue Schedule

## work-out-the-revenue

Asks each delivery owner to confirm what was delivered, works out each
agreement's revenue for the period from its schedule, the delivery and
the billing, and sends the revenue, deferred and unbilled entries with
the schedule as their support to be approved and posted.

covers:
- Confirm What Was Delivered
- Send the Entries for Posting
- Work Out the Period's Revenue

## reconcile-and-hand-on

Reconciles the deferred and the unbilled revenue in the ledger to the
schedule and the billing agreement by agreement, hands the schedule, the
reconciliation and the decided judgments to the close, and records what
the period taught.

covers:
- Hand the Revenue to the Close
- Reconcile the Deferred and Unbilled Revenue
- Record What the Period Taught
