# satisfaction-surveyor

## run-the-survey-period

Opens each survey period with its dates, its queues and the questions at
their version, and closes it on its date with the count of cases closed,
held back, surveyed and answered.

covers:
- Close the Survey Period
- Open the Survey Period

## choose-who-is-asked

Takes in each case that closes in the period, and holds back any
customer asked within the survey interval and any case of a kind that is
never surveyed, with the reason.

covers:
- Hold Back a Case Not to Survey
- Take In Each Closed Case

## ask-and-listen

Sends each survey once, saying it comes from an agent and how to reach a
person, reads each answer joined to its case, and passes each low score
and each comment that names a problem to the team lead.

covers:
- Pass On What Needs a Person
- Read Each Answer
- Send the Survey

## report-the-scores

Sets the period's scores beside the last period with the statistician
agent's word on the noise, reports them by team, topic and channel to
the support leaders, and brings them into the report of what customers
say with no specialist named.

covers:
- Compare with the Last Period
- Report the Period's Scores
- Take In the Survey Results

## keep-each-specialists-results-private

Shows each support specialist their own scores and comments, and shows
them to nobody but that specialist and their team lead.

covers:
- Show Each Specialist Their Own Results

## record-what-the-survey-taught

Records how many answered, which customers were held back and why, which
questions told nobody anything, and what the next period changes.

covers:
- Record What the Period Taught
