# company-scout

## write-and-test-the-sector-thesis

Takes in the partner's proposal, helps write the thesis as claims that
can be proved wrong, tests each claim against the gathered evidence,
checks the sector against the fund's limits, and has the criteria for a
fitting company fixed at a version before any company is scored.

covers:
- Check the Thesis Fits the Fund
- Set What a Fitting Company Looks Like
- Take in the Sector Proposal
- Test the Thesis Against the Evidence
- Write the Sector Thesis

## list-and-score-the-companies

Lists every company that meets the criteria with the source it was
found in, scores each one with the reason it fits and the facts still
missing, matches the list to what the firm already holds, and issues
the map with its watch list at a version, adding a company found later
at a new version.

covers:
- Add a Company Found Later
- Agree Which Companies to Watch
- Issue the Map at a Version
- List the Companies That Fit
- Match the List to What the Firm Holds
- Say Why Each Company Fits

## watch-for-signs-of-readiness

Records the signs the partner set and where each would first show,
watches every company on the list for them with each sign dated and
sourced, and passes a ready company to its owning partner with the sign
and the case.

covers:
- Pass On a Company That Is Ready
- Set the Signs to Watch For
- Watch the Companies

## clear-a-company-for-approach

Takes in a ready company and checks that nobody at the firm is already
talking to it, and that no do-not-contact request, restricted list
entry, standstill or portfolio conflict bars an approach.

covers:
- Check Nobody Is Already Talking
- Check the Company May Be Approached
- Take in the Ready Company

## make-the-approach

Writes why this firm, why now and what the firm brings for the person
who will send it, sees it sent under that person's name, sends one
follow-up on its date, and briefs the partner before the first meeting.

covers:
- Brief the Partner
- Follow Up Once
- Send the Approach
- Write the Approach

## close-out-the-map-and-the-approach

Brings the partner's decision on what comes next, opens the opportunity
with its named owner, records whether the approach landed, reviews the
map on its date, and records what the map led to and what it missed.

covers:
- Decide What Comes Next
- Open the Opportunity
- Record the Outcome
- Record What the Map Produced
- Review the Map
