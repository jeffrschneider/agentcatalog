# commitment-keeper

## turn-a-signed-plan-into-tracked-items

Takes the milestones a signed plan carries, or the fixes a failed
control or an auditor's letter needs, and makes each one an item with
one owner, one date and a state, traceable to the line of the plan or
the finding it came from.

covers:
- Set the Milestones and the Dates
- Record the Commitments
- Load the Plan into the Tracker
- Load the Actions into the Tracker
- Load Each Fix Into the Tracker
- Record the Actions

## put-the-reviews-on-the-calendar

Sets how often the plan is reviewed, opens each review on that cadence
against the calendar of everyone the plan names, and dates the next one
before the current one closes.

covers:
- Set the Review Cadence
- Open the Review
- Set the Next Review
- Set the Check-In Dates
- Book the Coaching Session

## read-every-commitment-out-loud

Reads every commitment at review with its owner, its date and its state,
marked met, running, late or untouched, so nothing stays open by
silence.

covers:
- Read the Commitments
- Read Progress on the Value Creation Plan
- Read the Open Actions
- Read Progress Against the Plan
- Follow Up the Actions
- Record the Check-In
- Hold Each Check-In
- Review the Actions
- Chase Each Fix to Its Date
- Read Out Last Time's Actions
- Chase Each Action to Its Date

## close-what-nobody-is-going-to-do

Closes the commitments nobody is going to do, with the reason and the
date they were given up on, instead of carrying them forward.

covers:
- Say Which Commitments Are Dead

## write-the-review-decisions-back

Writes the new owners and the new dates back against each item, and
keeps every move of a date with the review that moved it, so a
commitment that has slipped twice can be seen to have slipped twice.

covers:
- Record the Decisions and the Dates
- Record the Decisions and Actions

## track-the-support-promised

Tracks the support the company promised in a plan, such as coaching,
training and time, as items with the manager as their owner, so the
record shows whether the company kept its side before anyone judges the
employee's progress.

covers:
- Give the Agreed Support

## set-out-the-record-for-the-person-deciding

Sets out a plan's whole record for the named person who decides on it,
such as a manager deciding a probation: each item with its state, every
check-in and the support given. It recommends no outcome.

covers:
- Prepare the Probation Review

## close-out-a-plan

At the end of a plan, records the last state of every item: met,
carried into the next plan with its owner and its date, or closed with
the reason. The plan closes with nothing left open by silence.

covers:
- Close the Plan
- Close the Coaching Cycle

## learn-from-a-coaching-cycle

Convenes the debrief at the end of a coaching cycle on which actions
held and which slipped, whether the support the team lead promised
arrived on its date, and what the next session changes, and counts for
the head of support how many specialists said coaching helped, with no
names attached.

covers:
- Record What the Cycle Taught
