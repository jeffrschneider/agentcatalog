# staffing-planner

## work-out-the-people-needed

Opens each period's forecast from the last version, turns the requests
into the people each channel and interval needs for its service level,
adds the time away from cases, sets the need beside the team, says what
a candidate service level would take, and publishes the forecast at a
version.

covers:
- Add the Time Away from Cases
- Open the Period's Forecast
- Publish the Forecast
- Say What Each Would Take in People
- Set the Need Beside the Team
- Turn the Requests into People Needed

## set-out-the-options

Sets out each way to meet a staffing gap or a queue about to miss, with
what each gives the service levels, what it does to the other queues and
what it costs, for the named person who decides.

covers:
- Set Out What Can Be Done
- Set Out the Options for a Gap

## build-the-schedule

Reads the people available, the approved leave and the working time
rules, drafts the schedule and the after-hours rota, checks every shift
against the rules and every interval against its need, shows the draft
to the team leads, and publishes it once it is approved.

covers:
- Build the After-Hours Rota
- Check Every Shift Against the Rules
- Check the Draft Covers the Need
- Draft the Schedule
- Open the Schedule Period
- Publish the Schedule
- Read the Approved Leave
- Read the People Available
- Read the Working Time Rules
- Show the Draft to the Team Leads

## handle-a-swap

Takes in each shift swap a support specialist asks for, checks the
channel's cover and the working time rules for both people as the
schedule would stand after it, and writes each approved swap into the
schedule.

covers:
- Check the Swap for Cover
- Take In a Swap Request
- Update the Published Schedule

## staff-the-day

Opens the day from the published schedule, watches the people working
each channel against the plan, writes each move a team lead decided into
the day's schedule, and checks each offer of cover against the working
time rules.

covers:
- Call In Cover
- Move People Between Channels
- Open the Day
- Watch the People on Each Channel

## keep-the-after-hours-cover

Opens each after-hours period with its rota, confirms the person on call
and the one behind them, names the next person on the rota when a page
goes unanswered, and checks that the morning team has the people for the
held queue.

covers:
- Check the Morning Has the People
- Confirm Who Is on Call
- Open the After-Hours Period
- Page the Next Person on the Rota

## record-what-staffing-taught

Records where cover fell short and why, which assumptions missed, which
swaps were refused, each day's coverage and decisions, and the pages and
waits of each after-hours period, for the next forecast and schedule.

covers:
- Record What the Forecast Taught
- Record What the Period Taught
- Record What the Schedule Taught
- Record the Day
