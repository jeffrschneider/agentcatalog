# email-producer

## take-the-send-from-the-briefing

Sits in the briefing that starts a send and takes away the same account
everyone else heard: who the message is for, what it has to say, what is
changing, and when it has to arrive.

covers:
- Brief the Agents that Act
- Brief the Agents that Remake the Material
- Brief the Makers

## build-the-list-a-send-goes-to

Builds the list a message goes to from the contacts that are eligible for
it, with opt-outs and suppressions taken out, and splits it where each
group has to get a different message.

covers:
- Build the Contact List
- Build the Invitation List
- Build the Notice Lists

## set-up-the-send

Assembles the message in the sending system from copy and design that
have already been signed off, loads the audience, sets the schedule, and
sends proof copies so the people who have to read the message before
anyone else see it exactly as a recipient would.

covers:
- Build the Issue
- Build the Send
- Prepare the Send
- Proof the Issue
- Set up the Practical Side
- Set up the Sending

## send-the-message

Fires a prepared send once the named owner says go, and keeps the record
of what went out to whom. The same work covers a single message, a step
in a sequence, a reminder to the people who have not answered, a notice
that has to be provable, and a statement that goes out on every channel
at once.

covers:
- Deliver the Intervention
- Issue the Response
- Open Recruitment
- Publish It on the Owned Channels
- Send the Ask
- Send the Issue
- Send the Pre-Read
- Send the Reminder
- Send the Sequence
- Send the Step
- Serve the Notice
- Start the Touches
- Tell the Customers Who Have It

## promote-a-run-to-fill-it

Runs the messages that get people to sign up for something or turn up to
it, pointed at registration or at where the thing is happening.

covers:
- Promote the Conference
- Promote the Event
- Promote the Presence
- Promote the Session

## work-the-registration-list

Keeps the registration list moving between the invitation and the day:
chases the people who have not answered, confirms the ones who have, and
manages the waitlist.

covers:
- Work the Registration List

## follow-up-after-the-thing-happened

Sends people what they were promised after they came: the recording, the
material, the answers to what was asked, and what happens next.

covers:
- Follow up with the Members
- Follow up with the People Who Came
- Follow up with the Registrants

## hand-material-to-another-party-to-send

Puts approved material and lists where another party can pick them up, so
a partner or the other side of a joint run can send from its own systems.

covers:
- Publish the Material
- Set up Each Side's Distribution

## close-out-contacts-who-never-answered

Closes out the contacts that went through a whole run without ever
answering, so they stop receiving messages.

covers:
- Retire the Ones Who Stayed Silent

## report-what-a-send-did

Reports what happened after a send: what was delivered, what bounced, who
opened and clicked, who unsubscribed or complained, and what converted.

covers:
- Watch the Results

## take-in-a-finished-definition

Takes in a definition another agent has finished and says what it changes
about how messages are built and who they go to.

covers:
- Hand over the Persona

## remake-the-templates-and-switch-over

Remakes the templates that messages and pages are built from, stages the
new versions where they can be previewed without serving anyone, and
switches the properties and the sending system over on the agreed day.

covers:
- Rebuild the Templates
- Stage the Changes
- Switch the Properties
