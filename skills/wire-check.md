# wire-check

## verify-payment-instructions

Checks each payee's account, each investor's account, each employee's
account, each supplier's account and the fund's own receiving account
against the verified record, and confirms any change by a call to a
contact already on file, through a channel independent of the one the
instruction came in. For a supplier it checks the request behind a new
or changed account for its channel, its sender, its timing and any sign
of fraud, sets the account name and the bank against the supplier's
legal name, and finds the contact on file, or one apart from the
request, that a person calls. An account it cannot confirm holds that
payment.

covers:
- Confirm the Fund's Bank Instructions
- Verify Each Investor's Payment Instructions
- Verify the Payment Instructions
- Confirm a New Bank Account
- Check the Bank Details
- Find a Contact Apart from the Request
- Check the Request
- Check the New Account
- Find the Contact on File

## follow-each-outgoing-payment

Checks each payment a named person releases, such as the chief
financial officer, the payroll approver or the releasers a company's
delegation of authority names, against the verified line it pays,
including each transfer between the company's accounts and each payroll
funding, then confirms where each one landed and that each payee
received it. It marks the first payment to a changed account for a
check, and holds a returned or rejected payment with the bank's reason
until it can be paid again to verified details. It can see payments and
holds no right to release one.

covers:
- Confirm Each Payment Landed
- Confirm the Money Arrived
- Check the Payment File
- Confirm the Payments Landed
- Mark the First Payment
- Confirm the First Payment Landed
- Confirm Where Each Payment Landed
- Handle a Returned Payment
- Check the Transfer
- Confirm the Transfer Landed
- Check the Funding Transfer
- Confirm the Funding Landed

## match-incoming-money

Sees incoming money cleared in the fund's account and matches each
amount to the investor, the call or the sale it belongs to, holding
aside anything late, short, over or sent from an account not on
record.

covers:
- Confirm the Cash Has Arrived
- Confirm the Money Has Arrived
- Match Each Receipt

## reconcile-to-the-cent

Compares what was called or approved with what was received or paid
and what was booked, investor by investor, with the
capital-account-keeper agent, and agrees the bank balance to the books.
For a payroll funding it sets the funding against every payment that
left the payroll account on payday.

covers:
- Reconcile the Call
- Reconcile the Distribution
- Match the Funding to the Payments

## record-the-signs-of-fraud

Writes up a bank change the supplier does not confirm, or a first
payment that did not land, as a suspected fraud for the controller the
same day, with the request as it arrived, its sender and what was
checked. Afterwards it records the signs of fraud each change showed
and how long its confirmation took, so the next change is checked
against them.

covers:
- Report a Suspected Fraud
- Record What the Change Taught
