# complaint-coordinator

## log-and-acknowledge

Takes in each complaint once in the customer's own words, reads it
against the complaints policy, logs it on the register with the deadline
the local rule sets, and acknowledges it in the approved words.

covers:
- Acknowledge the Complaint
- Check It against the Complaints Policy
- Log the Complaint with Its Deadline
- Take In the Complaint

## send-on-what-others-decide

Sends a legal threat or a regulator's letter to the legal team the same
day, and any refund or credit an answer would offer to the process where
a named person decides it.

covers:
- Get Any Refund or Credit Decided
- Send Anything Legal the Same Day

## set-out-the-record

Gathers every case, reply, call record and promise behind a complaint
with the case-manager agent, and sets out the complaint, the answer and
the record for the person who reviews an answer the customer does not
accept.

covers:
- Gather the Case History and the Records
- Review an Answer the Customer Does Not Accept

## record-the-outcome

Records the outcome of each point, the dates and the route to take the
complaint further on the register, and sends the cause of each complaint
to whoever owns it.

covers:
- Feed the Cause Back
- Record the Outcome
