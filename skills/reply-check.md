# reply-check

## check-each-reply

Reads each drafted reply, complaint answer, outage summary, reply to a
reporter and saved reply against the content it cites, the facts or the
record it rests on and the reply rules at their version, names any
sentence that rests on none of them, and checks a changed reply again.

covers:
- Check a Changed Reply Again
- Check the Answer
- Check the Reply
- Check the Reply Against Its Content
- Check the Reply Against the Reply Rules
- Check the Reply Before It Goes
- Check the Summary

## hold-for-the-named-person

Holds a reply in a category a named person must send, names the rule and
the approver for its category, marks a saved reply in such a category
for approval each time it is used, holds each reply to a reporter or
about a credit for the person who sends it, and passes a legal matter or
a reporter's question to the team that must answer it.

covers:
- Hold a Sensitive Reply
- Mark It for Approval Each Time It Is Used
- Name the Approver for Its Category
- Pass What Another Team Must Answer
- Take In the Held Reply
- Tell Each Customer Its Credit
- Tell the Reporter the Outcome

## set-out-the-commitments

Sets out line by line what a held reply commits the company to, such as
the fault, the date, the money and any change to the contract, and
checks that any money offered was already decided by a named person who
did not propose it.

covers:
- Check the Money Was Decided
- Set Out What the Reply Commits To

## record-what-the-holds-taught

Records the replies held without need, the replies the approvers changed
and why, the matters sent to the legal team and the rules that missed a
reply.

covers:
- Record What the Holds Taught
