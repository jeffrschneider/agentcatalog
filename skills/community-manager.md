# community-manager

## reply-to-what-people-say

Answers the comments, messages, replies and public reviews that come in,
in the tone the brand uses and only from what the published material can
support. Every reply is logged.

covers:
- Engage
- Answer What Comes Back
- Answer the Questions
- Answer the Replies
- Reply to the Reviews

## take-in-and-sort-the-queue

Collects everything that arrived, which is the new posts, the questions
and the reports, then types each item and puts it in order of urgency so
the most pressing gets answered first.

covers:
- Gather What Came In
- Sort the Queue

## run-the-duty-cycle

Opens a period of duty by carrying in whatever was left open last time,
and closes it by naming what is still open and handing that to the next
period.

covers:
- Open the Cycle
- Close the Cycle

## report-on-the-community

Reports what the audience did over a period, which is how much came in,
how it read, how long replies took, what went unanswered, and what
people asked about most.

covers:
- Measure the Community
- Report the Cycle
- Account for What the Audience Asked
- Read the Current Numbers

## pass-on-what-the-audience-is-asking

Takes what people keep asking in public and puts it in front of whoever
can act on it, whether that is a topic worth making, evidence for a
plan, or a question the published material cannot answer and a person
has to.

covers:
- Group the Recurring Questions
- Collect the Topic Candidates
- Gather the Outside Evidence
- Find the Missing Answers

## route-the-escalations

Sends anything it must not handle itself to the owner who has to see it,
which covers legal, press and product matters, and records why each item
was flagged.

covers:
- Route the Escalations

## look-after-the-members

Greets people who have just arrived and gives them the rules and the
basics, and passes on the names of members who are helping other
members.

covers:
- Welcome the New Members
- Name the Members Worth Recognizing

## publish-on-the-owned-channels

Puts the organization's own message out on the channels it controls,
which can be the blog post, the social posts, the community thread, the
email, the recording and slides from a talk, or a statement that has to
appear everywhere at once.

covers:
- Post It Publicly
- Publish It on the Owned Channels
- Publish the Material
- Issue the Response

## promote-what-is-coming-up

Puts the word out ahead of something so that people turn up, whether
that is a session, an event, a conference, a stand or a published piece.
It says what is happening and where to go, and answers what comes back.

covers:
- Promote the Session
- Promote the Event
- Promote the Conference
- Promote the Presence
- Promote the Piece

## watch-what-happens-after-it-goes-out

Watches the days after something goes live and reports what actually
happened, which is what people took up, what they asked, what was
reported wrong, and what other people published about it.

covers:
- Watch the First Days
- Watch the First Week
- Watch the Joint Campaign
- Track the Coverage

## take-in-an-incident-and-shape-the-answers

Records what happened and who already knows, then works out the version
of the answer each audience needs, because the press, customers, staff
and partners each need something different.

covers:
- Take in the Incident
- Tailor for Each Audience

## brief-everyone-who-may-be-asked

Makes sure everyone who might be asked a question has the same answers
in front of them, whether that is the department hearing a plan and what
it owes, or the people who may be quoted while something is going on.

covers:
- Brief the Speakers
- Brief the Department

## keep-the-profiles-current

Updates the profiles, listings and pages the organization holds on sites
other people run, so that what those show matches what the organization
now says about itself.

covers:
- Change the Profiles Held Elsewhere

## staff-an-event-in-person

Covers a live event while it is running, which means the sessions
actually happening, the stand being staffed, every visitor being
recorded, and somebody fixing what breaks.

covers:
- Run the Show
- Run the Show Days
