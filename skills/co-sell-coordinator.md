# co-sell-coordinator

## open-a-joint-pursuit

Takes in a pursuit two organizations will work as one deal, reads the
account as each side holds it, and picks a deal back up when a conflict
is settled by both sides working it together.

covers:
- Take in the Joint Pursuit
- Read the Account on Both Sides
- Restart the Deal

## name-who-does-what-on-each-side

Gets the lead named in writing before the first joint approach, names
the people on each side with what each is there to do, and divides the
solution so a question can be routed to a side without asking.

covers:
- Name the Lead and the Support
- Name the People on Each Side
- Agree What Each Side Sells

## build-one-plan-across-both-sides

Builds one plan with one set of dates and owners spanning both
organizations, sets the rule saying who may approach the customer and
how long the other side waits, and puts the whole of it in front of both
teams at once.

covers:
- Build the One Joint Plan
- Set the Contact Rule
- Brief Both Teams

## keep-the-two-sides-in-step

Logs every approach on the day it was made, holds the touches that would
put the customer in front of both sides at once, and runs the joint
check-in against the one plan with each step marked done, open or
dropped.

covers:
- Keep the Approaches in Step
- Hold the Joint Check-In

## close-the-pursuit

Settles which organization papers the order and what the other side
invoices, then closes the run with a debrief.

covers:
- Settle Who Books the Order
- Debrief the Pursuit
