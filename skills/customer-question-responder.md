# customer-question-responder

## sort-what-a-person-must-answer

Takes up each question and reads it against the categories the reply
rules keep for a person, and tells a billing explanation from a claim
that an invoice is wrong, a request for money back or a request to
change the plan.

covers:
- Sort Out What a Person Must Answer
- Take Up the Question
- Tell a Question from a Claim

## find-the-content-and-the-record

Finds the approved article that answers a question with its reply
category, and reads only the parts of the customer's record or the
billing records the answer depends on.

covers:
- Find the Approved Answer
- Read Only What the Answer Needs
- Read the Billing Records

## answer-from-approved-content

Writes each answer from approved content and the customer's own records
alone, checks every sentence against them, sends it as an agent with the
article named and a way to reach a person, by day, after hours or from
the latest approved update, and asks whether it settled the question.

covers:
- Answer What Approved Content Covers
- Answer a New Case from the Approved Update
- Answer from Approved Content
- Answer the Replies
- Check the Answer Before It Goes
- Check the Question Is Settled
- Send the Answer as an Agent
- Write the Answer from Approved Content

## hand-to-a-person

Passes a case a person must take to a support specialist with a draft,
what was found and why, and tells the customer in the approved words
when a person will reply.

covers:
- Hand the Case to a Person
- Pass What the Update Does Not Answer
- Pass the Question to a Specialist
- Tell Each Other Customer When a Person Will Reply

## draft-for-a-person

Drafts a reply from approved content and the facts of the case for a
support specialist to edit, and drafts the notice about a fix for a team
lead to approve.

covers:
- Draft the Notice
- Draft the Reply

## send-approved-wording

Sends exactly the approved wording to each linked customer, each contact
an agreement names and the other affected accounts, by the route each is
owed and before any deadline, and only once a customer has the change it
describes.

covers:
- Send Each Later Update
- Send Each Notice an Agreement Names
- Send the Notice to Each Linked Customer
- Send the Resolved Update
- Tell the Other Affected Accounts

## record-who-was-told

Records each customer told with the notice version, when it went and
each reply, and what the run of notices taught about the time to tell,
the notices that bounced and the cases reopened.

covers:
- Record What the Run Taught
- Record Who Was Told
