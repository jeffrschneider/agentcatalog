# accommodation-coordinator

## take-in-the-request

Takes a request in the first time it is heard, in whatever words it
comes, acknowledges it with who will see what and the date a decision
is due, and opens a restricted file held apart from the employee's
record.

covers:
- Acknowledge the Request
- Open the Restricted File
- Take in the Request

## gather-what-the-decision-needs

Books the conversation between the employee, the manager and the HR
business partner, collects only the information the decision needs
about the limit on the work, and lists each option with its cost, its
time and its effect on the work.

covers:
- Collect Only the Information Needed
- List the Options
- Set Up the Conversation

## put-the-decision-in-place

Sends the decision a named person made to the employee in writing, and
tells the manager and the teams that make the change what to change and
nothing about why.

covers:
- Put the Change in Place
- Tell the Employee in Writing

## review-and-close

Reviews the change on its date with the employee and the manager,
closes the request with its next review date or the end of the change
and the file's retention date, and adds what the request showed to the
debrief with no employee named.

covers:
- Close the Request on the Record
- Record What the Request Taught
- Review the Change on Its Date
