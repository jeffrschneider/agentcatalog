# dispute-coordinator

## take-in-a-claim-and-promise-a-date

Takes a claim that a statement or a customer invoice is wrong,
acknowledges it, and names who holds it and the date the answer comes,
before anybody knows whether the claim is right. Puts the claim in a
class, arithmetic, credit, plan wording or bad data, or for an invoice a
price, a quantity or usage, the tax, the terms or work not received,
because the class decides who is heard and what evidence is pulled.

covers:
- Acknowledge and Promise a Date
- Classify the Claim
- Take in the Claim
- Take In the Claim
- Take In the Claim at Finance
- Tell the Customer Who Holds It Now

## get-the-evidence-behind-the-disputed-line

Freezes the statement or the invoice at its version so the argument is
about one document, has the records the number rests on pulled, such as
the booking, the usage readings and the agreement behind an invoice, and
reads out the plan sentence or the clause that governs the line at the
version the claimant signed rather than the current one.

covers:
- Freeze the Statement Version
- Pull the Evidence Behind the Line
- Read the Plan Sentence That Governs It
- Freeze the Invoice Version
- Pull the Records Behind the Invoice

## hear-everybody-the-claim-touches

Hears the claimant say what they believe happened in their own words,
and hears the other seller and its manager whenever credit would move away
from somebody, or for an invoice the account owner on what was promised
and the delivery lead on what was delivered. Nobody's statement is shown
to anybody else.

covers:
- Hear the Claimant
- Hear the Other Seller
- Hear the Customer
- Hear the Account Owner and Delivery

## decide-the-answer-and-send-it-with-its-evidence

Convenes the decision that upholds a claim, upholds part of it, or
refuses it, with the sentence and the record behind it. The answer
reaches the claimant with its evidence and the route to escalate in the
same message.

covers:
- Decide the Answer
- Tell the Claimant
- Tell the Customer

## escalate-what-the-claimant-does-not-accept

Takes a claimant who does not accept the answer to the people partner,
or a customer to the controller, on the route the answer named and
inside the window it gave. A refused claim is kept with its evidence,
because the next claim on the same sentence is the one that changes the
plan.

covers:
- Escalate What Is Not Accepted

## feed-the-cause-back

Names the cause behind a claim, such as the booking, the price book, the
meter, the invoice template, the tax setting or the delivery, and sends
it to whoever owns it, so the same claim does not arrive again.

covers:
- Feed the Cause Back
