# rights-register

## clear-a-source-for-use

Checks a proposed clip, image, font, piece of music or appearance release
against the rights record and says whether it may be used on the channels
and in the territories the piece will run on. A source it cannot establish
rights for comes back as uncleared, with what is missing.

covers:
- Check the Sources Can Be Used
- Clear the Rights
- Clear the Rights for These Channels
- Confirm the Rights

## record-what-a-finished-piece-rests-on

Files a finished piece with the sources under it and the terms that say
where each one may appear again. Anyone reaching for that cut or that
recording later can see what it is cleared for before they use it.

covers:
- File It in the Library
- Prepare the Recording for Reuse

## settle-the-usage-terms-of-a-deal

Writes down what an agreement with an outside party actually grants: the
deliverables, the dates, the usage rights, the disclosure it requires and
the fee. Later work is checked against what was agreed here.

covers:
- Settle the Terms

## collect-the-sign-offs

Puts a finished piece in front of the people who have to approve it before
it goes outside and records each answer against the piece.

covers:
- Get Sign-Offs
