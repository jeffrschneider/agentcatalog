# reporting-accountant

## consolidate-the-group

Opens each consolidation with the entities and the share owned of each,
takes in each entity's signed-off trial balance, maps each local account
to the group chart, sets the dated rates, translates each entity into
the group currency, prepares the eliminations and the other group
entries, builds the consolidated trial balance once they are approved,
and brings the posted tax into it.

covers:
- Bring the Tax Into the Group Accounts
- Build the Consolidated Trial Balance
- Map Each Entity to the Group Chart
- Open the Consolidation
- Prepare the Eliminations
- Prepare the Other Group Entries
- Record What the Consolidation Taught
- Set the Exchange Rates
- Take In Each Entity's Trial Balance
- Translate Into the Group Currency

## draft-the-statements

Opens the reporting pack, takes in the signed consolidated trial
balance, maps each account to its line, drafts the statements, gathers
the inputs to each note from their owners, drafts the notes and the tax
note, revises each draft with every change marked, and hands the
year-end draft to the external audit.

covers:
- Draft the Notes
- Draft the Statements
- Draft the Tax Note
- Gather the Inputs to the Notes
- Hand the Draft to the Audit
- Map the Accounts to the Statements
- Open the Reporting Pack
- Record What the Period Taught
- Revise the Draft
- Take In the Consolidated Trial Balance

## check-the-figures

Sets every figure in each entity, the group and the statements beside
the last period, flags each movement above the threshold for its owner
to explain with evidence, and ties every figure in the statements and
the notes to the trial balance and its support.

covers:
- Compare With the Last Period
- Explain Each Movement
- Tie Out Every Figure

## record-and-hand-on

Records each sign-off with the version signed, who signed it and when,
and hands the closed books and the group figures to the processes that
read them, including each entity's results before tax for the tax
provision and the closed figures for the covenant tests.

covers:
- Hand On the Closed Books
- Hand On the Group Figures
- Record the Sign-Off
- Supply the Results Before Tax
- Take In the Closed Figures
