# partner-monitor

## read-what-a-partner-delivered

Pulls the same measures, over the same period, from the same sources
for every partner: the deals and registrations they brought and what
became of them, what a funded activity actually produced, what the
partnership did last year, and what moved on the accounts a plan named.
Every reading names its measure set at a version and the date each
number was pulled, so somebody else can recompute it.

covers:
- Check What the Activity Produced
- Gather What the Partner Delivered
- Pull the Same Evidence for Everybody
- Read Last Year
- Read the Accounts
- Read What Each Partner Delivered
- Read What the Partner Did Before

## read-the-obligations-beyond-selling

Reads what a partner owes besides revenue, in the same round as the
numbers: the certifications held and by whom, the marketing run, the
support given and the reporting delivered. Certificates lapse and
certified people leave, so it keeps a dated line for each.

covers:
- Check What They Already Know
- Read the Obligations Beyond Selling
- Watch for Expiry and Drift

## watch-the-money-as-it-is-committed

Watches a fund while the period runs and says what is approved, what is
claimed and what is still free.

covers:
- Watch the Fund as It Is Committed

## check-what-the-partner-actually-experiences

Checks the path as the partner meets it rather than as the record
describes it. A test deal and a test ticket have to land, and the
entitlement a tier promises has to be the one the partner can actually
see.

covers:
- Check the Partner Sees the Right Thing
- Test the Path End to End

## check-the-accounts-a-plan-names

Picks the named accounts and segments two sides will work together, and
reads them against coverage for overlap, for conflicts and for accounts
already spoken for.

covers:
- Check the Accounts Against Coverage
- Pick the Accounts and Segments

## watch-a-first-deal-and-close-the-run-out

Watches a first deal while the partner leads it and says when it is
slipping, so a person can decide whether a stage is taken back. Then it
debriefs what the partner did alone and what the company did for them,
hands the relationship to its ongoing manager, and records where a
setup run stalled for the next one.

covers:
- Debrief with the Partner
- Hand the Partner to Their Ongoing Manager
- Record What the Onboarding Cost
- Step In When It Slips

## list-what-is-still-open-at-an-ending

Lists every customer the partner holds and every piece of work still in
flight before anything is closed, names what is still open as the
portal, the systems, the price lists and the leads are closed off, and
confirms the exit by naming anything nobody could close.

covers:
- Close Off the Access
- Confirm the Exit Is Complete
- List the Customers the Partner Holds
- List the Work in Flight
