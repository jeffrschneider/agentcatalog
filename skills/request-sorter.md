# request-sorter

## take-in-and-match

Takes in each request as it arrived on its channel, by day or after
hours, matches the sender to one customer and one account from the
details on file, holds a request it cannot match with no account
attached, and joins a second contact to the case already open on the
same matter.

covers:
- Hold an Unmatched Request
- Join a Case Already Open
- Match the Sender to an Account
- Take In Each Request
- Take In the Request

## send-on-what-cannot-wait

Reads each request for what cannot wait, such as a security or safety
report, a legal threat or a sign of a takeover, sends it to its owner
within the hour, and outside the team's hours pages the person on call
and then the next person on the rota.

covers:
- Page the Next Person on the Rota
- Page the Person on Call
- Send What Cannot Wait to Its Owner
- Spot What Cannot Wait
- Spot What Cannot Wait for Morning

## sort-and-place

Sorts each request by urgency and topic under the routing rules at their
version, sets the service level the customer's plan carries, marks what
needs a person or an identity check, places the case in its queue, and
sorts again a case a specialist says is in the wrong queue.

covers:
- Mark What Needs a Person or a Check
- Move a Misrouted Case
- Place the Case in Its Queue
- Set the Service Level
- Sort by Urgency and Topic

## hand-the-queue-between-shifts

Holds each case that waits for a person overnight with its clock
running, sets the held cases in order of urgency and service level,
hands them to the morning team, and hands the cases still waiting at the
close to the person on call.

covers:
- Hand Over to the Morning Team
- Hand the Queue to After-Hours Cover
- Hold What Waits for Morning
- Set the Held Cases in Order
- Take Over the Overnight Cases

## watch-for-a-surge

Reads what is arriving on each channel, counts the requests by symptom,
sends a symptom that passes the threshold to the outage process, gathers
and joins every case that describes it, and returns the cases to their
queues when the engineer rules it out.

covers:
- Gather the Cases on the Symptom
- Hold New Requests on the Symptom
- Notice a Rise on One Symptom
- Raise a Surge on One Symptom
- Read What Is Arriving
- Send Back a Ruled-Out Alarm

## try-a-routing-change

Sorts past requests under a proposed routing change away from live,
checks the first live requests under the new version, and watches for
work that lands in the wrong queue.

covers:
- Check the First Requests Land Right
- Replay It on Past Requests
- Watch for Work in the Wrong Queue

## record-what-intake-taught

Records which cases were misrouted and by which rule, which senders
could not be matched, which joins were undone and which urgent requests
reached their owner late.

covers:
- Record What the Intake Taught
