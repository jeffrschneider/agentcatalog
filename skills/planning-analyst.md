# planning-analyst

## open-the-plan

Opens the budget year or a reforecast with its calendar, its due dates
and its approvers, takes in the company's targets, proposes the
assumptions with a source for each, and issues the instructions every
department head drafts to.

covers:
- Issue the Budget Instructions
- Open the Budget Year
- Open the Reforecast
- Propose the Assumptions
- Take in the Company Targets

## gather-the-inputs

Takes in each department's draft, the closed results, the published
revenue forecast, the hiring plan and the changes each budget owner
already knows, and checks each draft against the agreed assumptions and
the workforce plan.

covers:
- Check Each Draft
- Collect the Changes Already Known
- Draft Each Department's Budget
- Take in the Hiring Plan
- Take in the Results So Far
- Take in the Revenue Forecast

## build-the-budget

Puts the drafts together into the company's income, costs, cash and
balance sheet, tests the whole against the targets, builds the options
for closing a gap, and tells each budget owner what their lines hold.

covers:
- Build the Options
- Put the Drafts Together
- Tell Each Budget Owner What They Hold
- Test the Budget against the Targets

## rebuild-the-year

Rebuilds each line for the months left, explains every movement from the
last forecast above the threshold, sets the year beside the budget,
takes each owner's confirmation and tells each owner what changed.

covers:
- Confirm Each Owner's Lines
- Explain Each Movement
- Rebuild the Rest of the Year
- Set the Year beside the Budget
- Tell the Budget Owners What Changed

## compare-results-with-the-plan

Takes in each closed month, ties every figure to the ledger, compares
each line with the budget and the latest forecast, marks the differences
above the threshold, checks each explanation against the entries behind
it, answers the leaders' questions, and hands the lasting differences to
the next reforecast.

covers:
- Answer the Leaders' Questions
- Check Each Explanation against the Ledger
- Compare the Results with the Plan
- Hand On What the Next Forecast Takes In
- Mark the Differences above the Threshold
- Take in the Closed Results
- Tie Every Figure to the Ledger

## write-up-an-investment-case

Takes in a sponsor's proposal, gathers its costs, checks each assumption
the model rests on, sets out the risks with the sponsor, finds where the
money comes from, and writes the case up in one document.

covers:
- Check the Assumptions
- Find Where the Money Comes From
- Gather the Costs
- Set Out the Risks
- Take in the Proposal
- Write Up the Case

## project-forward

Gives the cash forecast what the latest reforecast implies for the
months the open items do not cover, and projects each covenant to its
next tests from the cash forecast and the reforecast.

covers:
- Add the Plan for the Later Months
- Project the Covenants Forward

## record-what-the-plan-rests-on

Records what each budget, reforecast and case rests on, how the last
forecast compared with what happened, and what the next run checks
first.

covers:
- Record What the Budget Rests On
- Record What the Case Rests On
- Record What the Reforecast Rests On
