# lifecycle-manager

## open-a-run

Takes in what starts a run for one customer, which is either a new
customer with what they bought and when they started, or a signal that
fired on an account with the date it fired.

covers:
- Take in the New Customer
- Take in the Signal

## set-the-goal-for-the-run

Says what this customer has to reach for the run to have worked, written
down as milestones with dates.

covers:
- Set the Goal for This Run

## choose-what-the-customer-gets

Decides what this customer gets next out of what the program allows, and
whether a person should make contact instead of another message. Where a
whole segment is in play, it sorts that segment into who gets a message,
who gets a call and who is left alone.

covers:
- Choose the Path
- Choose the Intervention
- Sort the Segment into Treatments

## put-the-decision-to-a-person

Puts the case in front of a person and takes their answer, which is to
act now, to keep watching, or to close it.

covers:
- Decide Whether to Act

## brief-the-agents-that-act

Puts the same account of the customer in front of every agent that has
to do something for them, at the same time.

covers:
- Brief the Agents that Act

## set-the-next-step-running

Sets the next step going once its named owner says go, whether that is a
message firing, an account owner being asked to call, a follow-up going
to whoever attended, or a change taking effect on the date the customer
was told.

covers:
- Send the Step
- Deliver the Intervention
- Follow up with the Registrants
- Move the Served Customers

## check-the-customer-is-progressing

Reads where the customer actually is against the milestones the run set,
and confirms each milestone against the evidence underneath it rather
than against somebody's claim.

covers:
- Confirm the Customer Is Active

## change-the-path

Changes what the customer gets when they stall, which means swapping a
step, delaying it or dropping it. Every change lands dated, with what
the customer did or failed to do that caused it.

covers:
- Change the Path When They Stall

## hand-the-customer-to-a-person

Hands the customer to a person to make contact, with what that person is
being asked to do and everything the customer has already been sent.

covers:
- Bring a Person In

## answer-what-comes-back

Answers what the customer sends back, which is the questions, the
objections, and the accounts saying they want to leave.

covers:
- Answer What Comes Back

## close-the-run

Closes the run and records what was sent, what was offered, what the
customer did, whether the goal was reached and the date it ended. It
also records which signals turned out to be worth acting on.

covers:
- Close the Run
- Record the Outcome
- Record What Was Learned
