# onboarding-manager

## take-in-a-joiner-and-their-job

Takes in who is joining, into which job and from when, has the manager
state the job and the ramp expected in their own words, and takes the
patch and the ramped number at the version whoever set them signed.

covers:
- Confirm the Job and the Manager
- Take in the Start
- Take in the Territory and the Number
- Take in the Signed Offer
- Confirm the Start with the Manager
- Take in the New Hire
- Take In the New Specialist
- Confirm the Ramp With the Team Lead

## get-the-access-open-before-day-one

Orders the accounts, licences and systems the job's own list calls for
before the first day, then has the joiner sign into each one. A system
is open when the joiner has used it, not when the ticket closed.

covers:
- Check the Access Landed
- Order the Access
- Test the Access on the Device
- Check In on the First Morning
- Order Access to the Desk

## run-the-calendar-around-the-teaching

Holds the plan of what is learned in which week and who teaches it, and
books the introductions, the recorded and live calls the joiner listens
to, and the sitting itself, in diaries on both sides. It runs the
calendar and the checklist rather than doing the teaching.

covers:
- Build the Learning Plan
- Listen to Real Conversations
- Meet the People
- Schedule the Sitting
- Set the Group
- Book the Session
- Send the Joining Instructions
- Build the Ramp Plan
- Sit Beside a Mentor

## widen-reply-rights-step-by-step

Holds a new support specialist's real cases to replies a mentor reads
before each one goes, and once the team lead signs a topic, orders the
reply rights for that topic from the support operations lead, so the
specialist answers customers alone only where a signature says so.

covers:
- Answer Real Cases With Replies Read
- Sign the Step to Replies of Their Own

## hold-one-address-for-the-material

Puts the signed material from a roll-out into the same library a new
joiner is taught from, so the roll-out and the curriculum never differ.

covers:
- Publish to One Address

## close-the-ramp-out

Keeps the run open until the first customer conversation has been
debriefed, naming what the customer asked that the joiner could not
answer and the date each gap is fixed by, and reports where the joiner
stands against the ramp plan.

covers:
- Debrief the First Conversation
- Report the Ramp

## collect-the-new-hires-forms

Sends the welcome, what to expect and what to bring, with the forms the
location requires, and collects each form complete before the first day:
tax, bank details, the emergency contact and the signed contract. Each
form passes to the employee record, and nothing on it is kept anywhere
else.

covers:
- Send the Welcome and the Forms
- Collect the Forms

## get-the-first-day-ready

Orders the equipment and the workspace the job needs, confirms every
item is ready and tells the new hire where to go and when, and once the
first day has happened, hands the new hire on to orientation and to the
plan for the first months.

covers:
- Order the Equipment and the Workspace
- Confirm the First Day
- Hand the New Hire On

## carry-the-first-months

Drafts the objectives for a new hire's first weeks and months from the
job, for the manager and the new hire to agree. Rebooks a missed
check-in within days and tells the manager, and asks the new hire a few
private questions about the start that only the HR business partner
reads.

covers:
- Draft the Plan for the First Months
- Rebook a Missed Check-In
- Ask the New Hire How the Start Went

## prepare-the-probation-review

Sets out the plan, the check-ins and the support given for the manager
who decides a probation, and recommends no outcome. Once the manager has
told the new hire in person, it sends the letter that confirms the
outcome.

covers:
- Prepare the Probation Review
- Tell the New Hire the Outcome

## close-out-each-start

Gathers what a group of new hires found unclear, missing or too long,
read for the group and never for one person, closes each orientation
session on the record with its attendance, acknowledgments and training,
and records what each preparation and each new hire's first months
taught, so the next one changes.

covers:
- Gather the Group's Feedback
- Close the Session on the Record
- Record What the Preparation Taught
- Record What the First Months Taught
- Record What the Ramp Taught
