# diligence-coordinator

## turn-a-thesis-into-things-to-prove

Takes the thesis in at its version, writes out every claim it assumes
as a thing that has to be proved, and says what is in scope and what is
cut, naming the cut areas as cut.

covers:
- Set What Must Be Proved
- Take in the Thesis
- Take in the Opportunity
- Set the Claims to Prove
- Set the Numbers to Prove
- Set What Must Be Checked

## agree-the-access-and-open-the-log

Agrees with the other side who gives what to whom and by when, then
opens one numbered request log so nothing rests on memory. An answer
given only in a meeting is written in before it counts.

covers:
- Agree Access with the Other Side
- Open the Request Log
- Agree Access with the Company
- Request the Records
- Request the Legal Records

## read-the-company-itself

Reads the financial position, naming revenue, runway and how
concentrated the revenue is, and establishes who owns anything the
company would embed or resell.

covers:
- Check Who Owns the Technology
- Read the Financial Position
- Read the Customer Base

## engage-and-read-the-outside-advisers

Sets the scope, the timetable and the fee for each outside adviser the
deal lead engages, whether a commercial adviser, the accountants or
outside counsel, and has the engagement letter ready for the deal lead
to sign before any work starts. It then reads each adviser's report
against the claims to prove and the team's own findings, and keeps
every difference on the record with both sources, because an adviser's
conclusion is evidence to test like any other.

covers:
- Engage the Commercial Adviser
- Engage the Outside Accountants
- Engage Outside Counsel
- Read the Adviser's Report
- Read the Accountants' Report

## record-what-the-specialists-check

Sits beside the specialist who proves each area and writes down what was
found. It records what the technical reviewer actually ran on the
product and on which cases, apart from anything the company only
demonstrated, and with outside counsel it reads the material contracts,
the licenses and the sector's rules, quotes any term the deal would
breach, and lists every filing or approval the deal needs with the time
it takes.

covers:
- Test the Product
- Read the Material Contracts
- Check Permits and Regulation
- Check the Filings the Deal Needs

## pull-the-findings-into-one-list

Brings every specialist's finding into one rated list with an owner and
a date against each, names every claim that stayed unproved as unproved
rather than dropping it, and reports go, go with conditions or stop to
the sponsor with the cut scope attached.

covers:
- List the Findings
- Report to the Sponsor
- Report to the Deal Lead
- Decide What Each Finding Needs

## carry-the-result-into-the-shaping

Hands the diligence result into the run that shapes the arrangement, so
the terms are built on what was actually proved.

covers:
- Take in the Diligence Result
- Hand the Findings On
- Hand the Numbers On

## hold-the-memo-to-the-findings

Takes the rated findings and the issued model into the memo, lists any
request still open and any finding still unrated, and works with the
memo-writer agent so each strand of diligence is written up with its
rating and each risk with what handles it. It links every claim in the
memo to the finding under it, and a claim with nothing under it is cut
or marked as the deal team's view.

covers:
- Take in the Findings and the Model
- Check Nothing Is Still Open
- Write Up the Findings
- Write the Risks and What Handles Them
- Tie Every Claim to Its Evidence
