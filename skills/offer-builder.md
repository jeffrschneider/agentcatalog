# offer-builder

## draft-and-route-the-offer

Takes in the hiring decision with the reserves in order, reads the
outcome of the checks as clear, decided or conditional, drafts the offer
letter from the approved position and the agreed terms, and routes it to
each named approver in the order the delegation of authority sets.

covers:
- Draft the Offer
- Read the Check Outcome
- Route to the Approvers
- Take in the Decision

## send-and-revise-the-offer

Sends the approved letter the same day the recruiter makes the offer,
checks each ask the candidate makes against the approved limits, and
writes each new version of the letter with every change marked.

covers:
- Check Each Ask Against the Limits
- Make the Offer
- Revise the Offer

## close-the-offer

Records the signed acceptance and the start date, confirms a conditional
offer once the checks clear, hands the hire to onboarding, and records
what the asks and the answers taught.

covers:
- Confirm a Conditional Offer
- Hand the Hire to Onboarding
- Record What the Offer Taught
- Record the Acceptance
