# supplier-manager

## requirement-definition

Writes down what is needed before any candidate has been looked at,
starting from the gap the department cannot cover today and ending with
requirements that are ranked and testable or a scope of work with its
deliverables and dates.

covers:
- Take in the Gap
- Write down the Requirements
- Write the Scope

## scoring-criteria

Fixes how a candidate or a proposal will be judged, and does it before
any of them arrive, so nobody can change the measure once the names are
known.

covers:
- Set the Scoring Criteria
- Set the Selection Criteria

## register-lookup

Reads the register of what the department already buys and already owns,
to answer whether something on hand does the job and how much money is
already committed to contracts and licenses running into the coming
period.

covers:
- Check What Is Already Owned
- List the Money Already Committed

## candidate-search

Finds every candidate worth looking at against the requirements, then
narrows the list by dropping the ones that miss a requirement, so what
is left can be looked at properly.

covers:
- Build the Candidate List
- Cut to a Shortlist
- Search the Market

## name-and-mark-clearance

Searches the outside registers for what is already taken or already held
in the organization's own name, covering trademarks, company registries,
domains and handles, and puts the surviving candidates to counsel to say
which can be used and defended.

covers:
- Clear the Shortlist
- Find What Is Registered Outside
- Search the Registers

## name-and-mark-registration

Takes and maintains what the organization holds on outside systems,
which means securing the domains, handles and marks and authorizing the
filings, and bringing the profiles, listings and pages that other people
serve up to the current version.

covers:
- Change the Profiles Held Elsewhere
- Register the Names and the Marks
- Secure the Name

## same-request-to-every-candidate

Puts one request in front of everyone at the same time, with the same
scope and the same questions, so that what comes back can be compared
line for line.

covers:
- Brief the Selection
- Issue the Request

## candidate-evidence

Gathers what each candidate can actually show, which means the
presentation they give against the request, what their existing clients
say about them, and how they do on a trial using work the department
really has.

covers:
- Check the References
- Hear the Pitches
- Try the Finalists on Real Work

## contracting

Settles the terms the organization is bound by and gets to a signature,
covering price and rates, the term and how it ends, what the supplier
has to meet, who owns the work, and what happens to the data.

covers:
- Agree the Terms and Sign
- Settle the Contract

## register-entry

Lands a new agreement in the register with everything a later reader
needs, which is what it is for, what it costs, the term, the renewal
date and the named owner, and hands it to that owner with a date to
review it.

covers:
- Hand It to the Owner
- Write It into the Register

## access-and-capacity-requests

Puts in the requests that let new work start, which covers the accounts
and systems an outside party needs with the date that access comes back,
and the hiring and agent requests a capacity plan calls for.

covers:
- Commission the New Capacity
- Set up the Access

## outside-research-supply

Buys in the research capacity the department cannot supply itself, such
as respondents the contact base does not reach and an interviewer from
outside the deal who will hear more than the people who ran it.

covers:
- Arrange the Outside Interviewer
- Arrange the Outside Sample

## agency-briefing

Tells an engaged agency what the work is for, who it is aimed at, the
standards it has to meet and how it will be measured, so their first
piece is made against the same brief as everything else.

covers:
- Brief the Agency

## selection-learnings

Files what the selection or the engagement taught, which means what to
repeat and what to avoid the next time the department buys something
like it.

covers:
- Record What Was Learned
