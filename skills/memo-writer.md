# memo-writer

## frame-the-case

Reads in the rated findings, the issued model and the mandate result
with their dates, sits in the deal lead partner's decision to recommend
or pass, and lays the memo out with the decisions it will ask of the
committee.

covers:
- Decide Whether to Recommend
- Outline the Case
- Take in the Findings and the Model

## write-the-case

Writes the thesis as the claims diligence tested, each strand of
findings with its rating and the areas cut from scope, each risk with
what handles it, the returns from the issued model at its version, and
the proposed terms inside the mandate result.

covers:
- Write the Proposed Terms
- Write the Returns
- Write the Risks and What Handles Them
- Write the Thesis
- Write Up the Findings

## tie-every-claim-to-evidence

Links every claim to the finding, model case or source under it, cuts
or marks as the deal team's view any claim with nothing under it, and
names each open question with what would settle it. It checks the same
again before the committee reads the memo.

covers:
- Confirm the Memo Is Final
- Name the Open Questions
- Tie Every Claim to Its Evidence

## answer-the-challenge

Takes the deal team's corrections and the challenge partner's case
against the deal, and answers each point in the memo with its evidence
or leaves it open in the challenger's words. It answers the committee
members' written questions the same way.

covers:
- Answer the Challenge
- Answer the Questions in Writing
- Challenge the Case
- Read It as the Deal Team

## carry-the-memo-to-the-committee

Sends the signed memo at its version to the committee before the
reading deadline, takes it in again for the committee's run, and hands
the approved terms on with the decision-coordinator agent.

covers:
- Hand the Approved Terms On
- Send the Memo to the Committee
- Take in the Memo

## record-the-outcome-against-the-memo

Records a pass or a decline with its reason and what would bring the
deal back. For a failed holding it marks each claim in the approved
memo held, failed or never tested, and writes the lessons where the
next memo's author will read them.

covers:
- File the Write-Off Record
- Read the Memo against What Happened
- Record a Decline with Its Reason
- Record a Pass
- Write the Lessons
