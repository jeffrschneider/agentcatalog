# compensation-analyst

## level-jobs-and-keep-ranges

Places a job at a level in the job framework by matching its duties and
must-haves to the level descriptions, confirms the job and level a new
figure is for, reads the range kept for each level and location, and
refreshes every range from the market data in the pay review.

covers:
- Confirm the Job and Its Level
- Confirm the Job at the Next Level
- Place the Job at a Level
- Refresh the Pay Ranges
- Set the Pay Range

## propose-pay-figures

Proposes a figure inside the range for an offer, a promotion, an
internal move or a pay change outside the review, with the reason for
each point and the exception and approver it needs where it falls
outside the range or the budget.

covers:
- Propose a Figure
- Propose the Figure
- Propose the Pay for Each Promotion
- Work Out the Pay for the New Role

## check-pay-against-like-work

Checks a figure, a candidate's ask or a manager's proposal against the
range, the budget, the guidelines and the pay of people doing like work,
using only the factors the pay policy names, and models what each budget
option would cost.

covers:
- Check Each Ask Against the Limits
- Check Each Proposal
- Check the Cost Against the Budget
- Check the Figure Against Like Work
- Check the Figure Against the Range
- Compare with People Doing Like Work
- Model the Cost

## carry-pay-to-the-approvers

Takes in a request for a pay change and checks it carries the reason,
the evidence and the approvals the policy asks for, prepares the pay
part of a promotion panel's pack, carries each proposed figure to the
named approvers with the decision it rests on, and hands each approved
promotion on to the record.

covers:
- Check the Request Is Complete
- Get the Pay Approved
- Get the Pay Changes Approved
- Hand On Each Promotion
- Prepare the Panel Pack
- Take in the Request

## learn-from-the-pay-decisions

Records where requests fell outside the range or the budget and flags
every range that keeps being breached for the next pay review.

covers:
- Record What the Change Shows
- Record What the Review Taught
