# financial-analyst

## rebuild-the-numbers-from-records

Ties revenue, cash and debt to the company's own invoices, contracts,
accounts and bank statements, splits recurring revenue from one-off
revenue, reads the margin and each cost line, and follows the cash, so
every figure carries the record and the period it came from.

covers:
- Build the Baseline
- Follow the Cash
- Read the Margins and Costs
- Rebuild the Revenue
- Take in the Opportunity
- Test the Quality of Revenue
- Tie the Numbers to the Accounts

## test-what-management-claims

Accepts, cuts or leaves open each adjustment management proposes on its
evidence, reads working capital and net debt, sets the forecast beside
the company's past accuracy, records the tax adviser's view as the
adviser's, reads the accountants' report against its own figures, and
asks the company once for whatever is missing or does not tie.

covers:
- Ask the Company What Is Missing or Wrong
- Read the Accountants' Report
- Read the Forecast
- Read the Tax Position
- Read Working Capital and Net Debt
- Test Management's Adjustments

## agree-and-hand-on-the-numbers

Agrees with the deal lead the figures the model and the price will use,
each with its source and its adjustments, hands them to the model, the
memo and the other diligence runs, and reads them into the model with
their sources carried across.

covers:
- Agree the Numbers to Model From
- Hand the Numbers On
- Take in the Agreed Numbers

## project-and-check-the-case

Projects revenue, margins and cash beside management's plan, models the
later rounds or the debt year by year, sizes what each change in a value
creation plan is worth and costs, and checks a model it did not build
for its formulas, links, signs and totals.

covers:
- Check the Model
- Model the Later Rounds or the Debt
- Project the Operating Case
- Size Each Change

## read-a-holding-against-its-plan

Reads each period's results against the budget, the value creation plan
and the prior period, works out the months of cash left, and calculates
each covenant as the loan agreement defines it with the headroom left.

covers:
- Read Progress Against the Plan
- Read the Results against Plan
- Test the Covenants
- Work Out the Cash Runway
