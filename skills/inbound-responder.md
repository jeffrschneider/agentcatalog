# inbound-responder

## open-an-inquiry-and-start-the-clock

Takes the inquiry in on the channel it arrived on and starts the clock
the promised answer time is measured against. Checks the inquiry may be
answered at all, and names who answers it.

covers:
- Take in the Inquiry
- Start the Response Clock
- Check Whether It May Be Answered
- Decide Who Answers

## answer-what-was-actually-asked

Writes a reply that answers the question that was asked, having first
read what is already known about whoever asked, and sends it on the
channel the inquiry came in on. Offers a time to talk and holds it.

covers:
- Write the First Reply
- Send the First Reply
- Offer a Time to Talk

## write-down-what-was-said

Writes the first conversation up against the record, including every
commitment made in it, who made it and when it is due.

covers:
- Write Down What Was Said

## close-the-run-against-the-promise

Hands the inquiry and everything read on the way to qualification, then
records how long the answer took against what was promised. A miss is
recorded as a miss.

covers:
- Hand It to Qualification
- Record the Response Time
