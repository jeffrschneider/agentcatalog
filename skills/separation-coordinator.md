# separation-coordinator

## run-the-leaving-checklist

Takes in each departure, opens one checklist across pay, benefits,
access, property and work, tells each team only what its own step
needs, collects the company property, and drafts the final documents
for signature. It confirms every item is done with its evidence and
closes the leaving record.

covers:
- Close the Leaving Record
- Collect the Company Property
- Confirm the Checklist Is Complete
- Issue the Final Documents
- Open the Leaving Checklist
- Take in the Departure
- Tell Each Team What It Owns

## hear-why-people-leave

Offers each leaver an exit interview they may decline, and passes the
reasons given to the workforce report with nothing that could identify
the leaver.

covers:
- Offer an Exit Interview
- Pass On the Reasons for Leaving

## prepare-a-dismissal

Takes in a proposed dismissal, gathers the record with the
employee-case-keeper agent, checks each step the policy requires and
any open matter, works out the notice and the last day, drafts the
letter, and prepares and records the conversation. It starts the
leaving only once named people have decided.

covers:
- Check for Open Matters
- Check the Steps the Policy Requires
- Draft the Dismissal Letter
- Gather the Record
- Prepare the Conversation
- Record the Conversation
- Set the Notice and the Last Day
- Start the Leaving Process
- Take in the Proposed Dismissal

## keep-the-layoff-record

Keeps the calendar of every notice, consultation and waiting period,
drafts each notice and each person's letter, and records each proposal
from the representatives and each score with its evidence. It starts
each person's leaving and records what the layoff taught.

covers:
- Apply the Criteria
- Consult the Employee Representatives
- Draft Each Person's Letter
- Give the Required Notices
- Record What the Layoff Taught
- Set the Notice and Consultation Calendar
- Start Each Person's Leaving

## prepare-separation-terms

Reads the separation policy, drafts terms within it, sends the offer
with the time to consider and the advice it allows for, counts that
time and any time to withdraw, and hands the payment to payroll and the
terms to the offboarding.

covers:
- Confirm the Time and the Advice
- Draft the Terms
- Hand the Payment to Payroll
- Hand the Terms to the Offboarding
- Read the Separation Policy
- Send the Offer
- Take in the Request for Terms
- Wait Out Any Time to Withdraw
