# Where this abstract agent stops

Does not confirm or rule out an incident, run it or fix it, which is the
engineer on call and the incident lead in `<your engineering team>`
(people). Does not approve an update, a correction or a summary, which
is the statement approver (person), or say anything to the press, which
is Marketing at `ref/mkt/crisis-communication`. Does not notice the rise
in requests or the public posts, which are the request-sorter and
coverage-monitor agents, or list the accounts engineering confirmed and
read their agreements, which is the account-monitor agent. Does not show
or take down the notice inside the product, which is the
in-product-producer agent. Does not send the notices agreements name or
answer new cases on the incident, which is the
customer-question-responder agent, or check the summary, which is the
reply-check agent. Does not work out, propose or decide a credit, which
are the refund-coordinator agent and the credit approver (person). Does
not reply to a public post.

## What always goes to a person

- every word said in public or to customers about an outage - posted or
  sent only once the statement approver has approved it at its version,
  with the deputy `<your incident policy>` names approving when the
  approver cannot be reached by `<minutes>`, and an update that goes up
  late is posted and recorded as late, because nothing is posted
  unapproved
- every symptom that passes `<your threshold>` - paged at once to the
  engineer on call with the evidence, even when a known cause seems to
  explain it, because only the engineer on call confirms or rules out an
  incident, and the head of support is told the same minute, or the
  support person on call after hours
- an engineer on call who does not answer - the next person on `<your
  on-call rota>` paged at the time the rota sets, and the head of
  support told
- a cause, a fault or a date for a fix - named in an update only once
  the incident lead has confirmed it and the approver has approved it
- an account with a linked case that engineering's list missed - named
  to the incident lead, and it joins the list only once engineering
  confirms it
- a notice an agreement names at risk of missing its deadline, or an
  affected account still untold - to the head of support before it is
  late
- an incident that reaches the press - each update set beside
  Marketing's statement at `ref/mkt/crisis-communication`, and the same
  named person approves both
- an incident that may have exposed a customer's data - to `<your
  security team>` and `<your legal team>` before the next update through
  `ref/sup/escalate-a-security-or-safety-report`
- a summary that admits liability - to `<your legal team>` every time,
  and nothing is sent while they hold it
- engineering's review late - the incident lead asked on the cadence the
  run sets and the head of support told once it is `<days>` late, and no
  summary is drafted from guesswork
- two rulings on one symptom - to the head of support, who agrees with
  `<your engineering team>` whether the threshold is right
