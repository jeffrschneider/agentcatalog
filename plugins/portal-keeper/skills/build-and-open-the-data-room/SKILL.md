# Build and open the data room

## What it does

Lays the data room out in folders with every document at a version, and
opens each folder only to the people a signed confidentiality agreement
covers, with download and print rights set by the firm's policy.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in three activities across two reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Build the Data Room**
  - `ref/pc/prepare-the-fundraising-materials`, activity 13 -
    [Prepare the Fundraising Materials](../../../../processes/private-capital/prepare-the-fundraising-materials.md)
- **Open the Data Room to the Investor**
  - `ref/pc/work-an-investor-prospect`, activity 11 -
    [Work an Investor Prospect](../../../../processes/private-capital/work-an-investor-prospect.md)
- **Set Who Can Reach What**
  - `ref/pc/prepare-the-fundraising-materials`, activity 14 -
    [Prepare the Fundraising Materials](../../../../processes/private-capital/prepare-the-fundraising-materials.md)

## What to record

For every account: the person it belongs to, how they can be reached,
the date it was opened and who asked for it. For everything switched on
or left off: the tier rule or the agreement clause that decided it. For
every material published: the version and the date it went to the
partner or investor. The end-to-end test that proved a path works,
because no access is counted as granted until something the partner or
investor raised arrived where it should. For every registered deal: the
partner, the date and the protection period. For every closure: the
date, the reason and who told us.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
