# Work the path in

## What it does

Prepares the ask that goes along a path: a note a connector can forward,
an approach to a candidate, or a reference call with an earlier investor
or founder who has agreed to speak. The person who holds the path makes
the introduction or the approach. The agent records each answer and
whether the path landed, and the firm never goes round someone who
declined.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in four activities across four reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Approach the Candidates**
  - `ref/pc/fill-a-key-role-at-a-portfolio-company`, activity 10 -
    [Fill a Key Role at a Portfolio Company](../../../../processes/private-capital/fill-a-key-role-at-a-portfolio-company.md)
- **Arrange the Reference Calls**
  - `ref/pc/work-an-investor-prospect`, activity 14 -
    [Work an Investor Prospect](../../../../processes/private-capital/work-an-investor-prospect.md)
- **Ask for the Introduction**
  - `ref/hr/find-candidates`, activity 12 -
    [Find Candidates](../../../../processes/human-resources/find-candidates.md)
  - `ref/pc/reach-out-to-a-company`, activity 9 -
    [Reach Out to a Company](../../../../processes/private-capital/reach-out-to-a-company.md)
- **Record the Outcome**
  - `ref/pc/reach-out-to-a-company`, activity 18 -
    [Reach Out to a Company](../../../../processes/private-capital/reach-out-to-a-company.md)

## What to record

Per person: the decision they sit under, whether they decide, influence,
block or use, the source they were found in and that source's
publication date, the reporting line behind any claim of authority, the
strength score with the contact history it was computed from, the path
in and who at our end holds it, and the stance with what was said or
done behind it. Per map: every decision with no decider found or nobody
we know marked as a gap rather than left blank, what the privacy check
removed and the rule behind each removal, and the issued version naming
who corrected what and when they signed. A claim that somebody knows a
buyer well is recorded as a claim, with the person who made it.
