# Get Sign-Offs

## What happens

The processes that use this activity word it differently:

- in `ref/mkt/account-based-play`, brand and legal approve the material
- in `ref/mkt/advisory-board`, what may be shown to the board is
  approved
- in `ref/mkt/announce-company-news`, brand, legal and rights approve
  the announcement
- in `ref/mkt/award-entry`, brand, legal, rights and the customer
  approve it
- in `ref/mkt/brand-refresh`, brand, legal and rights approve the remade
  material
- in `ref/mkt/brief-executive-leadership`, brand, legal and leadership
  approve the pack
- in `ref/mkt/brief-industry-analyst`, the claims are scored, then legal
  and product sign
- in `ref/mkt/channel-program`, brand, disclosure, legal and finance
  approve it
- in `ref/mkt/conversion-experiment`, brand, accessibility and legal
  clear every version
- in `ref/mkt/crisis-communication`, legal and leadership sign the exact
  wording
- in `ref/mkt/customer-newsletter`, brand, claims and legal approve the
  issue
- in `ref/mkt/display-retargeting`, brand and legal approve the ads and
  the targeting
- in `ref/mkt/enable-channel-partner`, brand, legal and rights approve
  what goes outside
- in `ref/mkt/field-event`, brand and legal approve the material
- in `ref/mkt/long-form-content`, brand, legal and rights approve the
  piece
- in `ref/mkt/paid-search-campaign`, brand and legal approve the ads and
  the pages
- in `ref/mkt/pitch-earned-media`, brand, legal and the spokesperson
  approve it
- in `ref/mkt/pricing-change`, brand, legal, disclosure and finance
  approve it
- in `ref/mkt/product-launch`, brand, legal, and product accuracy
- in `ref/mkt/product-positioning`, brand, legal and product accuracy
  sign at a version
- in `ref/mkt/refresh-content`, brand, legal and rights approve the
  changed pages
- in `ref/mkt/social-campaign`, brand and legal approve the material
- in `ref/mkt/speaking-engagement`, brand, legal and rights approve the
  talk
- in `ref/mkt/test-ad-creative`, brand, disclosure and legal clear every
  variant
- in `ref/mkt/trade-show`, brand and legal approve the booth material
- in `ref/mkt/webinar`, brand and legal approve the material

## What to record

A score per item, each citing the standard, its version, and the rule
applied. What passed, what failed, and why, in a form the human signers
read in minutes.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
