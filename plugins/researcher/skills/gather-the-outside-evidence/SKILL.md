# Gather the Outside Evidence

## What happens

The processes that use this activity word it differently:

- in `ref/mkt/annual-marketing-plan`, the market, the competitors, and
  what people asked
- in `ref/mkt/ideal-customer-profile`, market reports, public data, and
  who else sells here
- in `ref/mkt/segment-the-market`, market reports, public data, and what
  buyers search for
- in `ref/mkt/size-market-opportunity`, published figures, public
  counts, search demand

## What to record

A source line for every fact: what was read, where it lives, the date it
was read, and the part of it the fact came from. Every number with the
query or the report it came from, so somebody can pull it again and see
whether the answer still holds. Interview notes attributed to the person
who said it, marked with what that person agreed could be quoted.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
