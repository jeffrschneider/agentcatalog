# Shape the plan with management

## What it does

Hears what management thinks will and will not move the value, lists
every change either side proposes with the thesis line it serves, and
checks the people, cash and attention each change needs against what the
company has.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in three activities across one reference process.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Check What the Company Can Carry**
  - `ref/pc/write-the-value-creation-plan`, activity 6 -
    [Write the Value Creation Plan](../../../../processes/private-capital/write-the-value-creation-plan.md)
- **Hear Management's View**
  - `ref/pc/write-the-value-creation-plan`, activity 3 -
    [Write the Value Creation Plan](../../../../processes/private-capital/write-the-value-creation-plan.md)
- **List the Candidate Changes**
  - `ref/pc/write-the-value-creation-plan`, activity 4 -
    [Write the Value Creation Plan](../../../../processes/private-capital/write-the-value-creation-plan.md)

## What to record

The handover leaves the starting points, each with the finding and the
line of the thesis it serves, and each committee condition carried with
an owner on the portfolio team and a date. Writing the plan leaves
management's view in its own words, the list of candidate changes with
who proposed each one, the capacity read for each change, and the chosen
changes each with one owner, one measure and one date, while a change
that could not carry all three is named as struck. The first months are
planned week by week with an owner on every step, and the firm's help is
recorded as commitments with an owner at the firm and a date. The plan
is issued at a version that says what changed since the last one and
why, and every earlier version stays on file. Each board meeting leaves
the progress reading, with every change marked met, running, late or
untouched and its measure set beside the baseline, the director's brief
keeping what the pack says apart from what the firm thinks, and the plan
updated for what the board decided. A dead change is closed with its
reason and its date, or replaced by one with its own owner. Anyone
reading the plan can say who owns each change, how it is measured, when
it is due and what the firm promised to put in.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
