# Write the account of the numbers

## What it does

Writes the explanation of why the numbers moved, in plain words, for the
people who decide what happens next.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in four activities across four reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Write Why the Numbers Moved**
  - `ref/hr/report-on-the-workforce`, activity 11 -
    [Report on the Workforce](../../../../processes/human-resources/report-on-the-workforce.md)
- **Write the Account**
  - `ref/mkt/marketing-performance-report`, activity 11 -
    [Report Marketing Performance](../../../../processes/marketing/marketing-performance-report.md)
- **Write the Account of the Month**
  - `ref/fin/report-the-monthly-results`, activity 11 -
    [Report the Monthly Results](../../../../processes/finance/report-the-monthly-results.md)
- **Write the Quarter's Letter**
  - `ref/pc/issue-the-quarterly-report-and-statements`, activity 10 -
    [Issue the Quarterly Report and Statements](../../../../processes/private-capital/issue-the-quarterly-report-and-statements.md)

## What to record

The report at a version for each period, with every line attributed to
its source and set beside the commitment it is measured against. A dated
record of what it asked for, who answered, and who did not. A correction
goes out as a new version of the report, and the version it corrects
stays where it was.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
