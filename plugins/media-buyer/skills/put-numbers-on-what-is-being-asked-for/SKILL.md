# Put numbers on what is being asked for

## What it does

Puts cost and expected return on each request, initiative or proposed
change, with the assumptions written underneath and what it would
displace. It also reads the terms behind money already placed, so a
proposal to pull that money out carries the notice, the minimums, the
rate tiers and the cancellation cost.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in five activities across five reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Check What a Move Would Cost**
  - `ref/mkt/reallocate-media-spend`, activity 5 -
    [Reallocate Media Spend](../../../../processes/marketing/reallocate-media-spend.md)
- **Price the Requests**
  - `ref/mkt/set-marketing-budget`, activity 5 -
    [Set Marketing Budget](../../../../processes/marketing/set-marketing-budget.md)
- **Put Numbers on Each Initiative**
  - `ref/mkt/annual-marketing-plan`, activity 7 -
    [Build Annual Marketing Plan](../../../../processes/marketing/annual-marketing-plan.md)
- **Put Numbers on Each Request**
  - `ref/mkt/prioritize-initiatives`, activity 7 -
    [Prioritize Marketing Initiatives](../../../../processes/marketing/prioritize-initiatives.md)
- **Put Numbers on the Proposed Changes**
  - `ref/mkt/quarterly-business-review`, activity 9 -
    [Conduct Quarterly Business Review](../../../../processes/marketing/quarterly-business-review.md)

## What to record

Every campaign configuration at a version, every budget move with the
number, the direction, and the reason, and the stop record when the buy
comes off.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
