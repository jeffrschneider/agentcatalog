# Lay out what runs when

## What it does

Lays the agreed work across the calendar: what runs on which dates and
channels, at what weight, and who owes what when more than one party is
paying.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in two activities across two reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Build the Calendar**
  - `ref/mkt/annual-marketing-plan`, activity 11 -
    [Build Annual Marketing Plan](../../../../processes/marketing/annual-marketing-plan.md)
- **Plan the Joint Campaign**
  - `ref/mkt/co-marketing-campaign`, activity 3 -
    [Run Co-Marketing Campaign](../../../../processes/marketing/co-marketing-campaign.md)

## What to record

Every campaign configuration at a version, every budget move with the
number, the direction, and the reason, and the stop record when the buy
comes off.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
