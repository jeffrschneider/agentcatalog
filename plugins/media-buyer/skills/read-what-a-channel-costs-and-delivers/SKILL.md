# Read what a channel costs and delivers

## What it does

Gathers the evidence behind a buying decision: what each channel has
cost, delivered and returned, what the creative in flight is delivering
by placement, and what people search for and what a click on those terms
costs.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in three activities across three reference
processes. Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Gather the Channel Evidence**
  - `ref/mkt/media-plan`, activity 5 -
    [Develop Media Plan](../../../../processes/marketing/media-plan.md)
- **Read the Current Performance**
  - `ref/mkt/test-ad-creative`, activity 2 -
    [Test Ad Creative](../../../../processes/marketing/test-ad-creative.md)
- **Research the Search Demand**
  - `ref/mkt/paid-search-campaign`, activity 2 -
    [Run Paid Search Campaign](../../../../processes/marketing/paid-search-campaign.md)

## What to record

Every campaign configuration at a version, every budget move with the
number, the direction, and the reason, and the stop record when the buy
comes off.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
