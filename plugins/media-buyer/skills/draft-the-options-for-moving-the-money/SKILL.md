# Draft the options for moving the money

## What it does

Puts up two or three ways the money could be split across channels or
moved between lines, saying for each one which lines give money up and
which lines take it.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in two activities across two reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Draft the Candidate Mixes**
  - `ref/mkt/media-plan`, activity 6 -
    [Develop Media Plan](../../../../processes/marketing/media-plan.md)
- **Name the Candidate Moves**
  - `ref/mkt/reallocate-media-spend`, activity 4 -
    [Reallocate Media Spend](../../../../processes/marketing/reallocate-media-spend.md)

## What to record

Every campaign configuration at a version, every budget move with the
number, the direction, and the reason, and the stop record when the buy
comes off.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
