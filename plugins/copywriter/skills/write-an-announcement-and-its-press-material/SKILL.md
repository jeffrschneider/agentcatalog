# Write an announcement and its press material

## What it does

The agent writes the release and the shorter versions of it, and puts
together the facts and the answers a reporter will want. Every quote is
approved by the person it is attributed to before it goes anywhere.

## Where it happens

The agent does this in five activities across three reference processes.
Each one names the activity as that process words it.

- **Gather the Quotes**
  - `ref/mkt/announce-company-news`, activity 5 -
    [Announce Company News](../../../../processes/marketing/announce-company-news.md)
- **Make the Press Materials**
  - `ref/mkt/announce-company-news`, activity 9 -
    [Announce Company News](../../../../processes/marketing/announce-company-news.md)
- **Produce the Pitch Materials**
  - `ref/mkt/pitch-earned-media`, activity 6 -
    [Pitch Earned Media](../../../../processes/marketing/pitch-earned-media.md)
- **Write the Announcement**
  - `ref/mkt/feature-release`, activity 3 -
    [Announce Feature Release](../../../../processes/marketing/feature-release.md)
- **Write the Release**
  - `ref/mkt/announce-company-news`, activity 6 -
    [Announce Company News](../../../../processes/marketing/announce-company-news.md)

## What to record

Every draft at a version, and the DONE records of the patterns it works
inside. A claim it uses cites the register entry it came from.

That contract covers every activity this abstract agent takes on, and it
is repeated in `com.agentcatalog.agent/RECORDS.md`. What the abstract
agent does not do is in `com.agentcatalog.agent/NOT.md`.
