# Departments and lines of business

Every folder under `processes/` and `streams/` is one of two kinds, and
this table says which. A **department** is a function that companies of
every kind have, such as marketing or sales. A **line of business** is
the core work of one kind of company, such as the investing and fund
work of a private capital firm. The catalog, the map and the wall read
this table to group what they show and to let a reader filter by kind.

Each row names a folder, the name a reader sees, and its kind, which is
either `Department` or `Line of business`. A folder with no row here is
shown as a department, and the loader warns about it.
